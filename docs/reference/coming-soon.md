# Coming Soon

These features are on the way. **None of them is available yet**, and they have no release date. Each one moves to its own guide the day it ships. For everything you can use today, see [Everything Pixture Does](./feature-map.md).

## Multi Camera

One booth with two or more cameras, in any mix of DSLRs and webcams. Before every photo, the customer chooses which camera takes it, for example a close-up or a wide angle. You switch it on per booth and name each camera.

It comes with the next Pixture Photobooth update, for booths in Kiosk mode first.

:::note You may already see the switch
A **Multi camera** card may already show on a booth's **Settings** tab. It does nothing until the booth runs the update.
:::

**Today:** one camera per booth. See [Supported Cameras](./supported-cameras.md).

## Print Station for Multi-Booth Groups

One computer prints for every booth in a [Multi-Booth group](../guides/multi-booth-setup.md), in order, so a studio needs fewer printers.

**Today:** share one printer between booths with your computer's printer sharing. See [Sharing One Printer Between Booths](../guides/shared-printer.md).

## Pix Station

A stand-alone station for your customers that prints for the booths around it.

**Today:** use a shared printer as above.
