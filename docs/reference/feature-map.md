# Everything Pixture Does

Pixture is photobooth software in two parts: the **Pixture Photobooth** app runs the booth on a Mac or Windows computer, and the **Pixture dashboard** at pixture.io runs everything else. This page lists everything they do. Each line links to its full guide. Features that are not out yet are on [Coming Soon](./coming-soon.md).

## The Booth

- **Mac and Windows.** The booth app runs on macOS and Windows and updates itself. See [Installation](../desktop-app/installation.md) and [System Requirements](./system-requirements.md).
- **Real cameras.** Canon EOS, Sony Alpha, Nikon Z and Fujifilm over USB, with live view and exposure control from the booth. See [Supported Cameras](./supported-cameras.md) and [Camera Settings](../desktop-app/camera-setup/camera-settings.md).
- **Webcams too.** A built-in camera, a USB webcam or a virtual camera. See [Webcam Fallback](../desktop-app/camera-setup/webcam-fallback.md).
- **Flash and lighting.** Built-in flash, speedlites and studio strobes on a wireless trigger. See [Lighting & Flash](../desktop-app/camera-setup/lighting-and-flash.md).
- **Any printer your computer can print to.** Dye-sub and inkjet, with the paper size read from the driver and the DNP 2-inch cut. See [Supported Printers](./supported-printers.md) and [Printer Setup](../desktop-app/printer-setup.md).
- **One printer for several booths.** Share a printer between booths with your computer's printer sharing. The computer holding the printer does not need Pixture. See [Sharing One Printer Between Booths](../guides/shared-printer.md).
- **Keeps working offline.** Sessions, prints and payment codes carry on when the venue internet drops, and photos upload when it comes back. See [Offline Mode](../desktop-app/offline-mode.md).
- **Locked to customers, open to you.** A hidden operator menu behind a PIN. See [Operator Menu](../desktop-app/admin-panel.md).

## Photo Sessions

- **Booth modes.** Photo, Studio (a timed pool of shots, then pick the best), Notes (write or draw on the photo), Newspaper, Video, 360/Slow-mo, Photographer (a roaming photographer) and Mingle (carry the booth to the guests). See [UI Editor](../dashboard/pix-design/ui-editor.md), [Studio Sessions](../desktop-app/studio-sessions.md) and [Events](../dashboard/events.md).
- **What customers take home.** Prints, single photos, an animated GIF, a live photo and video clips. See [Capture Modes](../desktop-app/session-flow/capture-modes.md) and [Event Video Modes](../desktop-app/event-video-modes.md).
- **Every screen is yours.** Start screen, payment, template choice, capture, review, filters and sharing, each designed in the UI Editor. See [Photo Session Flow](../desktop-app/session-flow/overview.md).
- **Filters.** 14 built-in looks plus your own .cube filters. See [Filters](../desktop-app/session-flow/filters.md).
- **Review and retake.** Customers move, scale and mirror each photo in its slot. See [Review and Adjust](../desktop-app/session-flow/review-and-adjust.md).
- **AI portraits (beta).** Customers turn a photo into an AI portrait in a style you chose. See [Pix AI](../dashboard/pix-design/pix-ai.md).

## Sharing

- **QR share page.** Every session gets its own page with the print, the singles, the GIF and the live photo, in your branding. Photos stay for 60 days. See [Sharing](../desktop-app/session-flow/sharing.md) and [Microsite](../dashboard/pix-design/microsite.md).
- **Email my photos.** Customers send the photos to their own email from the booth. See [Email My Photos](../desktop-app/session-flow/email-my-photos.md).
- **Event albums and slideshow.** One online album per event, with download, embed and a slideshow for a venue screen. See [Events](../dashboard/events.md).

## Payments

- **QR and card through your own gateway.** DOKU, Midtrans, Xendit, Stripe, Razorpay, Kashier and HitPay, in any currency. The money goes straight to you. See [Payment Gateway Reference](../guides/payment-gateway-setup.md) and [Create a Payment Gateway](../tutorials/create-a-payment-gateway.md).
- **Coins, notes and card readers.** Stripe Terminal, coin and note acceptors, Nayax and EDC readers and a Transpire QR DuitNow terminal. See [Hardware Payments](../desktop-app/hardware-payments.md) and [Take Card, Coin and Cash Payments](../tutorials/connect-a-coin-or-card-reader.md).
- **Packages and prices per booth.** Sessions, prints, extra copies and a price per mode. See [Booth Pricing](../dashboard/booth-pricing.md).
- **Vouchers.** Session tickets, staff passes and batches of codes, for cash at the counter or gifts. See [Vouchers](../dashboard/vouchers.md).
- **Sell sessions online.** Customers pay on their phone and get a code the booth accepts, even offline. See [Sell Sessions Online](../tutorials/sell-sessions-online.md).
- **Promo.** Amount off, a promo price or pay 1 get 1, automatic or with a code. See [Marketing Studio](../dashboard/marketing-studio.md).

## Events

- **Events that run themselves.** Dates, modes, outputs, templates, pricing and a start screen per event; booths pick it up on their own. See [Events](../dashboard/events.md) and [Run an Event](../tutorials/run-an-event.md).
- **A start screen per event.** Background, logo, colours and the modes guests choose from. See [Event Start Screen](../dashboard/event-start-screen.md).
- **Event Pass.** 24 hours of Pix Pro on one computer for $5, for the days you work. See [Plans & Pricing](../pricing/plans.md).

## Self-Service Booths

- **Queue line.** Customers take a number on their phone and are called by a TV screen and a voice. See [Queue Line](../guides/queue-line.md) and [Queue Display](../dashboard/pix-design/queue-display.md).
- **Multi-Booth groups.** Several booths with shared settings, a shared queue and a central cashier. See [Multi-Booth Setup](../guides/multi-booth-setup.md).
- **Frame Lab.** Customers design their own frame on their phone and use it at the booth. See [Frame Lab](../tutorials/frame-lab.md).
- **Your own Website.** A page at `handle.pixture.io` with your booths, a shop, Frame Lab and photo lookup. See [Website](../dashboard/pix-design/website.md).
- **Marketing Studio.** Consent photos, your customer audience, campaigns and how they perform. See [Marketing Studio](../dashboard/marketing-studio.md).

## Design

- **Brand once, use everywhere.** Logo, colours and fonts that every screen, page and event starts from. See [Brand](../dashboard/pix-design/brand.md).
- **Print templates.** From your own PNG or JPG, with photo slots, folders and categories. See [Template Editor](../dashboard/pix-design/creating-templates.md) and [Design a Print Template](../tutorials/design-a-print-template.md).
- **Booth screens.** See [UI Editor](../dashboard/pix-design/ui-editor.md).
- **GIF and video overlays.** See [Photo Filters and Overlays](../dashboard/pix-design/filters-and-overlays.md).

## Running Your Business

- **One dashboard for every booth.** Revenue, sessions, prints and top templates for a day, week or month. See [Dashboard Home](../dashboard/dashboard-home.md).
- **Kiosk and event money apart.** Pixture Kiosk, Pixture Event and an Overview of both. See [Pixture Event, Pixture Kiosk and Overview](../dashboard/views.md).
- **Booth health.** Online status, camera, printer, paper and errors for every booth. See [Health](../dashboard/health.md).
- **Gallery and transactions.** Every photo and every payment, searchable and exportable. See [Gallery](../dashboard/gallery.md) and [Transactions](../dashboard/transactions.md).
- **Your team.** Members and roles, down to which booths each person can reach. See [Settings](../dashboard/settings.md).
- **Any country, any currency.** Any timezone and ISO currency, a different currency per booth if you need it. The dashboard is in English and Bahasa Indonesia, and the booth's customer language is your choice. See [Settings](../dashboard/settings.md).
- **The iPhone app.** Pixture Manager runs your account from your phone, with booth alerts. See [Pixture Manager for iPhone](../iphone-app/overview.md).
- **Lucy and AI assistants.** Lucy answers questions in the dashboard and the iPhone app, and Claude or ChatGPT can work with your account through the Pixture connector. See [Connect an AI Assistant](../guides/connect-an-ai-assistant.md).
- **Plans.** Pix Starter is free on one computer with a watermark. Pix Pro is per computer, monthly or yearly. See [Plans & Pricing](../pricing/plans.md) and [Feature Comparison](../pricing/feature-comparison.md).
