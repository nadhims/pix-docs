# Everything Pixture Does

Pixture is photobooth software in two parts: the **Pixture Photobooth** app runs the booth on a Mac or Windows computer, and the **Pixture dashboard** at pixture.io runs everything else. This page lists everything they do. Each line links to its full guide. Features that are not out yet are on [Coming Soon](./coming-soon.md).

## The Booth

- **Mac and Windows.** The booth app runs on macOS and Windows and updates itself. See [Installation](../desktop-app/installation.md) and [System Requirements](./system-requirements.md). Tutorial: [Set Up a Booth From Scratch](../tutorials/set-up-a-booth.md).
- **Real cameras.** Canon EOS, Sony Alpha, Nikon Z and Fujifilm over USB, with live view and exposure control from the booth. See [Supported Cameras](./supported-cameras.md) and [Camera Settings](../desktop-app/camera-setup/camera-settings.md). Tutorial: [Connect a Camera](../tutorials/connect-a-camera.md).
- **Webcams too.** A built-in camera, a USB webcam or a virtual camera. See [Webcam Fallback](../desktop-app/camera-setup/webcam-fallback.md). Tutorial: [Connect a Camera](../tutorials/connect-a-camera.md).
- **Multi camera.** Two or more cameras on one booth, and the customer picks one before every photo (Kiosk, Pix Pro). See [Multi Camera](../desktop-app/camera-setup/multi-camera.md). Tutorial: [Use Two or More Cameras](../tutorials/multi-camera.md).
- **Flash and lighting.** Built-in flash, speedlites and studio strobes on a wireless trigger. See [Lighting & Flash](../desktop-app/camera-setup/lighting-and-flash.md) and the tutorial [Get a Bright Live View with Flash](../tutorials/bright-live-view-with-flash.md).
- **Any printer your computer can print to.** Dye-sub and inkjet, with the paper size read from the driver and the DNP 2-inch cut. See [Supported Printers](./supported-printers.md) and [Printer Setup](../desktop-app/printer-setup.md). Tutorial: [Set Up a Printer](../tutorials/set-up-a-printer.md).
- **One printer for several booths.** Share a printer between booths with your computer's printer sharing. The computer holding the printer does not need Pixture. See [Sharing One Printer Between Booths](../guides/shared-printer.md). Tutorial: [Share One Printer Between Booths](../tutorials/share-a-printer.md).
- **Keeps working offline.** Sessions, prints and payment codes carry on when the venue internet drops, and photos upload when it comes back. See [Offline Mode](../desktop-app/offline-mode.md). Tutorial: [Keep the Booth Running Offline](../tutorials/keep-running-offline.md).
- **Locked to customers, open to you.** A hidden operator menu behind a PIN. See [Operator Menu](../desktop-app/admin-panel.md) and the tutorials [Lock the Booth and Use the Operator Menu](../tutorials/operator-menu.md) and [Lock the Screen to the Booth](../tutorials/lock-the-screen.md).
- **Starts by itself.** The booth app opens when the computer starts, so a booth comes back on its own after a power cut. See [Make the Booth Start by Itself After a Power Cut](../tutorials/start-after-a-power-cut.md).
- **Button keys.** Capture, Retake and Keep on a button box or keyboard keys, set once for the whole account. Tutorial: [Use a Button Box](../tutorials/use-a-button-box.md).

## Photo Sessions

- **Booth modes.** Photo, Studio (a timed pool of shots, then pick the best), Decor (write, draw or type on the photo, in fonts you choose), Newspaper, Video, 360/Slow-mo, Photographer (a roaming photographer) and Mingle (carry the booth to the guests). See [UI Editor](../dashboard/pix-design/ui-editor.md), [Studio Sessions](../desktop-app/studio-sessions.md) and [Events](../dashboard/events.md). Tutorials: [Run a Studio Mode Session](../tutorials/studio-mode.md) and [Run a Decor Session](../tutorials/notes-session.md) and [Run a Newspaper Session](../tutorials/newspaper-session.md) and [Run Video and 360 Sessions](../tutorials/video-and-360-sessions.md) and [Shoot as a Roaming Photographer](../tutorials/photographer-mode.md) and [Take the Booth to the Guests](../tutorials/mingle-mode.md).
- **What customers take home.** Prints, single photos, an animated GIF, a live photo and video clips. See [Capture Modes](../desktop-app/session-flow/capture-modes.md) and [Event Video Modes](../desktop-app/event-video-modes.md). Tutorial: [Set Up the Share Page](../tutorials/share-page.md).
- **Every screen is yours.** Start screen, payment, template choice, capture, review, filters and sharing, each designed in the UI Editor. See [Photo Session Flow](../desktop-app/session-flow/overview.md). Tutorial: [Customise the Booth Screens](../tutorials/customise-kiosk-screens.md).
- **Filters.** 14 built-in looks plus your own .cube filters. See [Filters](../desktop-app/session-flow/filters.md). Tutorial: [Add Filters to Your Booth](../tutorials/add-filters.md).
- **Review and retake.** Customers move, scale and mirror each photo in its slot. See [Review and Adjust](../desktop-app/session-flow/review-and-adjust.md). Tutorial: [Let Customers Review and Retake](../tutorials/review-and-retake.md).
- **AI portraits (beta).** Customers turn a photo into an AI portrait in a style you chose. See [Pix AI](../dashboard/pix-design/pix-ai.md). Tutorial: [Sell AI Portraits From the Share Page (Beta)](../tutorials/sell-ai-portraits.md).

## Sharing

- **QR share page.** Every session gets its own page with the print, the singles, the GIF and the live photo, in your branding. Photos stay for 60 days. See [Sharing](../desktop-app/session-flow/sharing.md) and [Microsite](../dashboard/pix-design/microsite.md). Tutorial: [Set Up the Share Page](../tutorials/share-page.md).
- **Email my photos.** Customers send the photos to their own email from the booth. See [Email My Photos](../desktop-app/session-flow/email-my-photos.md). Tutorial: [Let Customers Email Their Photos](../tutorials/email-my-photos.md).
- **Event albums and slideshow.** One online album per event, with download, embed and a slideshow for a venue screen. See [Events](../dashboard/events.md). Tutorial: [Share an Event Album and Slideshow](../tutorials/event-album-and-slideshow.md).

## Payments

- **QR and card through your own gateway.** DOKU, Midtrans, Xendit, Stripe, Razorpay, Kashier and HitPay, in any currency. The money goes straight to you. See [Payment Gateway Reference](../guides/payment-gateway-setup.md) and [Create a Payment Gateway](../tutorials/create-a-payment-gateway.md). Tutorial: [Accept Payments at the Booth](../tutorials/accept-payments.md).
- **Coins, notes and card readers.** Stripe Terminal, coin and note acceptors, Nayax and EDC readers and a Transpire QR DuitNow terminal. See [Hardware Payments](../desktop-app/hardware-payments.md) and [Take Card, Coin and Cash Payments](../tutorials/connect-a-coin-or-card-reader.md).
- **Packages and prices per booth.** Sessions, prints, extra copies and a price per mode. See [Booth Pricing](../dashboard/booth-pricing.md). Tutorial: [Set Prices, Packages and Extra Prints](../tutorials/prices-and-packages.md).
- **Vouchers.** Session tickets, staff passes and batches of codes, for cash at the counter or gifts. See [Vouchers](../dashboard/vouchers.md). Tutorial: [Sell and Give Vouchers](../tutorials/vouchers.md).
- **Sell sessions online.** Customers pay on their phone and get a code the booth accepts, even offline. See [Sell Sessions Online](../tutorials/sell-sessions-online.md).
- **Promo.** Amount off, a promo price or pay 1 get 1, automatic or with a code. See [Marketing Studio](../dashboard/marketing-studio.md). Tutorial: [Run a Promo](../tutorials/run-a-promo.md).

## Events

- **Events that run themselves.** Dates, modes, outputs, templates, pricing and a start screen per event; booths pick it up on their own. See [Events](../dashboard/events.md) and [Run an Event](../tutorials/run-an-event.md).
- **A start screen per event.** Background, logo, colours and the modes guests choose from. See [Event Start Screen](../dashboard/event-start-screen.md). Tutorial: [Design an Event Start Screen](../tutorials/event-start-screen.md).
- **Event Pass.** 24 hours of Pix Pro on one computer for $5, for the days you work. See [Plans & Pricing](../pricing/plans.md). Tutorial: [Add a Second Computer or Move Pix Pro](../tutorials/add-or-move-a-computer.md).

## Self-Service Booths

- **Queue line.** Customers take a number on their phone and are called by a TV screen and a voice. See [Queue Line](../guides/queue-line.md) and [Queue Display](../dashboard/pix-design/queue-display.md). Tutorial: [Run a Queue Line](../tutorials/queue-line.md).
- **Multi-Booth groups.** Several booths with shared settings, a shared queue and a central cashier. See [Multi-Booth Setup](../guides/multi-booth-setup.md). Tutorial: [Set Up a Multi-Booth Group](../tutorials/multi-booth-group.md).
- **Frame Lab.** Customers design their own frame on their phone and use it at the booth. See [Frame Lab](../tutorials/frame-lab.md).
- **Your own Website.** A page at `handle.pixture.io` with your booths, a shop, Frame Lab and photo lookup. See [Website](../dashboard/pix-design/website.md). Tutorial: [Publish Your Website](../tutorials/publish-your-website.md).
- **Marketing Studio.** Consent photos, your customer audience, campaigns and how they perform. See [Marketing Studio](../dashboard/marketing-studio.md). Tutorial: [Collect Consent Photos and Run a Campaign](../tutorials/marketing-studio.md).

## Design

- **Brand once, use everywhere.** Logo, colours and fonts that every screen, page and event starts from. See [Brand](../dashboard/pix-design/brand.md). Tutorial: [Set Up Your Brand](../tutorials/set-up-your-brand.md).
- **Print templates.** From your own PNG or JPG, with photo slots, folders and categories. See [Template Editor](../dashboard/pix-design/creating-templates.md) and [Design a Print Template](../tutorials/design-a-print-template.md).
- **Booth screens.** See [UI Editor](../dashboard/pix-design/ui-editor.md). Tutorial: [Customise the Booth Screens](../tutorials/customise-kiosk-screens.md).
- **GIF and video overlays.** See [Photo Filters and Overlays](../dashboard/pix-design/filters-and-overlays.md). Tutorial: [Add GIF and Video Overlays](../tutorials/gif-and-video-overlays.md).

## Running Your Business

- **One dashboard for every booth.** Revenue, sessions, prints and top templates for a day, week or month. See [Dashboard Home](../dashboard/dashboard-home.md). Tutorial: [Read Your Dashboard](../tutorials/read-your-dashboard.md).
- **Kiosk and event money apart.** Pixture Kiosk, Pixture Event and an Overview of both. See [Pixture Event, Pixture Kiosk and Overview](../dashboard/views.md). Tutorial: [Read Your Dashboard](../tutorials/read-your-dashboard.md).
- **Booth health.** Online status, camera, printer faults, paper and errors for every booth, with alerts. See [Health](../dashboard/health.md). Tutorial: [Watch Booth Health and Get Alerts](../tutorials/booth-health-alerts.md).
- **Gallery and transactions.** Every photo and every payment, searchable and exportable. See [Gallery](../dashboard/gallery.md) and [Transactions](../dashboard/transactions.md). Tutorial: [Find a Photo or a Payment](../tutorials/gallery-and-transactions.md).
- **Profit and loss.** Revenue, booking fees, costs, paper per sheet, depreciation and tax for a month or the year, per kiosk, per event or for the whole business. See [Finance](../dashboard/finance.md). Tutorial: [Track Profit and Loss](../tutorials/profit-and-loss.md).
- **PDF statements.** A Profit and Loss Statement for the business and a statement for each partner (Pix Pro). See [Statements as PDF](../dashboard/finance.md#statements-as-pdf). Tutorial: [Track Profit and Loss](../tutorials/profit-and-loss.md).
- **Partners.** A venue, an investor or staff on a revenue share, a profit share, a fixed monthly amount, a capital repayment or a minimum guarantee (Pix Pro). See [Share Revenue and Profit with Partners](../tutorials/share-revenue-and-profit.md).
- **Statements by email.** Each partner gets their own statement with the PDF attached, every month or every week, and you and your accountant get the Profit and Loss Statement. Mark statements as paid (Pix Pro). See [Statements by Email](../dashboard/finance.md#statements-by-email). Tutorial: [Share Revenue and Profit with Partners](../tutorials/share-revenue-and-profit.md).
- **Your team.** Members and roles, down to which booths each person can reach. See [Settings](../dashboard/settings.md). Tutorial: [Add Your Team and Set Roles](../tutorials/team-and-roles.md).
- **Any country, any currency.** Any timezone and ISO currency, a different currency per booth if you need it. The dashboard is in English and Bahasa Indonesia, and the booth's customer language is your choice. See [Settings](../dashboard/settings.md). Tutorial: [Set Your Country, Currency and Language](../tutorials/country-currency-language.md).
- **The iPhone app.** Pixture Manager runs your account from your phone, with booth alerts. See [Pixture Manager for iPhone](../iphone-app/overview.md). Tutorial: [Run Your Account from Your iPhone](../tutorials/iphone-app.md).
- **Lucy and AI assistants.** Lucy answers questions in the dashboard and the iPhone app, and Claude or ChatGPT can work with your account through the Pixture connector. See [Connect an AI Assistant](../guides/connect-an-ai-assistant.md). Tutorial: [Ask Lucy and Connect Claude or ChatGPT](../tutorials/lucy-and-ai-assistants.md).
- **Plans.** Pix Starter is free on one computer with a watermark. Pix Pro is per computer, monthly or yearly. See [Plans & Pricing](../pricing/plans.md) and [Feature Comparison](../pricing/feature-comparison.md). Tutorial: [Add a Second Computer or Move Pix Pro](../tutorials/add-or-move-a-computer.md).
