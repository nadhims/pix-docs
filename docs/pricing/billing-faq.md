# Billing FAQ

Common questions about how billing works in Pix.

## How does billing work?

Business Monthly is billed on the same date each month. Business Yearly is billed once per year. Your subscription renews automatically unless you cancel before the next billing date.

## When does a Day Pass activate?

A Day Pass activates the moment you purchase it. From that point, you have exactly 24 hours of full access to every feature. The countdown runs continuously — it does not pause overnight.

:::caution
Plan your Day Pass purchase to start close to your event time. If you buy it the night before, several hours will be used up before guests arrive.
:::

## Can I upgrade from Starter to Business?

Yes. You can upgrade at any time from the Pix dashboard. When you upgrade to Business Monthly or Yearly, billing starts immediately and you get instant access to all features.

## Can I downgrade from Business to Starter?

Yes. When you cancel a Business subscription, you retain access until the end of your current billing period. After that, your account reverts to the Starter plan. Your data is preserved, but features like sharing, printing, and analytics will be disabled.

## Can I switch between Monthly and Yearly?

Yes. If you switch from Monthly to Yearly, the change takes effect at the start of your next billing cycle. If you switch from Yearly to Monthly, it takes effect when your annual term ends.

## What payment methods do you accept?

Pix accepts major credit and debit cards. Payment is processed securely through our billing provider.

## Do you offer refunds?

Day Passes are non-refundable once activated. For Business subscriptions, contact support within 7 days of a charge if you believe it was made in error. We handle refund requests on a case-by-case basis.

:::info
Cancelling your subscription stops future charges but does not trigger a refund for the current period. You keep access until the period ends.
:::

## What happens to my data if I stop paying?

Your account reverts to Starter. All your photos, templates, and booth configurations are preserved. You can access them again by resubscribing. Pix does not delete your data when a subscription lapses.

## Is there a free trial?

The Starter plan is free forever with no time limit. Use it to evaluate Pix before committing to a paid plan. For a full-feature trial, a single Day Pass at $7 is the most affordable way to test everything.
