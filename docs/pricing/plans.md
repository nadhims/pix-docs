# Plans & Pricing

Pix offers flexible plans designed around how photobooth operators actually work — whether you run events once in a while or manage booths full-time.

## Starter — Free

The Starter plan is completely free and lets you get up and running with one booth and two templates. It's ideal for testing Pix before committing to a paid plan.

**Includes:**
- 1 booth
- 2 templates
- All soft file types (composite, GIF, live photo, singles)
- Photo filters
- Camera support (Canon DSLR + webcam)
- Offline resilience

**Not included:** Template editor, UI editor, microsites, printer support, real-time analytics, booth health, license management, custom branding, or payment gateway integration.

## Day Pass — $5 per day

The Day Pass unlocks every feature for a single 24-hour activation window. Perfect for operators who run occasional events and don't need a monthly subscription.

**Includes everything in Business**, activated from the moment you purchase. The pass expires exactly 24 hours later.

:::tip When to use a Day Pass
If you run fewer than three events per month, Day Passes are more cost-effective than a monthly subscription.
:::

## Business Monthly — $40/month

The full Pix experience on a monthly billing cycle. Manage multiple booths, use unlimited templates, and access every feature.

**Includes:**
- Unlimited booths
- Unlimited templates + template editor
- UI editor (drag-and-drop kiosk interface design)
- All soft file types (composite, GIF, live photo, singles)
- Photo filters (14 built-in + custom .CUBE LUT upload)
- Microsites (QR sharing + branded guest pages)
- Printer support with alignment controls
- Offline resilience with automatic cloud sync
- Real-time analytics with booth leaderboard
- Booth health monitoring
- License management and transfer
- Camera support (Canon DSLR + webcam)
- Payment gateway integration
- Custom branding

## Business Yearly — $25/month ($300/year)

Everything in Business Monthly at a reduced annual rate. Pay once per year and save compared to monthly billing.

## Choosing a Plan

| Scenario | Recommended Plan |
|---|---|
| Trying Pix for the first time | Starter |
| One-off event this weekend | Day Pass |
| Running events every week | Business Monthly |
| Full-time booth operator | Business Yearly |

All paid plans include the same feature set. The only difference is billing frequency and cost. You can upgrade or switch plans at any time from the dashboard.
