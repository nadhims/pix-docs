# Plans & Pricing

Pixture is priced per computer, not per account. Booths are free profiles: create as many as you like in the Pixture dashboard. What you pay for is Pix Pro on each computer that runs the Pixture Photobooth app.

Your account is **Pix Starter** while no computer has Pix Pro, and **Pix Pro** the moment one does, whether through a subscription, a running Event Pass or the free trial. You buy and manage everything on the **Computers** page of the dashboard.

## Pix Starter (free)

Pix Starter is for trying Pixture and for booths that do not charge customers.

- **One computer.** Pairing a second computer is refused at the pairing code. Replacing the computer on a booth that already has one still works.
- **Watermarked output.** Every photo and every print carries the Pixture watermark.
- **Unlimited booth profiles**, two ready-made print templates and the default screen design.
- **Pix Design is open to try.** Creating or saving your own designs needs Pix Pro; the dashboard offers **Start free trial** or **Buy Pix Pro** when you try. Marketing Studio needs Pix Pro.
- **No payment screen.** Every photo session is free and the booth skips the Payment screen.

You do not even need an account to try the booth: install the Pixture Photobooth app and tap **Try it without an account** on the login screen. It runs with built-in frames and screens, watermarked, without cloud sharing.

## Event Pass

**$5 or Rp 85.000.** 24 hours of Pix Pro on one computer. The 24 hours start only when you use the Event Pass at the booth.

- Buy any time, in any quantity, at a flat price. Unused Event Passes wait in your account; the **Event Passes** card on the Computers page shows "N unused".
- Use one at the booth: on an event, tap **Use Event Pass** when the booth asks "Use an Event Pass for today?". Any other time, open the operator menu and tap **Use an Event Pass on this computer**. A computer paired while you hold unused Event Passes stays blocked until you use one on it.
- While it runs, the booth shows a badge "Event Pass until HH:mm".
- If its computer is unlinked, deactivated, removed or signs out, a running Event Pass is set aside with the same end time, and the next event computer that starts its booth continues it. A computer in Kiosk mode cannot pick it up. Its 24 hours keep counting while it waits.
- Event Passes never renew.

:::tip When an Event Pass fits
Weddings, markets, brand activations and school events, where you pass the cost on to the client. If a computer runs more than about eight event days a month, Pix Pro Monthly is cheaper.
:::

## Pix Pro Monthly

**$39.99 or Rp 850.000 per computer per month.**

- Paid by card, it renews every month until you cancel.
- In Indonesia, purchases through DOKU are prepaid for one month and do not renew. The checkout card says "Per computer, prepaid month".

## Pix Pro Yearly

**$299.99 per computer per year**, about $25 per month. In Indonesia **Rp 6.500.000 per year**. The checkout marks it **Best value**.

## Several computers at once

Adding several computers in one purchase gives a volume discount on Pix Pro. Open **Computers > Add computer > Pix Pro**, set **How many computers?** and open **Volume pricing** to see the price per computer before you pay.

Running a large fleet? The **Enterprise** tab on [pixture.io/pricing](https://pixture.io/pricing) offers "Many booths, one quote": click **Contact sales**, fill in the form and click **Send to sales**.

## Free 3-Day Trial

Every account can start one free trial: 3 days of Pix Pro on one computer, no card needed. It starts when the booth computer connects.

1. In the dashboard, tap **Start free trial** on the **You have a 3-day free trial** card in the **Pair a computer** popup, or open the trial popup from the bell menu. On the booth, the operator menu offers the same.
2. Pick the booth. The trial goes on the computer paired to it.

While the trial runs your account is Pix Pro. If you **Deactivate** that computer on the Computers page, the trial returns to your pool and the next computer that checks in takes it. It still ends for good 3 days after it started.

## Pix AI credits

Pix AI is in beta and available to beta accounts. Every generated image costs 1 credit. Credits are shared by every booth in the account and work on any plan. Buy them under **Computers > Pix AI credits > Manage credits**, as packs of 100, 400 or 1000 credits, or as a monthly plan.

## How you pay

- **Internationally:** checkout in US dollars by card, Apple Pay or PayPal, through Lemon Squeezy. Monthly and Yearly renew on the card; manage it under **Settings > Pixture billing**.
- **In Indonesia:** DOKU checkout in rupiah with QRIS, virtual account or e-wallet. Nothing is stored, and purchases are prepaid.

Prices above are list prices. In Indonesia a limited-time sale may show at checkout, for example "Limited-time sale: 20% off Pix Pro" with the list price struck through. Promotions running right now are shown on [pixture.io](https://pixture.io). The Indonesian checkout has a **Promo code** field; each code works once per account.
