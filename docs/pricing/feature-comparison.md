# Feature Comparison

Use the table below to see exactly what's included in each Pix plan. Day Pass and Business plans share the same feature set — the difference is billing (24-hour vs. monthly/yearly).

:::tip
Every capture mode — photo, GIF, and live photo — is available on all plans, including Starter. Paid plans unlock the sharing, printing, and management features that make your booth business-ready.
:::

## What "Offline Capture" Means

All plans support capturing photos without an internet connection. The difference is what happens afterward:

- **Starter**: Photos are stored locally on the device. No cloud upload or QR sharing.
- **Day Pass / Business**: Photos queue locally and automatically upload when connectivity returns. QR codes and guest pages become available once the upload completes.

## What "Payment Gateway" Means

Business and Day Pass plans let you connect a payment provider (Xendit, DOKU, or Midtrans) so guests can pay per session directly at the booth. This is optional — you can run a booth without guest-facing payments if your business model doesn't require it.

:::info
Need help deciding? If you run events regularly, Business Yearly offers the best value — especially at the current launch price of $150/year.
:::
