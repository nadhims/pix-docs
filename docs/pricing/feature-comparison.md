# Feature Comparison

Every paid plan activates one computer. A computer on a Day Pass has the same features as one on Pix Pro, for 24 hours. Booths are free profiles on every plan: create as many as you like. Your account is Pix Starter while it has no active device: it runs on **one computer**, watermarked, and pairing a second is refused at the pairing code, though replacing the computer on a booth that already has one always works. The moment one device is active, the account is Pix Pro, and pairing then follows however many devices your subscription covers.

## Notes

- **Templates**: while your account has no active device, Pix Design's Template Editor is open to try but nothing saves. The moment you own any device or unspent pass, saving opens up, unlimited, on every computer.
- **Watermark**: while your account has no active device at all, every photo and print carries the Pixture watermark, and so does the microsite. It stops on the photo sessions a computer runs after you put a pass or Pix Pro on it.
- **Starter pairing**: while your account has no active device or unspent pass, it can pair **one computer**. A second computer on a different booth is refused at the pairing code; replacing the computer on a booth that already has one always works.
- **Blocked**: once one device is active the account is Pix Pro, and a computer past what your subscription covers stays paired but cannot start a session until you deactivate another computer, use a pass on it, or add devices to your subscription. Once every device is active, a brand new computer cannot pair at all until you free one up or add another, unless you hold an unspent pass, which lets it pair but leaves it blocked until you use one. A Pix Pro account never has a watermarked computer.
- **Custom kiosk designs**: on Starter you can open the UI Editor and design your kiosk screens to try, but saving needs Pix Pro. The payment screen stays off until the computer runs Pix Pro.
- **Payment gateway**: Pix takes 0% of your photo session revenue; DOKU's own fees apply.
