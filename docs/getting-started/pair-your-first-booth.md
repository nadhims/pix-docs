# Pair Your First Booth

By the end of this page the booth PC is linked to a booth in your dashboard, has downloaded that booth's templates and screen design, and is showing its start screen. The dashboard shows the booth as Online with the computer's name. Pairing takes two minutes.

## Before You Start

- The [Pixture Photobooth app is installed](./download-desktop-app.md) and open on its pairing screen on the booth PC.
- You are logged in to the dashboard on any other device, for example your phone.
- A booth to pair. Quick setup already created one; you can also create a new one below.

## Steps

1. Open **Booths** in the sidebar. The booth from Quick setup is listed with no computer and a dash in the **Plan** column.

   ![The Booths page of a new account with two booths that have no device yet and a dash for the plan](/img/docs/steps/getting-started-pair-your-first-booth--booths-list-new-account.webp)

   To add another booth, click **+ New Booth**, stay on the **Single booth** tab, enter a **Booth Name** and, if you like, a **Location**, then click **Create**. Booths are free, so create one for every photobooth you run.

   ![The Create Booth modal on the Single booth tab with Booth Name and Location filled](/img/docs/steps/getting-started-pair-your-first-booth--booths-create-modal.webp)

2. Click **Configure** on the booth. The first time you open a booth that has no computer, a popup says "Pair your booth first" and explains that the booth is not paired to a computer yet. Click **Generate pairing code**.

   ![The Pair your booth first popup on an unpaired booth with the Generate pairing code and Maybe later buttons](/img/docs/steps/getting-started-pair-your-first-booth--booth-pair-first-popup.webp)

   If you clicked **Maybe later** earlier, open the booth's **Computer** tab and click **Generate code** under **Pairing & access** instead. Both open the same modal.

3. The **Pair a computer** popup shows a 6-digit code and the line "Open Pixture Photobooth on the booth computer and enter this Pairing Code." The code expires in 10 minutes; the "Code expires in" countdown is on the popup. **Copy code** copies it, **New code** makes a fresh one.

   Until the app is installed on one of your computers, the popup shows two steps around the code: "Install Pixture Photobooth on the booth computer" with **Download for Windows** and **Download for Mac**, then "Open it and enter this code".

   If your free trial is still unused and no Pix Pro or Event Pass is free for the computer, the popup first shows the **You have a 3-day free trial** card. **Start free trial** shows the code with a **Free trial ready for this computer** chip, and your 3 days start when the computer connects. **Pair without the trial** shows a plain code, and **Later** closes the popup.

   ![The Pair a computer popup with a 6-digit code, the Code expires in countdown, and the Copy code, New code and Done buttons](/img/docs/steps/getting-started-pair-your-first-booth--booth-pairing-code.webp)

4. On the booth PC, type the 6 digits on the app's pairing screen.
5. The booth downloads its templates, screen design and pricing and shows the start screen. Click **Done** on the popup. Back in the dashboard, the booth's status turns **Online** and the **Computer** tab shows the computer's name, its fingerprint and when it was last seen.

   ![The booth Computer tab with a paired computer showing its name, fingerprint, registered and last seen times, and the Rename computer and Unlink buttons](/img/docs/steps/getting-started-pair-your-first-booth--booth-device-tab-paired.webp)

## What Happens Next

- **The badge on the booth.** Open the operator menu on the booth (tap the top-right corner twice within three seconds). The footer shows the booth name, your email and a plan badge: **Watermarked** on Pix Starter, **Pix Pro**, **Trial until …** or **Event Pass until …** once the computer holds Pix Pro. See [Pix Pro on the Booth](../desktop-app/licence-on-the-booth.md).
- **On Pix Starter**, one computer is all the account runs. Pairing a second computer is refused at the code. Replacing the computer on a booth that already has one works: click **Unlink** on the **Computer** tab, then generate a new code for the new computer.
- **On a Pix Pro account**, a spare place on your subscription attaches itself to the computer when it checks in. If every place is taken, the computer stays paired but cannot start a session until you click **Deactivate** on another computer or **Add computer** on the **Computers** page. If the popup says "All N computers are in use", free a place or add a computer, then generate the code again.
- **One computer per booth.** The **Generate code** button is disabled while a computer is linked. To move the booth to a different computer, **Unlink** the old one first. Pix Pro and the free trial follow to the new computer. A running Event Pass keeps running and goes to the next event computer that starts its booth.
- **Online means a recent check-in.** The booth checks in every few minutes; the dashboard shows it Online when it has been heard from in the last 12 minutes.

:::tip
Pair from your phone. Open the dashboard in the phone's browser, generate the code, and type it on the booth while standing in front of it.
:::
