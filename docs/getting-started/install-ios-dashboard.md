# Install the iOS Dashboard

The Pix Dashboard iOS app lets you manage your photobooth business from your iPhone or iPad -- monitor revenue, swap templates, and handle billing without touching your booth computer.

## Download

1. Open the **App Store** on your iPhone or iPad.
2. Search for **"Pix Dashboard"**.
3. Tap **Get** to download and install.

<!-- Screenshot: Pix Dashboard on the App Store -->

## Requirements

- **iOS 26** or later
- Works on both **iPhone** and **iPad**

:::caution
The iOS Dashboard requires iOS 26+. If your device is on an older version, you can manage everything through the web dashboard at pixture.io instead.
:::

## Sign In

1. Open the Pix Dashboard app.
2. Sign in with the **same email and password** you used to create your Pix account.
3. Optionally enable **Face ID / Touch ID** for quick access.

:::tip
The iOS Dashboard and Desktop App share the same account. Any changes you make on one (templates, booth settings, billing) are reflected everywhere.
:::

## What You Can Do

From the iOS Dashboard you can:

- **Dashboard tab** -- View session counts and revenue at a glance
- **Booths tab** -- Create and configure booths, assign templates
- **Gallery tab** -- Browse captured photos across all booths
- **Billing tab** -- Manage your subscription and payment methods
