# Create Your Account

By the end of this page you have a Pixture account, an organisation named after your business, your first booth, and a dashboard that is waiting for the Pix Desktop App to be installed. Signing up takes about five minutes and asks for no card.

## Before You Start

- An email address you can open right away.
- A phone number on WhatsApp, for the 6-digit verification code. There is an escape hatch if your number does not use WhatsApp.

## Sign Up

1. Go to [pixture.io/signup](https://pixture.io/signup). The page is titled **Create your account** and reminds you that no credit card is needed.
2. Pick one way in: **Sign up with Google**, **Sign up with Apple**, or type your email address and click **Sign up with email**.

   ![The Create your account page with Sign up with Google, Sign up with Apple and an email address field](/img/docs/signup-page.webp)

3. If you used email, the page says **Check your email.** Open the message from pixture.io and click the link. It opens the Complete your account page. If nothing arrives within a few minutes, check your spam folder.

## Complete Your Account

1. On **Complete your account**, your email is already filled in. Set a **Password** and repeat it under **Confirm Password**. The password needs at least 8 characters with an upper-case letter, a lower-case letter, a number and a special character.
2. Enter your **Full Name** and your **Business Name**. The business name becomes the name of your organisation in the dashboard; you can change it later.
3. Enter your phone number and click **Send code via WhatsApp**. Type the 6-digit code from WhatsApp and click **Verify**. If your number does not use WhatsApp, click **My number doesn't use WhatsApp** instead.

   ![The Complete your account page with password, full name, business name and WhatsApp number fields](/img/docs/complete-profile.webp)

4. Click **Complete account**.

If you signed up with Google or Apple, you see a shorter **Set up your account** page instead: enter your **Business name**, verify your phone number the same way, and click **Get started**.

## Quick Setup

The first thing the dashboard shows is onboarding. It has two steps.

1. On **1 · Quick setup**, check the fields:

   | Field | What to enter |
   |---|---|
   | **Organization Name** | Your business name, already filled in from sign-up |
   | **Timezone** | Detected from your browser and shown as "Detected: …". Change it if the booth lives somewhere else |
   | **Currency** | The currency your booths charge in. Any ISO currency |
   | **Booth Name** | The name of your first booth. The hint says: "Give it a name that helps you identify this booth's purpose or location." |
   | **Location** | Optional, for example the mall or venue |

   ![Onboarding step 1, Quick setup, with Organization Name, Timezone, Currency, Booth Name and Location filled and a Continue button](/img/docs/onboarding-quick-setup.webp)

2. Click **Continue**. **Skip for now** also works; you can rename the organisation later under **Settings > General** and set the timezone and currency under **Settings > Preferences**.
3. On **2 · You're ready**, the dashboard lists what comes next: install the app, pair your booth, run a test session. Click **Go to Dashboard**.

   ![Onboarding step 2, You're ready, listing Install the app, Pair your booth and Run a test session with a Go to Dashboard button](/img/docs/onboarding-ready.webp)

## Your New Dashboard

You land on the dashboard home. Until the booth is paired and has run a session, every page shows a setup banner: "Your photobooth isn't fully working yet. Complete your setup to start taking photos (2 of 4 steps done)." with **Watch the setup video** and **Install the app**. The banner goes away on its own once setup is complete.

![The dashboard of a brand new account with the setup banner showing 2 of 4 steps done and an Install the app button](/img/docs/dashboard-new-account.webp)

A few things worth knowing on day one:

- **One account, one organisation.** Signing up creates one organisation for your business. Everything you make, booths, templates, events and transactions, belongs to it.
- **Staff do not sign up separately.** Invite them from **Settings > Members > + Invite** with an email address and a role. They accept the invitation by email and join your organisation.
- **Booths are free.** Create as many as you like under **Booths**. What you pay for later is Pix Pro on the computers that run them.
- **The account starts on Pix Starter.** One computer, watermarked output, two ready-made templates and the default kiosk design. See [Choose Your Plan](./choose-plan.md).
