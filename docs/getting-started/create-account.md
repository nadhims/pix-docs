# Create Your Account

By the end of this page you have a Pixture account, an organisation named after your business, your first booth, and a dashboard that is waiting for the Pixture Photobooth app to be installed. Signing up takes about five minutes and asks for no card.

## Before You Start

- An email address you can open right away.
- A phone number on WhatsApp, for the 6-digit verification code. There is an escape hatch if your number does not use WhatsApp.

## Sign Up

1. Go to [pixture.io/signup](https://pixture.io/signup). The page is titled **Create your account** and reminds you that no credit card is needed.
2. Pick one way in: **Sign up with Google**, **Sign up with Apple**, or type your email address and click **Sign up with email**.

   ![The Create your account page with Sign up with Google, Sign up with Apple and an email address field](/img/docs/steps/getting-started-create-account--signup-page.webp)

3. If you used email, the page says **Check your email.** Open the message from pixture.io and click the link. It opens the Complete your account page. If nothing arrives within a few minutes, check your spam folder.

## Complete Your Account

1. On **Complete your account**, your email is already filled in. Set a **Password** and repeat it under **Confirm Password**. The password needs at least 8 characters with an upper-case letter, a lower-case letter, a number and a special character.
2. Enter your **Full Name** and your **Business Name**. The business name becomes the name of your organisation in the dashboard; you can change it later.
3. Enter your phone number and click **Send code via WhatsApp**. Type the 6-digit code from WhatsApp and click **Verify**. If your number does not use WhatsApp, click **My number doesn't use WhatsApp** instead.

   ![The Complete your account page with password, full name, business name and WhatsApp number fields](/img/docs/complete-profile.webp)

4. Click **Complete account**.

If you signed up with Google or Apple, you see a shorter **Set up your account** page instead: enter your **Business name**, verify your phone number the same way, and click **Get started**.

## Set Up With Lucy

The first thing the dashboard shows is a short chat with Lucy, the dashboard assistant. It takes a minute. **Skip setup** in the top bar leaves it at any time.

1. Lucy says hello and asks "Which one sounds like you?" Pick a card:

   | Card | For |
   |---|---|
   | **I run events** | Weddings, parties |
   | **I run a kiosk** | A self-service photobooth |
   | **I do both** | Events and a kiosk |
   | **I'm just starting** | No booth yet |

   Lucy answers with the words you'll see a lot (or, if you are just starting, what a photobooth business needs). **Change** under your answer picks again.

2. "Quick check of your details. Change anything that's wrong." Check the fields:

   | Field | What to enter |
   |---|---|
   | **Business name** | Already filled in from sign-up |
   | **Timezone** | Picked from your browser. Change it if the booth lives somewhere else |
   | **Currency** | The currency your booths charge in |
   | **Booth name** | The name of your first booth. Kiosk and both only |
   | **Location** | Optional, for example a mall, cafe or city. Kiosk and both only |

3. Click **Looks good**. The details fold into one line with an **Edit** link. You can change them later under **Settings**.
4. Lucy suggests the next step, with a few quick questions you can tap to ask her:
   - **I run events**: **Create my first event**.
   - **I run a kiosk**: **Set up my kiosk**.
   - **I do both**: **Create my first event** or **Set up my kiosk**.
   - **I'm just starting**: **Try it on this computer**. This opens an install card: download the Pixture Photobooth app, then pair it with the code shown. If your account has no Pix Pro yet, the card offers **Start free trial** (3 days of Pix Pro on that computer) or **Pair without the trial**, and **Go to Dashboard** when you are done.

   ![Onboarding chat with Lucy](/img/docs/onboarding-quick-setup.webp)

## Your New Dashboard

You land on the dashboard. Until the booth is paired and has run a session, every page shows a setup banner: "Your photobooth isn't live yet. N of 3 done: install the app, pair it, run a test session." Its button is the next step (**Install the app**, **Pair your photobooth** or **Run a test session**), and **Watch the setup video** may show next to it. The banner goes away on its own once setup is complete.

![The dashboard of a brand new account with the setup banner](/img/docs/dashboard-new-account.webp)

A few things worth knowing on day one:

- **One account, one organisation.** Signing up creates one organisation for your business. Everything you make, booths, templates, events and transactions, belongs to it.
- **Staff do not sign up separately.** Invite them from **Settings > Members > + Invite** with an email address and a role. They accept the invitation by email and join your organisation.
- **Booths are free.** Create as many as you like under **Booths**. What you pay for later is Pix Pro on the computers that run them.
- **The account starts on Pix Starter.** One computer, watermarked output, two ready-made templates and the default screen design. See [Choose Your Plan](./choose-plan.md).
