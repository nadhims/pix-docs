# Your First Session

Everything is set up -- camera connected, templates assigned, app running. Let's walk through what your guests will experience.

## The Session Flow

### 1. Home Screen

The booth displays your branded home screen. A guest taps the screen (or you tap it for them) to begin.

<!-- Screenshot: Home screen with start prompt -->

### 2. Template Selection

The guest picks a photo template from the available options. Templates define the layout -- how many photos are taken and how they're arranged in the final composite.

<!-- Screenshot: Template selection grid -->

### 3. Capture

The countdown begins and the camera fires automatically. Depending on the template, guests pose for one or more shots. They see a live preview between takes so they can adjust.

:::tip
Pix supports three capture modes: **standard photos**, **GIFs**, and **live photos**. The mode depends on the template assigned to your booth.
:::

### 4. Filters

After capture, guests can optionally apply filters to their photos before finalizing.

### 5. Sharing

The final composite is ready! Guests can:

- **Scan a QR code** to download the photo to their phone instantly
- **Print** the composite on a connected photo printer

<!-- Screenshot: Sharing screen with QR code -->

The session ends and the booth returns to the home screen, ready for the next guest.
