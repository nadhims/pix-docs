# Your First Photo Session

Everything is set up -- camera connected, templates assigned, app running. Let's walk through what your customers will experience.

## The Photo Session Flow

### 1. Home Screen

The booth displays your branded home screen. A customer taps the screen (or you tap it for them) to begin.

<!-- Screenshot: Home screen with start prompt -->

### 2. Template Selection

The customer picks a photo template from the available options. Templates define the layout -- how many photos are taken and how they're arranged in the final composite.

<!-- Screenshot: Template selection grid -->

### 3. Capture

The countdown begins and the camera fires automatically. Depending on the template, customers pose for one or more shots. They see a live preview between takes so they can adjust.

:::tip
A photo session can produce the printed **composite**, individual **singles**, a **GIF**, and a **Live Photo**, depending on the booth's sharing settings. The mode depends on the template assigned to your booth.
:::

### 4. Filters

After capture, customers can optionally apply filters to their photos before finalizing.

### 5. Sharing

The final composite is ready! Customers can:

- **Scan a QR code** to download the photo to their phone instantly
- **Print** the composite on a connected photo printer

<!-- Screenshot: Sharing screen with QR code -->

The photo session ends and the booth returns to the home screen, ready for the next customer.
