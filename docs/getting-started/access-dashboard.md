# Access the Pix Dashboard

The Pix Dashboard lets you manage your photobooth business from any device -- monitor revenue, swap templates, and handle billing without touching your booth computer. There's nothing to install: it's a website at **pixture.io** that works from any desktop or mobile browser.

## Log In

1. Go to [pixture.io](https://pixture.io) in your browser -- desktop, phone, or tablet.
2. Sign in with the **same email and password** you used to create your Pix account.

<!-- Screenshot: Pix Dashboard on a mobile browser -->

:::tip
The Pix Dashboard and Desktop App share the same account. Any changes you make on one (templates, booth settings, billing) are reflected everywhere.
:::

## What You Can Do

From the Pix Dashboard you can:

- **Dashboard tab** -- View photo session counts and revenue at a glance
- **Booths tab** -- Create and configure booths, assign templates
- **Gallery tab** -- Browse captured photos across all booths
- **Licenses** -- your per-booth plans and licence pool
