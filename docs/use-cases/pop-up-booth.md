# Pop-Up Booth

A pop-up booth is a knockdown box that packs into a car and sets up in under an hour at a bazaar, a night market, a festival or a campus fair. Guests pay per session and you pay the organiser a stall fee. Income comes in bursts, a month's worth on one good weekend, and the internet is whatever the venue has. This page shows how to set that up in Pixture.

## The Setup

### 1. Pay for the days you work

Put a **Day Pass** on the booth computer for each event day, or Pix Pro if you go out most weekends. Buy passes ahead on the **Licenses** page. See [Plans & Pricing](../pricing/plans.md).

### 2. Take payment at the stall

- Connect a gateway for QR payments, the same as for a fixed booth. See [Create a Payment Gateway](../tutorials/create-a-payment-gateway.md).
- Put the **Online shop link** on a sign at the stall: guests buy a session on their phone and get a code the booth accepts even when the venue Wi-Fi is down. See [Sell Sessions Online](../tutorials/sell-sessions-online.md).
- For cash, take it yourself and hand over codes from a voucher batch generated before you leave, with the event date as the **Expiration Date**. See [Take Card, Coin and Cash Payments](../tutorials/connect-a-coin-or-card-reader.md#setup-d-cash-at-the-counter-as-voucher-codes).

### 3. Plan for weak internet

Bring a 4G router or a phone hotspot. If the line drops, the booth keeps taking and printing photos and uploads them later. See [Offline Mode](../desktop-app/offline-mode.md).

### 4. Build your following

Keep **Link Sharing** on and brand the share page, so every guest leaves with your name on their photos. The following you build at pop-ups is what sells event bookings later. See [Microsite (Share Page)](../dashboard/pix-design/microsite.md).

### 5. Learn from each weekend

Compare weekends on the **Transactions** period filter before committing to a fixed spot or raising prices.
