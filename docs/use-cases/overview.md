# Use Cases

There are four common ways to run a photo booth business, and each one uses Pixture a little differently. A self-service booth has to run on its own all day, an event booth is booked and paid for by a client, a multi-booth studio needs several booths to act as one, and a pop-up box works wherever the crowd is this weekend. Pick the one closest to your business; each page walks through the setup from plan to reporting and links to the tutorials for the detailed steps.

| You run | Who pays | What matters most | Page |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A self-service booth** in a mall, café, campus or cinema | Every guest, at the booth, with nobody there | Taking payment unattended, staying online, fresh frames | [Self-Service Photobooth](./self-service-photobooth.md) |
| **Event rentals** for weddings, corporate dinners and launches | The client, a flat fee per event | A branded look per event, free sessions for guests, the album for the client | [Event Rental](./event-rental.md) |
| **A multi-booth studio**, four to eight booths in one unit | Every guest, at the counter or at the booth | Setting booths up once, one queue, one cashier | [Multi-Booth Studio](./multi-booth-studio.md) |
| **A pop-up box** at markets, festivals and campus fairs | Every guest, at the stall | Quick setup, paying for the days you work, weak internet | [Pop-Up Booth](./pop-up-booth.md) |

Many operators mix them: a studio that also takes wedding bookings, or a mall booth that goes to a festival on the weekend. One Pixture account runs all of it; each booth and each event keeps its own templates, prices and reports.

## What Every Setup Shares

Whichever model you run, the first steps are the same:

1. [Create your account](../getting-started/create-account.md) and [install the Pix Desktop App](../getting-started/download-desktop-app.md) on the booth computer.
2. [Pair the booth](../getting-started/pair-your-first-booth.md) and [set it up](../tutorials/set-up-a-booth.md): templates, kiosk design, filters and pricing.
3. Decide how the computer is licensed: Pix Pro by the month or year for a booth that works most days, a Day Pass for a booth that works some days. See [Plans & Pricing](../pricing/plans.md).
4. Connect a way to get paid, unless your client pays you directly. See [Create a Payment Gateway](../tutorials/create-a-payment-gateway.md).
