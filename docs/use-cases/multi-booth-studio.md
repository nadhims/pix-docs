# Multi-Booth Studio

A multi-booth studio puts four to eight booths side by side in one unit, each with its own theme or background, with one attendant for all of them. Guests pick a booth the way they would pick a filter. Revenue grows with every booth while rent and staff stay the same, but so does the work: every booth has its own computer, printer and templates to keep in sync. This page shows how Pixture keeps several booths working as one.

## How It Makes Money

Guests pay per session, at a counter or at the booth. Packages, extra prints and themed booths priced above the plain one protect the ticket size once other studios open nearby.

## The Setup

### 1. Group the booths

Create a **Multi-Booth** group under **Booths > + New Booth**, set **How many photo booths?**, and set it up once: the group's **Templates**, **UI Project** and **Settings** (pricing) tabs apply to every booth when you click **Save for all booths**. Each booth is still a normal booth, so a themed booth can have its own templates on top. See [Multi-Booth Setup](../guides/multi-booth-setup.md).

### 2. One computer and one licence per booth

Each booth is paired to its own computer, and each computer needs its own Pix Pro. Add devices under **Licenses > Add more devices**; volume pricing applies as you add more. See [Add a Second Computer or Move Pix Pro](../tutorials/add-or-move-a-computer.md).

### 3. One queue, one cashier

- **Queue**: set up a shared queue for the group and show it on a screen at the venue with **Open TV view**.
- **Cashier**: choose **Pay at the booth**, where each kiosk shows its own Payment screen, or **Central cashier**, where guests pay the attendant and get an 8-character code that works at any booth in the group. See [Multi-Booth Setup](../guides/multi-booth-setup.md#step-3-choose-how-guests-pay).

### 4. Studio sessions

Studios often sell time rather than a fixed number of shots: guests get a timed session, take as many photos as they like and pick their favourites for the print. See [Run a Studio Mode Session](../tutorials/studio-mode.md).

### 5. Share printers and manage the team

- Two booths can share one printer through the computer's printer sharing. See [Sharing One Printer Between Booths](../guides/shared-printer.md).
- Give each attendant their own login under **Settings > Members** with a role that fits their job, instead of sharing yours. See [Settings](../dashboard/settings.md).

### 6. Compare booths

Filter **Transactions**, the **Dashboard** and **Health** by booth to see which theme earns most and which printer needs attention. With studios in several places, give each booth a **Region** and filter by it. See [Dashboard Home](../dashboard/dashboard-home.md).

## Typical Numbers

From the Pixture Photobooth Playbook (September 2026), for four booths in one unit at about US$4 a session: 18 sessions a day per booth brings roughly US$8,640 a month, 25 about US$12,000, and 40 about US$19,200, against around US$35,000 to build and US$4,200 a month in fixed costs. These are estimates; mall rent decides most of the difference.
