# Camera Settings

When using a Canon EOS camera, the Pix Desktop App gives you full remote control over exposure and focus settings. Access these from the **Camera Settings** page in the admin panel.

## Exposure Controls

### ISO

Controls sensor sensitivity. Lower values (100-400) produce cleaner images with less noise. Higher values (800-6400) help in dim lighting but introduce grain.

- For **well-lit booths** with studio strobes: ISO 100-400
- For **ambient-lit venues**: ISO 800-1600

### Aperture

Controls depth of field and light intake. Set via the app when your camera is in Manual or Av mode.

- **f/2.8 - f/4**: Shallow depth of field, great for single-subject portraits
- **f/5.6 - f/8**: Wider depth of field, better for groups

### Shutter Speed

Available in Manual mode. Keep it at 1/125 or faster to avoid motion blur, especially if guests are moving.

### White Balance

Match your lighting conditions to avoid color casts:

- **Auto**: Works for most mixed-lighting venues
- **Flash**: Best when using studio strobes
- **Tungsten/Fluorescent**: Use when venue lighting is the primary source

## Focus Aids

### Focus Peaking

Highlights in-focus edges on the live preview with a colored overlay. Useful when you have locked the lens to manual focus and want to verify sharpness.

### Exposure Preview

Shows a real-time exposure simulation on the live view so you can judge brightness before taking the shot.

### Histogram

A live histogram is displayed alongside the preview, helping you spot blown highlights or crushed shadows before capture.

:::tip
For a fixed photobooth setup, dial in your settings once during setup, switch the lens to manual focus, and lock everything down. This eliminates autofocus delays and ensures consistent results throughout the day.
:::

:::caution
Camera settings are only available when a Canon EOS camera is connected. Webcam sources have limited or no manual controls.
:::

## Saving Settings

Camera settings are saved per booth. When you switch booths or log in on a different device, the saved settings for that booth are restored automatically.
