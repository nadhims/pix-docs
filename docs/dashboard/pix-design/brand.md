# Brand

**Pix Design > Brand** holds your business's look in one place: your logo, three colours, a button style and two fonts. The page reads "Used as the default for your events, microsites and UI projects." Set it once and every new design starts in your colours instead of Pixture's. By the end of this page you will have your brand set and know where it shows up.

![Brand page with the Logo on light and on dark, the Brand palette (Accent, Dark, Light), Gradient and Flat buttons, Heading and Body fonts, and a live preview of the booth start screen, booth buttons and guest page](/img/docs/pix-design-brand.webp)

## Pix Starter and Pix Pro

You can set your brand on any plan. It turns on once your account has Pix Pro or an Event Pass running. Until then the page reads "Turns on with Pix Pro or an Event Pass." with a **Get Pix Pro** button that opens the Computers page. See [Choose a plan](../../getting-started/choose-plan.md).

## Logo

1. Under **Logo**, click **Add your logo** and pick a PNG or SVG file, up to 2 MB. A transparent PNG looks cleaner than one with a solid background.
2. The logo shows twice: **On light** and **On dark**. A dark logo is hard to see on dark booth screens, so click **Make a white version** to get one made for you, or **Upload a light version** to use your own.
3. **Change logo** and **Remove logo** replace or drop it later.

## Brand Palette

Three colours, each with a colour picker and a hex box:

| Colour | Used for |
|---|---|
| **Accent** | Buttons and highlights |
| **Dark** | Backgrounds and dark text |
| **Light** | Text and icon discs |

The reset button next to a colour puts the Pixture colour back. The page warns you when a choice will be hard to read, for example an Accent that is very light.

## Buttons and Fonts

- **Buttons**: **Gradient** or **Flat**.
- **Fonts**: a **Heading** font (titles and the countdown) and a **Body** font (buttons and everything else). Pick a built-in font or **Upload a font** (.woff2, .woff, .ttf or .otf).

Every change saves on its own; the page shows "Saved".

## Preview

The preview beside the settings shows your brand on four screens: **Start screen**, **Capture**, **Share** and **Guest page** (the share page customers open on their phone).

## Where Your Brand Shows Up

- **Events**: the booth screens of your events.
- **UI projects**: the booth screens you design in the [UI Editor](./ui-editor.md).
- **Share page and album**: the page customers open from the QR code, and the event album. See [Microsite (Share Page)](./microsite.md).

Your brand is the default. For one client with its own look, change that event, UI project or microsite on its own page; the rest keep your brand.
