# Pixture Event, Pixture Kiosk and Overview

If your account has events, the dashboard has three views. Pick one with the switcher at the bottom of the sidebar.

![The view switcher open at the bottom of the sidebar: Pixture Event, Pixture Kiosk and Overview, each with a short description](/img/docs/dashboard-views-switcher.webp)

| View | Use it for | Where you land |
|---|---|---|
| **Pixture Event** | A booth that runs for a set of dates, like a wedding, a party or a brand activation | Event dashboard |
| **Pixture Kiosk** | A self-service photobooth in one place, like a mall photobox | Kiosk dashboard, the dashboard as it always was |
| **Overview** | Both side by side | Overview |

The switcher only shows on accounts that have events. If you do not see it, nothing is missing: your dashboard works as before.

## What changes in each view

The sidebar and the pages follow the view you are in.

| Page | Pixture Event | Pixture Kiosk | Overview |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dashboard | Event numbers | Kiosk numbers | Both, split in two cards |
| Events | Yes | No | No |
| Booths and Vouchers | No | Yes | No |
| Transactions | **Event sales** | Transactions | Everything |
| Gallery | **Event gallery**, filter by event | Gallery | Everything |
| Health | **Event health** | Health | Every booth |
| Marketing Studio, Computers, Pix Design, Settings | Yes | Yes | Yes |

Opening a page that belongs to one view takes you to that view. Events always opens in Pixture Event, and Booths and Vouchers always open in Pixture Kiosk. So a link someone sends you still lands on the right page.

## How the money is split

Every sale is counted once, in exactly one view. **Pixture Kiosk plus Pixture Event always equals Overview.**

A sale is Event money when it belongs to an event or comes from a booth set up for events:

- a photo session started for an event,
- anything sold on a booth that is an event booth,
- an extra print or AI portrait sold for one of those sessions.

Everything else is Kiosk money, including sales taken at the counter. Booth revenue on the Kiosk dashboard no longer includes event money. If you ran events on a Kiosk booth before, your Kiosk numbers will look lower than they used to, and the Event dashboard shows the difference.

## The Overview

The Overview shows your total revenue with a chart that stacks Pixture Kiosk under Pixture Event, then one card for each. Each card shows its total, its share of the whole and its top booths. Change the period or the booth at the top and everything follows.

![The Overview: total revenue with a chart stacking Pixture Kiosk and Pixture Event, then a card for each](/img/docs/dashboard-overview.webp)

## Choosing your view

The first time you open the dashboard, a window asks **How will you use Pixture?** with the three choices. Close it and you stay in Pixture Kiosk. If you told Lucy what you run when you signed up, you start in the matching view and are not asked again.

Your choice is remembered in this browser. Switch any time from the sidebar.

## The top bar

Accounts with the switcher also get a bar across the top of every page. It holds the page title, the page's own filters (dates, region, booth, search), a **Docs** link to this documentation, the updates bell and your account menu. The account menu has **Settings**, **Language** and **Log out**. On a phone, the button on the left opens the sidebar.
