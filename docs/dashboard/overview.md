# Dashboard Overview

The Pixture dashboard is a web app at pixture.io. Nothing to install: it works in any modern browser, on a laptop, a tablet or a phone. Your booths run the [Pixture Photobooth app](../desktop-app/overview.md); the dashboard is where you set them up, price them, watch them and get paid.

![Dashboard home of a Pix Pro account, with the sidebar on the left and the Revenue, Sessions and Session Completion cards](/img/docs/dashboard-home.webp)

## The Sidebar

The sidebar lists the pages in this order. Your role decides which ones you see.

:::note Pixture Event, Pixture Kiosk and Overview
If your account has events, a switcher at the bottom of the sidebar picks a **workspace**, and the sidebar follows it: Events shows only in Pixture Event, Booths and Vouchers only in Pixture Kiosk. See [Pixture Event, Pixture Kiosk and Overview](./views.md).
:::

| Page | What it is for |
|---|---|
| [Dashboard](./dashboard-home.md) | Revenue, photo sessions, prints, vouchers, top booths and health for a day, week or month |
| [Events](./events.md) | A booking pointed at your booths for a set of dates, with its own pricing, templates and a public album |
| [Booths](./booth-management.md) | One page per booth: templates, filters, screen design, pricing, activity log, device and settings |
| [Transactions](./transactions.md) | Every payment, searchable, filterable and exportable to Excel |
| [Vouchers](./vouchers.md) | Free, fixed-amount or percentage codes, one at a time or in batches |
| [Marketing Studio](./marketing-studio.md) | Consent photos, audience, Promo, Frame Lab, campaigns and performance. Pix Pro only |
| [Gallery](./gallery.md) | Browse, download and delete what your booths captured |
| [Health](./health.md) | Which booths are online, their camera, printer, paper and error records |
| [Computers](./billing.md) | The computers running Pix Pro, your subscription, Event Passes and **Add computer** |
| [Pix Design](./pix-design/overview.md) | Brand, Template Editor, UI Editor, Photo Filters, GIF/Video overlay, Microsite and Pix AI (beta) |
| Help | Opens a chat with Lucy, the dashboard assistant |
| [Settings](./settings.md) | Organization, preferences, members, payment gateway, profile, billing, security and data |

Marketing Studio is not in the sidebar on Pix Starter. It appears once your account is Pix Pro.

## Around the Sidebar

- **Updates bell.** In the top bar, next to your avatar. Announcements from Pixture, and the place to open the free trial again if you chose **Later**.
- **Account menu.** Your avatar initials at the top right of the top bar, next to the updates bell and a **Docs** link. Open it for your email and role, **Settings**, **Affiliate program**, **Language** and **Log out**. Beta accounts also see their Pix AI credits here.
- **Organization switcher.** Shown only when you belong to more than one organization. Everything in the dashboard is scoped to the organization you have selected.

![Account menu opened from the avatar at the top right: the email and role, Settings, Affiliate program, Language and Log out](/img/docs/account-menu.webp)

## The Setup Banner

A new account sees a banner on every page until setup is done: "Your photobooth isn't live yet. N of 3 done: install the app, pair it, run a test session." Its button opens the next step: **Install the app**, **Pair your photobooth** or **Run a test session**. Follow [Pair your first booth](../getting-started/pair-your-first-booth.md) to finish the steps.

## Help and Lucy

**Help** in the sidebar opens a chat panel with Lucy, the dashboard assistant. Ask how something works or where a setting lives, in plain words. When the answer is a place on the page, tap **Show me**: the page darkens and that spot lights up.

## Currency Switcher

If your booths trade in more than one currency, the money pages (Dashboard, Transactions, Vouchers and Marketing Studio > Performance) show a row of "Showing" pills, one per currency. Pick one to see its totals. Totals are never combined across currencies, and nothing is converted. Each booth's currency is set on its **Settings** tab; see [Booth Pricing](./booth-pricing.md).

## Roles and Permissions

Owners and admins see every page. For everyone else, the role decides which sidebar entries appear and what they can change. Roles are managed from **Settings > Members > Manage Roles**, where each role picks its permission groups and whether it can reach all booths or none. Invite a team member with **+ Invite** and give them a role at the same time. See [Settings](./settings.md).

## On a Phone

The dashboard works on a phone. Keep it open at an event to watch sessions arrive and check a booth's health without leaving the floor.

On an iPhone, the [Pixture Manager app](../iphone-app/overview.md) does the same with booth alerts, Face ID and a layout made for the phone.

:::tip Language
The dashboard is available in English and Bahasa Indonesia. Switch from the account menu, or under **Settings > Preferences**. The booth's customer-facing language is a separate setting on the same page.
:::
