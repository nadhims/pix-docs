# Settings

**Settings** is a single page with these sections.

## Organization

Your business name and details as they appear to customers, and the organization's default currency and timezone.

## Preferences

- **Language** -- English or Bahasa Indonesia for the dashboard.
- **Tour and onboarding** -- replay the product tour.

## Members

Invite teammates by email and give each a **role**. Roles control which sidebar sections a member sees and what they can change. The **Pending** tab shows invitations not yet accepted, and the **Activity** tab lists what members have changed across the dashboard.

## Payment Gateway

Connect your **DOKU** account so customers can pay for photo sessions by QRIS at the kiosk, and switch between sandbox and production. A booth or an event can override the organization gateway. See [Payment Gateway Setup](../guides/payment-gateway-setup.md).

## Profile

Your own name, email, and avatar.

## Security

- **Password** and **two-factor authentication** for your login.
- **Menu Access PIN** -- the PIN operators must enter to open the kiosk's operator menu (double-tap the top-right corner of the kiosk screen). Set one on every booth in a public place.
- **Sessions** -- sign out other browsers.

## Data

Import templates from a previous account or another tool.

:::tip Two admins minimum
Add a second owner or admin so the business is not locked out if one phone or password is lost.
:::
