# Marketing Studio

**Marketing Studio** groups the tools that bring guests back: permission to reuse their photos, a contact list, a buy-one-get-one bonus, guest-designed frames, and campaigns that switch all of it on for a season. It is Pix Pro only and does not appear in the sidebar on Pix Starter.

![Marketing Studio Content tab: Marketing consent switch, Consent text, live kiosk consent prompt preview](/img/docs/marketing-content.webp)

## Content

Ask guests whether you may feature their photos. Pick a booth at the top, then:

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Marketing consent** | "Ask guests if you may reuse their photos for marketing. Opt-ins appear in the gallery below. Off by default." |
| **Consent text** | Your own wording. "Blank = built-in default in the booth's language." |
| Live preview | The kiosk prompt as guests see it: "May we feature your photos?" with **Yes, I agree** and **No thanks** |
| **Apply to all booths** | Copies this booth's consent settings to every booth |
| **Save changes** | Saves the selected booth |

Photos from guests who agreed appear in the gallery below these settings, so you can find them for social media.

## Audience

The contacts captured at your booths, with **Contact**, **Visits** and **Last seen**, and **Remove** for anyone who asks to be forgotten. Contacts come from Bonus Session's contact capture.

![Marketing Studio Audience tab](/img/docs/marketing-audience.webp)

## Bonus Session

![Marketing Studio Bonus Session tab: Buy 1 get 1 free, Daily limit, Active days, Active hours, Contact capture](/img/docs/marketing-bonus.webp)

**Buy 1, get 1 free.** A guest who pays for a photo session gets a second one free, right after the first, with a popup on the kiosk and a countdown into the next session. It fires only after a genuinely paid session: a free, voucher or bonus session never earns another bonus.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Daily limit** | How many bonus sessions a booth may give per day. Blank or 0 means no limit. Resets at midnight in your timezone |
| **Active days** | The days of the week the bonus runs |
| **Active hours** | The hours it runs, for example only on a quiet weekday afternoon |
| **Contact capture** | **Collect** or **Don't ask**, and whether to ask for **Email**, **Phone** or **Email + phone** before the free session starts |

Bonus sessions show a Bonus label on [Transactions](./transactions.md).

## Frame Lab

![Marketing Studio Frame Lab tab: per-booth price, canvas, voucher expiry, guest upload page QR](/img/docs/marketing-frame-lab.webp)

Frame Lab lets a guest design their own frame on their phone, pay for it, and use it at the booth. The guest flow is Upload, Pay, Get Voucher, Use at Booth. Each booth has its own **Price**, **Canvas** size in pixels and **Voucher expiry**. Below them are the shared **Guest upload page** QR, the **Page banner** and **Studio assets** the page shows, and **Recent Frame Lab uploads**. Frame Lab sales show a Frame Lab label on Transactions. The per-booth page, with the enable switch and a live preview, is reached from the booth's property grid; see [Booths](./booth-management.md).

## Campaigns

![Marketing Studio Campaigns tab](/img/docs/marketing-campaigns.webp)

A campaign switches a set of promotions on across booths for a period, so a school holiday or a mall anniversary is one thing to start and one thing to stop.

![New campaign modal: Name, Copy settings from, Roll out to, Promos to include, Starts, Ends](/img/docs/marketing-new-campaign-modal.webp)

| Field | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Name** | What you call it |
| **Copy settings from** | A booth whose current Bonus Session, Frame Lab and consent settings the campaign reuses |
| **Roll out to** | The booths it applies to |
| **Promos to include** | Bonus Session, Frame Lab, Marketing Consent, Promo Banner / Microsite |
| **Starts**, **Ends** | The campaign's dates |

Click **Save campaign**. Each campaign row has **Activate now**, **Deactivate** and **Delete**.

## Performance

![Marketing Studio Performance tab: This month and All time metrics](/img/docs/marketing-performance.webp)

**This month** and **All time** figures for Voucher redemptions, Bonus sessions granted, Bonus sessions used, Consented photos, Frame Lab sales and Microsite visits. On multi-currency accounts the "Showing" pills pick the currency for the money figures.

:::tip Start with Bonus Session
Of everything here, Bonus Session is the fastest win: it costs one extra photo session and, with contact capture on, gives you a contact you can invite back.
:::
