# Marketing Studio

**Marketing Studio** groups the tools that bring customers back: permission to reuse their photos, a contact list, discounts and Pay 1 get 1 deals, customer-designed frames, and campaigns that switch all of it on for a season. It is Pix Pro only and does not appear in the sidebar on Pix Starter.

![Marketing Studio Consent photos tab: Marketing consent switch, Consent text, live booth consent prompt preview](/img/docs/marketing-content.webp)

## Consent Photos

The **Consent photos** tab holds the photos customers let you use for marketing.
Ask customers whether you may feature their photos. Pick a **Booth** at the top, then:

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Marketing consent** | "Ask guests if you may reuse their photos for marketing. Opt-ins appear in the gallery below. Off by default." |
| **Consent text** | Your own wording. "Blank = built-in default in the booth's language." |
| Live preview | The booth prompt as customers see it: "May we feature your photos?" with **Yes, I agree** and **No thanks** |
| **Apply to all booths** | Copies this booth's consent settings to every booth |
| **Save changes** | Saves the selected booth |

Photos from customers who agreed appear in the gallery below these settings, so you can find them for social media.

## Audience

The contacts captured at your booths, with **Contact**, **Visits** and **Last seen**, and **Remove** for anyone who asks to be forgotten. Contacts come from Pay 1 get 1: on a booth's **Pricing** tab, set **Ask for contact after Pay 1 get 1** to **Email**, **Phone** or **Email + phone** (or **Don't ask**), and customers who claim the free session appear here.

## Promo

**Promo** holds discounts that switch on by themselves, or with a code. Click **+ New promo**, then set:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Name** | What you call it, for example Happy Hour |
| **Promo type** | **% off**, **Amount off**, **Promo price** (your own Single, Double and Group prices; leave Double or Group empty to keep the booth price) or **Pay 1 get 1 (free extra session)** |
| **Method** | **Automatic** (it applies by itself) or **With a code** |
| **Codes** | For **With a code**: **One code** that everyone types, or **Many single-use** codes. Enter **How many** and an optional **Prefix**, then **Add codes** |
| **Days & hours** | The days and hours it runs, in the **Booth's local time** |
| **Dates** | Start and end dates. Leave them empty to start today with no end |
| **Booths** | **All booths**, a booth group or single booths |
| **Limit** | How many uses **per day** and **in total**. Empty means **No limit** |
| **Start screen** | **Show the promo to people walking past** |

Click **Save promo**. The list filters by **All**, **Live now**, **Scheduled** and **Ended**, and each row's menu has **Pause**, **Resume** and **Delete**.

**Pay 1 get 1** gives a customer a second session free, right after a paid one. It fires only after a genuinely paid session: a hardware-paid session does not count.

:::note Pix Pro only
Promos run on Pix Pro booths. If some of your booths are on Starter, the page says so with an **Upgrade** link.
:::

## Frame Lab

![Marketing Studio Frame Lab tab: per-booth price, canvas, voucher expiry, guest upload page QR](/img/docs/marketing-frame-lab.webp)

Frame Lab lets a customer design their own frame on their phone, pay for it, and use it at the booth. The customer either builds the frame in a small studio (layout, colours, stickers, caption) or uploads a finished PNG, pays by QR code through your gateway, and gets a single-use voucher code that loads their frame on the booth. Each booth has its own **Frame Lab** switch, **Price**, **Canvas (px)** and **Voucher expiry** (hours). Below them are the shared **Guest upload page** QR and link, the **Page banner**, the **Studio assets** (textures and graphics customers can use in the studio) and **Recent Frame Lab uploads** with each order's voucher code and status. Frame Lab sales show as **CT** rows on Transactions. The per-booth page with the same settings is reached from the **Frame Lab** cell of the booth's property grid; see [Booths](./booth-management.md). The whole journey, from switching it on to the customer redeeming the code at the booth, is in [Let Customers Design Their Own Frame](../tutorials/frame-lab.md).

## Campaigns

![Marketing Studio Campaigns tab](/img/docs/marketing-campaigns.webp)

A campaign switches a set of promotions on across booths for a period, so a school holiday or a mall anniversary is one thing to start and one thing to stop.

![New campaign modal: Name, Copy settings from, Roll out to, Promos to include, Starts, Ends](/img/docs/marketing-new-campaign-modal.webp)

| Field | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Name** | What you call it |
| **Copy settings from** | A booth whose current Frame Lab, consent and promo banner settings the campaign reuses |
| **Roll out to** | The booths it applies to |
| **Promos to include** | **Frame Lab**, **Marketing Consent**, **Promo Banner / Microsite** |
| **Starts (optional)**, **Ends (optional)** | The campaign's dates. It turns on and off on those dates by itself |

Click **Save campaign**. Each campaign row has **Activate now**, **Deactivate** and **Delete**.

## Performance

![Marketing Studio Performance tab: This month and All time metrics](/img/docs/marketing-performance.webp)

**This month** and **All time** figures for **Promo uses**, **Tickets used**, **Staff passes used**, **Pay 1 get 1 granted**, **Pay 1 get 1 used**, **Consented photos**, **Frame Lab sales** and **Microsite visits** (always all-time).

:::tip Start with Pay 1 get 1
Of everything here, a Pay 1 get 1 promo is the fastest win: it costs one extra photo session and, with contact capture on, gives you a contact you can invite back.
:::
