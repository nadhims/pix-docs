# Computers

Pix Pro is per computer. A computer running the Pixture Photobooth app is active while it holds Pix Pro from your subscription, an Event Pass, or the free trial. The **Computers** page shows the computers that are active right now, how many your subscription allows, your unused Event Passes, and the buttons to buy more.

![Devices page of a Pix Pro account: Devices 21 of 42, Subscription, Next renewal, Event Passes, Pix AI credits, and the Active devices table with Deactivate](/img/docs/licenses-devices.webp)

## The Cards

| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Computers** | Active computers out of the number your subscription allows, for example "21 of 42" with "21 available" or "All in use" |
| **Pix Pro** | The cycle and computer count, for example "Monthly · 42 computers", the renewal date ("Renews …"), and **Manage billing** on card subscriptions. When computers renew on different days it says "Renewal dates differ. Each computer's is in its Manage menu." |
| **Event Passes** | Unused Event Passes, for example "3 unused", with **Buy Event Passes**. A running Event Pass adds a line such as "1 running on Mall Kiosk PC 2 until Oct 28, 18:00" |

Beta accounts also see a **Pix AI credits** box under the cards: credits left, with **Manage credits**.

## The Computers Table

Search by computer or booth name at the top of the page. The table lists each computer that holds Pix Pro or an Event Pass, and each event computer.

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Computer** | The computer's name. Click the pencil to rename it |
| **Booth** | The booth it is paired to, "On its event list" for an event computer, or "Not paired to a booth" |
| **Last seen** | When it last checked in |
| **Version** | The Pixture Photobooth app version it runs |
| **Plan** | **Pix Pro**, **Event Pass**, **Trial**, "Pix Pro, payment failed", **Needs a pass**, **Blocked** or **Watermarked** |
| **Manage** | A menu with **Rename**, **Copy computer ID**, the renewal date ("Renews …"), **Manage billing**, **Deactivate**, and **Remove** for an event computer |

A computer does not need activating by hand. When your subscription has a place to spare, the next computer that checks in takes it. An Event Pass is used at the booth, on the computer that needs it: on the event start screen or from the operator menu.

## Deactivate

![Deactivate this computer modal: Keep Pix Pro or Deactivate](/img/docs/licenses-deactivate-modal.webp)

**Deactivate** (in the **Manage** menu) takes Pix Pro off a computer: "This computer stops running Pix Pro right away. It stays paired, and its place on the subscription is free for another computer." The next computer that checks in claims the place. An Event Pass on the computer is not lost: it keeps running with the same end time, and the next event computer that starts its booth continues it. A computer in Kiosk mode cannot pick it up. The modal offers **Keep Pix Pro** or **Deactivate**.

## Remove an Event Computer

**Remove** (in the **Manage** menu of an event computer) signs the computer out and takes it off your account, for one that is lost, sold or retired. Pix Pro on it goes back to your subscription, and a running Event Pass keeps running and goes to the next event computer that starts its booth. To run events on it again, pair it again with a code from an event page.

## Add a Computer

![Pix Pro checkout: How many devices, See volume pricing, Pix Pro (1 Month) $40 and Pix Pro (1 Year) $300 with Save 38%](/img/docs/licenses-add-devices.webp)

1. Click **Add computer** at the top of the page. If your subscription still has places free, a popup says "N computers available" and "Pair a computer to use one.", with the two ways to pair: **At a booth** and **At an event**. Click **Buy more** to go on, or **Got it** to pair instead.
2. In the **Add computer** popup, pick **Event Pass** (for an event) or **Pix Pro** (for regular use). Each has its own checkout.
3. **Pix Pro:** the checkout is titled **Get Pix Pro**. Pick **Monthly** or **Yearly** and set **How many computers?** with the stepper. Several at once earn a volume discount; open **Volume pricing** to see the price per computer at each quantity. If your plan already covers computers, the table ends with "After this purchase your plan covers N computers (X now + Y new)." **Event Pass:** the checkout is titled **Buy Event Pass**; set **How many computers?**. The price is flat; to run longer, extend from the event page. A checkout opened from an event brings you back to it.
4. Check the **Order summary** and **Total today** on the right, then click **Pay**.

| Plan card | Price | Note on the card |
|---|---|---|
| **Event Pass** | $5, Rp 85.000 in Indonesia | "One-time", "24 hours on one computer, starts when you use it" |
| **Monthly** | $39.99 per month, Rp 850.000 in Indonesia | "Per computer, cancel anytime" ("Per computer, prepaid month" in Indonesia) |
| **Yearly** | $299.99 per year (about $25 per month), Rp 6.500.000 in Indonesia | **Best value**, "Per computer, cancel anytime" ("Per computer, prepaid year" in Indonesia) |

![Volume pricing table: Pix Pro Monthly and Yearly per quantity](/img/docs/licenses-volume-pricing.webp)

Outside Indonesia you pay with "Card, Apple Pay or PayPal via Lemon Squeezy", and Monthly and Yearly renew until you cancel. In Indonesia the checkout shows rupiah and "QRIS, virtual account or e-wallet via DOKU"; a rupiah purchase is prepaid and simply ends when its period ends. The Indonesian checkout also has a **Promo code** field with **Apply**. A code works once per account: an applied code shows "Applied. One use per account.", and a code you have used before shows "You've already used this promo code." A limited-time sale may also show, for example "Limited-time sale: 20% off Pix Pro", with the list price struck through.

![Pix Pro checkout as seen from Indonesia: rupiah prices, prepaid notes, promo code](/img/docs/licenses-add-devices-idr.webp)

Adding computers to a card subscription you already have shows **Confirm this charge** before anything is billed.

:::tip Events
Whether an event's computers are covered shows on the event itself: its **Computers** card says "All N computers have an Event Pass or Pix Pro." or how many still need one. See [Events](./events.md).
:::

## Before Pix Pro Ends

- An Event Pass or the free trial warns on the booth's idle screen 60 and 15 minutes before it ends. When it has ended, a popup on the booth names what ended (for example "Your Event Pass has ended") and offers **Manage devices**, a QR code to this page.
- A subscription that will not renew (cancelled, or a prepaid rupiah purchase) warns by email and a dashboard banner 7 days and 1 day before it ends.
- If a subscription renewal payment fails, you get an email and a dashboard banner at once, the computer's Plan column reads "Pix Pro, payment failed" and the booth shows a payment-failed banner. The computer keeps Pix Pro for a 3-day grace, then is blocked. Fix the card under **Settings > Pixture billing**.

## Cancelling and Invoices

Stop a card subscription from **Settings > Pixture billing > Manage subscription**. Pix Pro stays on the computer until the end of the paid period. Rupiah purchases are prepaid and end on their own. Invoices and receipts are under **Settings > Pixture billing**; see [Settings](./settings.md).

## The Free Trial

Every organization can start one free 3-day Pix Pro trial on one computer, with no card. Start it with **Start free trial** on the **You have a 3-day free trial** card in the **Pair a computer** popup, or from the Updates bell, and pick your booth: the trial goes on the computer paired to it. It starts when the booth computer connects. While it runs your account is Pix Pro on that computer. After 3 days that computer goes back to watermarked unless you buy Pix Pro.

## Pix Starter

![Devices page of a Pix Starter account: No devices yet, Subscription, Event Passes, Buy license](/img/docs/licenses-starter.webp)

With no active computer the account is Pix Starter: the **Computers** card reads "No computers yet" and "Pix Starter runs one computer, watermarked." Every photo and print carries the Pixture watermark, and a second computer is refused at the pairing code. Booths, templates and screen designs are still yours to prepare. See [Plans](../pricing/plans.md).

## Pix AI Credits

Beta accounts have a **Pix AI credits** page: credits left, **Buy more**, the cost per image (1 credit), **How you pay** (a monthly plan or pay as you go) and credit packs of 100, 400 or 1000. Credits are separate from Pix Pro and do not change your computer count.

![Pix AI credits page: Credits left, Buy more, cost per image, How you pay, credit packs](/img/docs/licenses-ai-credits.webp)
