# Track Profit and Loss

In this tutorial you will enter what your business spends, the price of your paper, your tax and the booking fees your event clients pay. Then you will read a month's profit and loss, open one kiosk on its own, and download the statement as a PDF. Allow about 20 minutes the first time; after that, a few minutes a month for new one-time costs.

Sales come in by themselves from your booths. Finance only needs the money that does not pass through a booth.

## When to Use It

- **A self-service kiosk in a mall.** The rent, the staff and the paper are the same every month whether customers come or not. Finance shows whether each kiosk covers its own costs, and by how much.
- **An event rental.** A client pays you a booking fee, guests may pay at the booth too, and each event costs crew and transport. Enter the fee and the costs on the event and you see each event's net profit.
- **A studio with several booths.** **Performance by unit** ranks every kiosk and event by revenue, with its net profit and margin, so the weak booth stands out.
- **Monthly books.** Download the month as a PDF for your own records or your accountant.

Finance is a management report, not an accounting system: no invoices, no bank matching, and the PDF says "Management report, unaudited". To split money with a venue, an investor or staff, see [Share Revenue and Profit with Partners](./share-revenue-and-profit.md).

## Before You Start

- Any plan. Profit & Loss, costs, paper, tax and booking fees work on Pix Starter and Pix Pro. The PDF needs paid Pix Pro, monthly or yearly; an Event Pass or the free trial does not unlock it.
- Your role needs **View finance** to open Finance, and **Manage finance** to add or change anything. Owners and admins have both. See [Add Your Team and Set Roles](./team-and-roles.md).
- Last month's bills at hand: rent, salaries, internet, equipment you bought, and the paper invoice.

## Prepare

Sort your costs before you type them. Each one goes into one of three **Cost type**s:

| Cost type | Use it for | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **One-time** | Something paid once, counted in its month | A printer repair, a set of props, crew for one event |
| **Monthly** | Something that repeats every month until you stop it | Venue rent, salaries, internet, your Pix Pro subscription |
| **Depreciation** | Equipment you will use for years, spread evenly over its life | A Rp 18.000.000 camera over 36 months counts Rp 500.000 a month |

Then decide where each cost belongs. A cost that belongs to one kiosk (that kiosk's rent or internet) goes on that kiosk, so the kiosk's own numbers are honest. A cost the whole business shares (your salary, accounting) goes on the **Whole business**.

Work out your paper price **per sheet**: the price of a box or roll divided by the prints it makes. A box that costs Rp 1.600.000 and makes 800 prints is Rp 2.000 per sheet. Use the price including the ink ribbon if it comes with the paper.

:::tip Depreciation or one-time?
Buying a Rp 18.000.000 camera as a one-time cost makes that one month look like a big loss and every later month look better than it is. Depreciation spreads it over the months you use it, which is what a lender or a partner expects to see.
:::

## Step 1: Open Finance and pick the month

1. Click **Finance** in the sidebar. It opens on last month, since that is the month you close.
2. Pick the month at the top, or **All months** for the year so far.
3. Pick the year.
4. Leave **View** on **All kiosks and events** for the whole business.

![Finance with the Finance menu item, the month and year pickers and the View picker marked, above Total revenue, Total costs, Net profit and Owner's net](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-profit-and-loss--open-finance.webp)

## Step 2: Add your costs

1. Open the **Costs** tab.
2. Click **Add cost**.

![Costs tab with the Costs tab and the Add cost button marked, above the Fixed costs table](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-profit-and-loss--costs-tab.webp)

In the **Add cost** dialog:

1. Type a **Description**, for example "Mall Kota rent".
2. Type the **Amount**.
3. Choose the **Cost type**. **Monthly** asks for its first month and, optionally, a **Last month**. **Depreciation** asks for the **Useful life (months)** and shows the **Monthly charge**.
4. Under **Allocate to**, keep **Whole business** or choose one kiosk or one event.
5. Click **Save cost**.

![Add cost dialog with Description, Amount, Cost type, Allocate to and Save cost marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-profit-and-loss--add-cost.webp)

Repeat for each cost. For your own Pixture bill, click the **Pix Pro subscription** shortcut at the top of the dialog: it fills in the name and makes the cost monthly, and you type the amount you pay. The shortcut hides once a cost with that name exists.

## Step 3: Set the paper price and your tax

1. Under **Paper**, click **Add paper**. Type a **Paper name** (for example "DNP 4R") and the **Cost per sheet**, then click **Apply**. Your first paper becomes the **Default**, used by every kiosk with no other paper.
2. A kiosk on a different paper: add that paper, switch **Default paper** off and tick its kiosks. The **Default** tag shows which paper the rest use.
3. Under **Tax**, click **Add tax**. Type a **Name**, the **Rate (%)**, choose **Charged on**: **Revenue** or **Profit before tax**, and click **Save tax**.

![Paper and Tax on the Costs tab with Add paper, the Default tag and Add tax marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-profit-and-loss--paper-and-tax.webp)

You never type how many sheets you used. **Paper and consumables** is counted every month from the prints each kiosk made, times that kiosk's paper price. Free and test sessions count too, because they use paper.

Indonesia example: the 0.5% final income tax for small businesses is a tax on **Revenue**. Taxes are taken before profit is shared, so partners' profit shares come from profit after tax.

## Step 4: Add an event's booking fee and costs

What a client pays you for an event is its booking fee. You enter it on the event, not on the Costs tab.

1. Open the event in **Events**, then the **Report** tab. The **Money** card comes first. Click **Add booking fee** (or **Edit** to change it), type the **Amount** and click **Save**. The fee counts in the month of the event date.
2. Click **Add cost** on the card for the event's own one-time costs: crew, transport, a venue fee.
3. **Edit costs in Finance** opens the Costs tab for everything else.

![The Money card of an event with the booking fee Edit link, Add cost and Edit costs in Finance marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-profit-and-loss--event-money.webp)

The card shows the fee, **Guest sales** (what guests paid at the booth), the event's costs and its **Net profit** over the whole event.

## Step 5: Read the month

The **Profit & Loss** tab reads top to bottom like any profit and loss statement: **Revenue** (**Kiosk sales**, **Event sales** and **Booking fees**), **Variable costs** (costs that move with sales, such as paper and venue revenue shares), **Contribution margin**, **Fixed costs**, **Profit before tax**, **Tax** and **Net profit**.

<!-- SHOT-NEEDED: dashboard: the Profit and loss statement card of the whole business with the Kiosk sales, Event sales and Booking fees lines (the existing finance-profit-and-loss.webp shows an older "Event bookings" line) -->

On the right:

1. Each partner's share of the net profit (empty until you add partners).
2. **Retained by owner**: what is left for you.
3. A capital repayment, when an investor is being paid back.
4. **Owner's net**: what you keep after the repayment.
5. **Performance by unit**: every kiosk and event, highest revenue first. Click a row to open it on its own.

![Profit distribution and Performance by unit with a partner's share, Retained by owner, Capital repayment, Owner's net and a kiosk row marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-profit-and-loss--distribution.webp)

## Step 6: Look at one kiosk or event

1. Open **View** and choose a kiosk (**Choose a kiosk...** searches when you have many), or click its row in **Performance by unit**.
2. The last card turns into **Contribution margin**: the kiosk's sales minus its own variable costs.
3. Whole-business costs and partners are left out, and the page says so.

![Finance for one kiosk with the View picker, the Contribution margin card and the note about whole-business partners and costs marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-profit-and-loss--one-kiosk.webp)

A kiosk with a healthy contribution margin pays its way, even if its net profit looks small after a big shared cost. A kiosk with a thin contribution margin loses money on every month you keep it open.

## Step 7: Download the PDF

1. Set **View** back to **All kiosks and events**. The button is hidden for one kiosk or event.
2. On the **Profit & Loss** tab, click **Export PDF**. The **Profit and Loss Statement** downloads, numbered by period: October 2026 is PL-2026-10, and **All months** gives PL-2026.

<!-- SHOT-NEEDED: dashboard: the Profit & Loss tab row with the Export PDF button marked -->

![Profit and Loss Statement PDF for October 2026, number PL-2026-10: revenue, costs, net profit, profit distribution and performance by unit](/img/docs/finance-pl-pdf.webp)

The PDF carries your logo from **Pix Design > Brand** and comes in your dashboard language, English or Bahasa Indonesia. Without paid Pix Pro the button carries a **Pix Pro** tag and opens the **Computers** page.

## Check It Worked

- **Total costs** on Profit & Loss matches the bills you entered, plus paper and any tax.
- **Paper and consumables** shows on the Costs tab, marked **automatic**, with the sheets printed.
- An event with a booking fee shows it under **Booking fees** in the month of the event date.
- The PDF opens with the right month in its number.

## Tips

- **Close the month in the first week.** Add last month's one-time costs, check the paper price, then download the PDF. Finance opens on last month for this.
- **Stop a monthly cost, do not delete it.** When a venue contract ends, edit the rent and set its **Last month**. Removing it changes every earlier month too.
- **Revenue differs from the Dashboard by the booking fees.** Kiosk and event sales follow the same rules as the Dashboard; Finance adds the booking fees of events dated in the period.
- **One currency at a time.** If your booths sell in more than one currency, the **Showing** pills at the top pick the currency; nothing is converted.
- **No months ahead.** The month picker stops at the current month. Plan next month's costs when it comes.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Why | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Profit & Loss says "No sales in this period" | No sales and no costs in that month yet | Pick last month, or check on the Dashboard that the booth uploaded its sessions |
| **Paper and consumables** is 0 | No paper price yet | Add a paper on the Costs tab (Step 3) |
| A kiosk's paper cost looks wrong | It uses the **Default** paper, not its own | Add its paper and tick that kiosk, or edit the default price |
| An event's booking fee is missing from this month | The fee counts in the month of the event date, and the card says "Counts in" that month | Pick that month, or fix the event date |
| The Money card says "Add the event date to count this fee" | The event has no date, so the fee counts in no month | Set the event's dates |
| **Export PDF** is missing | **View** shows one kiosk or event | Choose **All kiosks and events** |
| **Export PDF** has a **Pix Pro** tag | The account has no paid Pix Pro (an Event Pass or trial does not count) | Add a computer on the **Computers** page |
| You can see Finance but every button is missing | Your role has **View finance** only | Ask the owner for **Manage finance** |
