# Share Revenue and Profit with Partners

In this tutorial you will add three partners: a venue that takes a share of one kiosk's revenue, an investor who takes a share of profit and gets their money back in instalments, and a staff member on a share of profit. At the end you will read what each one gets for a month and download a statement for each of them. Allow about 15 minutes, once your costs are in.

## When to Use It

- **A self-service kiosk in a mall or a cafe.** The venue gives you the spot for 20% of what the kiosk takes, instead of rent. A **Revenue share** on that one kiosk works it out every month.
- **An investor who paid for the booths.** They get 40% of the profit, plus their capital back over 24 months. A **Profit share** and a **Capital repayment** on one partner do both.
- **Staff or a co-founder on a percentage.** 5% of profit, with a **Minimum guarantee** so a slow month still pays something.
- **An event rental with a partner who brings the clients.** A share on the **Whole business**, so the booking fees and event sales count too.

Use a cost instead of a partner when the amount does not depend on sales or profit, such as a flat rent: add it as a **Monthly** cost (see [Track Profit and Loss](./profit-and-loss.md)) or give the partner a **Fixed monthly amount**.

## Before You Start

- Partners need paid Pix Pro, monthly or yearly. An Event Pass or the free trial does not unlock them; the **Partners** tab then reads "Partners are part of Pix Pro".
- Your role needs **Manage finance**. Owners and admins have it.
- Your costs, paper and tax entered first, so the profit that is shared is the real one. See [Track Profit and Loss](./profit-and-loss.md).
- The deal terms at hand: the percentages, which kiosks they cover, the capital, and how many months it is repaid over.

## Prepare

Know the order in which the money is split every month, so you can explain it to your partners:

1. Revenue: kiosk sales, event sales and booking fees.
2. Revenue shares come off first, as a cost.
3. Your other costs and the paper.
4. Tax.
5. What is left is net profit. Profit shares are taken from it.
6. You keep the rest.

Pick the right term for each promise:

| Term | The partner gets | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| **Revenue share** | A percentage of revenue, whatever the profit | A venue: easy to check against sales, paid even in a loss month |
| **Profit share** | A percentage of net profit; nothing in a month with a loss | An investor or a partner who shares the risk |
| **Fixed monthly amount** | The same amount every month | Rent or a retainer |
| **Capital repayment** | Their money back in equal monthly instalments | An investor who paid for equipment |
| **Minimum guarantee** | At least this much a month from their percentage terms | Staff who need a floor |

Write the deal down with the partner before you enter it: the percentage, the scope (one kiosk, several, or the whole business) and from which month it applies.

## Step 1: Add the venue

1. Click **Finance** in the sidebar, open the **Partners** tab and click **Add partner** at the right of the tab row.
2. Type the venue's **Name** and choose the **Role** **Venue**. The **Email** is for your own reference.
3. Under **Add a term**, click **+ Revenue share** and type the **Rate (%)**, for example 20.
4. Set **Scope** to **Selected kiosks** and tick the venue's kiosk.
5. Leave **Changes apply from** on this month, or choose an earlier month or **The start** if the deal already ran.
6. Click **Save partner**.

The venue's share now appears under **Variable costs** on the Profit & Loss, marked **automatic**. On Rp 10.750.000 of kiosk sales, 20% is Rp 2.150.000.

## Step 2: Add the investor

1. Click **Add partner**, type the investor's name and choose the **Role** **Investor**.
2. Click **+ Profit share**.
3. Type the **Rate (%)**, for example 40, and keep **Scope** on **Whole business**. The **Basis** reads **Net profit**.
4. Click **+ Capital repayment** and type the **Capital** (Rp 60.000.000).
5. Type the **Months** (24).
6. Choose the **First month**. The **Instalment** shows below: Rp 2.500.000 per month.
7. Leave **Paid from** on **Owner's share** if the instalments come out of what you keep. Choose **Kiosk profit, before split** if they come off the profit before anyone's share.
8. Click **Save partner**.

![Edit partner dialog with Role, + Profit share, Rate, Capital, Months, First month, Paid from and Save partner marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-share-revenue-and-profit--investor-dialog.webp)

The instalment is the capital divided by the months; the last one takes any rounding. It is due every month of the schedule, also in a month with a loss.

## Step 3: Add staff

1. Click **Add partner**, type the name and choose the **Role** **Staff**.
2. Click **+ Profit share**, type 5 and keep **Whole business**.
3. To promise a floor, click **+ Minimum guarantee** and type the **Minimum per month**. In a slow month the page tops the share up to it.
4. Click **Save partner**.

## Step 4: Read the month

1. Pick the month at the top of Finance and open the **Partners** tab.
2. The venue's row shows its revenue share for the month.
3. The investor's row adds their profit share and the instalment.
4. The **Owner** row shows what you keep: the remainder of the profit, less the capital repayment.

![Partners tab for October 2026 with the Partners tab and the amounts for the venue, the investor and the owner marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-share-revenue-and-profit--partners-month.webp)

On **Profit & Loss**, **Profit distribution** shows the same split with **Retained by owner**, the capital repayment and **Owner's net**. See [Track Profit and Loss](./profit-and-loss.md#step-5-read-the-month).

## Step 5: Download their statement

1. Keep the month picked at the top, or choose **All months** for the year so far.
2. On the **Partners** tab, click **Statement** on the partner's row. A PDF downloads.
3. Send it to the partner yourself, by email or chat.

![Revenue share statement PDF for a venue, number RS-2026-10-001: amount due, the 20% revenue share and gross sales by week](/img/docs/finance-partner-statement-pdf.webp)

The statement shows only that partner's own figures: what they are due and how it is worked out. It never shows another partner's name or your share. A revenue share adds gross sales by week, a profit share the net profit it comes from, and a capital repayment the capital account. Its number starts with **RS** (revenue share), **PS** (profit share), **IS** (investor) or **ST**.

## Check It Worked

- The **Partners** tab lists each partner with their terms as chips, and an amount for the month.
- The **Owner** row is the remainder, less any capital repayment from your share.
- On **Profit & Loss**, the venue's share is under **Variable costs**, and **Profit distribution** lists the investor and staff.
- The statement PDF opens with the partner's name and only their figures.

## Tips

- **When a deal changes, do not make a new partner.** Edit the partner, change the term, and pick **Changes apply from**. Months before it keep their old numbers. Choose **The start** only to fix a typo or a deal that always applied.
- **Ending a deal:** **Remove** ends a partner from this month and keeps earlier months as they were. A partner added this month by mistake is deleted completely.
- **Venues share only their own kiosk.** A share set on a kiosk sees only that kiosk's own sales. Sessions at an event count under the event, so to share event income, put the term on the **Whole business**.
- **Shares cannot pass 100%.** Revenue shares on one kiosk, or on the whole business, cannot add up to more than 100%, and the same goes for profit shares.
- **Check before you pay.** The dialog shows **Estimated,** for the month as you type, so you can compare it with the amount in the contract.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Why | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| The tab reads "Partners are part of Pix Pro" | The account has no paid monthly or yearly Pix Pro | Add a computer on the **Computers** page; an Event Pass or the trial does not unlock partners |
| "Revenue shares on this scope would total more than 100%" | The shares on that kiosk or on the whole business add up to more than 100% | Lower a rate, or check the scope of each term |
| "Choose this month or an earlier one" | **Changes apply from** cannot be a future month | Make the change when that month comes |
| A venue gets nothing from an event held at its kiosk | Event sessions count under the event, not the kiosk | Put the venue's share on the **Whole business**, or agree a fixed amount for events |
| The investor gets nothing this month | A profit share is 0 in a month with a loss | Expected; the **Capital repayment** is still due. Add a **Minimum guarantee** if the deal promises a floor |
| **Statement** is missing on a row | The partner has no term this month and nothing due | Pick a month in which the deal ran |
