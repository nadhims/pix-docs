# Share Revenue and Profit with Partners

In this tutorial you will record your monthly costs and paper price, then add three partners: a venue that takes a share of one kiosk's revenue, an investor who takes a share of profit and gets their money back in instalments, and a staff member on a share of profit. At the end you will read what each one gets for a month. Allow about 15 minutes.

## Before You Start

- Partners need paid Pix Pro, monthly or yearly. Costs, paper and tax work on every plan.
- Your role needs **Manage finance**. Owners and admins have it.
- Have the deal terms at hand: the percentages, the capital, and how many months it is repaid over.

## How the Money Is Split

Every month, in this order:

1. Revenue: kiosk sales, event bookings and paid invoices.
2. Revenue shares come off first, as a cost.
3. Your other costs and the paper.
4. Tax.
5. What is left is net profit. Profit shares are taken from it.
6. You keep the rest.

## Step 1: Record your costs

1. Click **Finance** in the sidebar and open the **Costs** tab.
2. Click **Add cost**. Enter "Salaries", the amount, choose **Monthly**, and leave **Allocate to** on **Whole business**. Click **Save cost**.
3. Add the other regular costs the same way: internet, the Pix Pro subscription (the dialog has a shortcut for it), rent you pay yourself. Put a cost on a kiosk when it belongs to that kiosk only.
4. For equipment, choose **Depreciation** and enter the useful life in months. A Rp 18.000.000 camera over 36 months counts Rp 500.000 a month.
5. Under **Paper**, click **Add paper**, enter its name and the cost per sheet, and save. The first paper becomes the **Default** for every kiosk.
6. If you pay a tax on revenue or on profit, click **Add tax** under **Tax**.

## Step 2: Add the venue

1. Open the **Partners** tab and click **Add partner**.
2. Enter the venue's name and choose the role **Venue**.
3. Under **Add a term**, click **+ Revenue share**. Enter the rate, for example 20.
4. Set **Scope** to the venue's kiosk: choose **Selected kiosks** and tick the kiosk.
5. Leave **Changes apply from** on this month, or choose an earlier month or **The start** if the deal already ran.
6. Click **Save partner**.

The venue's share now appears under **Variable costs** on the Profit & Loss, marked automatic. On Rp 10.750.000 of kiosk sales, 20% is Rp 2.150.000.

## Step 3: Add the investor

1. Click **Add partner**, enter the investor's name and choose **Investor**.
2. Click **+ Profit share** and enter the rate, for example 40, on the **Whole business**.
3. Click **+ Capital repayment**. Enter the capital (Rp 60.000.000), the months (24) and the first month. The instalment shows below: Rp 2.500.000 per month.
4. Leave **Paid from** on **Owner's share** if the instalments come out of what you keep. Choose **Kiosk profit, before split** if they come off the profit before anyone's share.
5. Click **Save partner**.

![Edit partner: a 40% profit share and a capital repayment of Rp 60.000.000 over 24 months, paid from the owner's share](/img/docs/finance-partner-dialog.webp)

## Step 4: Add staff

1. Click **Add partner**, enter the name and choose **Staff**.
2. Click **+ Profit share**, enter 5, and keep **Whole business**.
3. To promise a floor, click **+ Minimum guarantee** and enter the monthly minimum. In a slow month the page tops the share up to it.
4. Click **Save partner**.

## Step 5: Read the month

1. Pick the month at the top of the page.
2. The **Partners** tab shows each partner's terms and what they get that month, with the owner's part on the last row.
3. On **Profit & Loss**, **Profit distribution** shows each share, **Retained by owner**, the capital repayment and **Owner's net**.

![Partners for October 2026: the venue gets Rp 2.150.000, the investor Rp 9.571.850 (profit share plus instalment), staff Rp 883.981, and the owner keeps Rp 7.223.794 after the repayment](/img/docs/finance-partners.webp)

## When a Deal Changes

Edit the partner, change the term, and pick **Changes apply from**. Months before it keep their old numbers. **Remove** ends a partner from this month and keeps earlier months as they were.
