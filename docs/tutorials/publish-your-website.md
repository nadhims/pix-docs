# Publish Your Website

In this tutorial you will build your business page at **`handle.pixture.io`**: your logo and bio, buttons for your shop, Frame Lab and photo lookup, your branches with opening hours, and a look that matches your brand. Then you publish it and share the link. Allow about 30 minutes; most of it is choosing pictures and words.

## When to Use It

- **A self-service booth in a mall or café.** Customers scan a QR at the entrance, buy a session on their phone, and walk up to the booth with a code. Your page also shows the opening hours and the way to each branch.
- **A multi-booth studio.** One link lists every branch, the price list, and, with the [queue line](./queue-line.md) on, how many people are waiting at each booth.
- **An event rental business.** Put the link in your Instagram bio: your upcoming events, album cards from past events, and WhatsApp in one tap for new bookings.
- **Bringing photo guests back.** Every guest photo page links to your Website, so someone who came for their photos lands on your shop and your booths.

Not needed if you only want customers to buy a session online: the booth's own shop link and QR work without a Website. See [Sell Sessions Online](./sell-sessions-online.md).

## Before You Start

- **Pix Pro paid Monthly or Yearly** to publish. A free trial or an Event Pass does not count: you can build the page, but the button reads **Pix Pro to publish**.
- **Your Brand set up** (logo, colours, fonts) in **Pix Design > Brand**, if you want the page to match it. See [Set Up Your Brand](./set-up-your-brand.md).
- **Booth prices and a payment gateway** if you want to sell sessions: the shop takes its prices from each booth's **Pricing** tab and pays into your gateway.
- **A phone next to you** to check the page as customers see it.

## Prepare

- **Pick your address.** The handle is 3 to 30 letters, numbers and dashes, for example `sunny-side-booths`. Short and the same as your Instagram name is easiest to remember. Some words are kept back for Pixture, such as `shop`, `support` and `docs`.
- **Your profile picture:** your logo by default. A square logo on a plain background reads best in the small circle.
- **A home poster (optional):** one strong photo or a short video of your booth with happy customers, plus a one-line headline and a button text.
- **A picture of each booth** and its exact place (mall, floor, near which shop), the opening hours, a Google Maps link and a WhatsApp number per branch.
- **Your links:** Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp, email. Every link is checked for scams when you add it; a link that fails ("This link can't be added") cannot go on the page.

## Step 1: Open the Website Editor

1. Open **Pix Design** and click the **Website** card ("Your own site: home, links, shop and photos.").
2. The editor has the sections on the left (**Home**, **Links**, **Header**, **Booths**, **Frame Lab**, **Theme**), the settings in the middle and a phone preview on the right that updates as you type.
3. Changes save by themselves. The status next to the title reads **Saving**, **Saved**, **Draft** (not published yet) or **Live**.

## Step 2: Fill In Your Header

1. Click **Header**.
2. Pick a layout: **Classic**, **Hero** or **Banner**.
3. Check the profile picture (your logo by default), the **Title** (your business name) and write a short **Bio**: what you do and where.
4. Click **Add a social icon** for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, X, WhatsApp or Email.

<!-- SHOT-NEEDED: dashboard: the Header section with layout choices, Title, Bio and Add a social icon -->

## Step 3: Add Your Buttons

1. Click **Links**, then **Add**.
2. Add the buttons that come from your booths:
   - **Buy a photo session** opens your shop.
   - **Design your own frame** opens Frame Lab. See [Let Customers Design Their Own Frame](./frame-lab.md).
   - **Get my photos** lets a customer type their booth code and get their photos.
   - **Branches** lists every branch with directions, and **Price list** shows the prices from your booths.
   - **Event album** shows one event's album as a cover card, and **Upcoming events** shows where your booth goes next.
3. Add your own: **WhatsApp**, **Call**, **Email**, **YouTube**, an **Image gallery**, a **Heading**, a **Text** block, or any **Link**. Paste a link into the search box and Pixture picks the right kind of button. **Add collection** groups several links under one button.
4. Drag a button to change the order. Its three dots have **Edit**, **Share**, **Hide** and **Archive**.

Put **Buy a photo session** first: it is the button that earns money.

## Step 4: Add Your Booths' Details

1. Click **Booths**. It lists the booths you sell from.
2. Add a picture and a location to each booth.
3. Click **Details** to add the **Opening hours**, a **Google Maps link** and a **WhatsApp** number for that branch.

Prices are not set here: they come from each booth's **Pricing** tab, so there is nothing to keep in step. Booths in a Multi-Booth group appear together.

## Step 5: Choose the Look and the Home Poster

1. Click **Theme** and pick **Your Brand** (your logo and colours from Brand) or a ready-made theme: **Cream**, **Night**, **Coral**, **Mint** or **Mono**. Then change the wallpaper, buttons, colours and font if you like.
2. Click **Home** and switch **Show home** on for a full-screen poster customers see first. Add an **Image** or **Video**, a **Logo**, a **Headline**, a **Subline** and the **Button** text.
3. Click **Frame Lab** to set the look of the Frame Lab start screen: a picture (**Poster**, **Logo**, **Yours** or **None**), a layout (**Side**, **On top** or **Full photo**), a **Headline**, the line under it and the **Button** text.

Watch the phone preview as you go. Dark text on a busy photo is hard to read on a phone in sunlight; pick a calm photo or the **Night** theme.

## Step 6: Pick Your Address and Publish

1. In the address box at the top, type your handle. Pixture suggests one from your business name. The box says **Available**, or why not: **Too short**, **Letters, numbers and dashes only**, **Not available** or **Taken**.
2. Click **Publish**. Without a paid Pix Pro, the button reads **Pix Pro to publish**.

![The Website editor with the address box ending in .pixture.io and the publish button marked](/img/docs/steps/dashboard-pix-design-website--pix-design-website.webp)

The status changes to **Live** and your page is at `handle.pixture.io`. The handle is yours from the first publish.

## Step 7: Share the Link

1. Click **Copy link** and put the address in your Instagram and TikTok bio, your WhatsApp Business profile and your Google Business profile.
2. Make a QR code of the address and print it on a sign at the booth, at the entrance and on your receipts.
3. To share one button only (for example straight to the shop), open its three dots and pick **Share**.

## Check It Worked

- Open `handle.pixture.io` on your phone, outside your Wi-Fi. The home poster (if on), your profile, the buttons and the booth picker appear.
- Tap **Buy a photo session**, pick a booth and a package: the prices match the booth's **Pricing** tab.
- The page opens in English or Indonesian, following the phone's language.

## Tips

- **Change your address carefully.** **Change address**, then **Save address**, moves the page, and the old link stops working at once: reprint your QR codes and update your bios.
- **Taking it down:** **Unpublish** takes the page offline and keeps the handle yours for 30 days.
- **If your Pix Pro ends,** the page stays online for 6 months but you cannot edit it; a banner says "Your page is still online. Renew Pix Pro to edit it." After 6 months it goes offline and the handle is released.
- **Event guests are safe.** An event album shows on the page only as a cover card with a link; guest photos never appear on the page itself.
- **Keep links healthy.** Saved links are checked again every day; one that turns bad shows **Link blocked** in the editor and disappears from the page.
- **Visitors can report a page** with the **Report** link at the bottom, so keep the page about your photobooth business.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Why | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| The button says **Pix Pro to publish** | The account has no paid Pix Pro (a trial or an Event Pass does not count) | Get Pix Pro, Monthly or Yearly, on the **Computers** page |
| The address box says **Taken** or **Not available** | Another account uses it, or Pixture keeps the word back | Try another handle, for example with your city |
| "This link can't be added" | The link failed the scam and safety check | Use your own profile link; check it opens in a browser |
| A button shows **Link blocked** | The site behind it became unsafe in the daily check | Remove it or replace the link |
| The shop shows a price you did not expect | The shop takes its prices from each booth's **Pricing** tab | Change the price on that booth's **Pricing** tab; nothing to change on the Website |
| "Your page is still online. Renew Pix Pro to edit it." | Your Pix Pro ended | Click **Renew Pix Pro** within 6 months to edit again |
| The old link stopped working | The address was changed | Share the new address and reprint the QR codes |
