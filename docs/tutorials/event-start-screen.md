# Design an Event Start Screen

In this tutorial you will design the start screen of an event: the screen guests see when they walk up to the booth. What you set here also styles every other guest screen of the event, from the camera to Share. You will also set the countdown, the session timers and which steps guests go through. Allow about 15 minutes.

## When to Use It

- **Wedding or private party:** the couple's names, the date and a photo of them as the background, in the colours of their invitation.
- **Corporate event or brand activation:** the client's logo, their brand colours and a short line such as the campaign name.
- **Event with a queue:** a **Live view** background lets people in line see themselves on screen, which draws more of them to the booth.
- **Several modes at one event:** Photo, Video and 360/Slow-mo as round buttons, so guests pick what they want on the first screen.

This screen belongs to one event. For a booth that runs on its own day after day (a Kiosk booth), design its screens in the UI Editor instead: see [Customise the Booth Screens](./customise-kiosk-screens.md).

## Before You Start

- **An event** created in **Events**, with its modes picked. See [Run an Event](./run-an-event.md).
- **Plan:** you can design the start screen on any plan. To run the event, each computer needs Pix Pro or an Event Pass.
- **Booth app:** Pixture Photobooth 1.2.44 or newer shows the full design, including the Darkroom look, a Gradient background, the **Templates** start and the lock in the timer. The event page names any booth that needs an update.
- **Templates** on the event if you offer **Photo**, **Studio** or **Notes**: their buttons stay hidden until the event has a template.

## Prepare

- **Background photo or video:** a JPG, PNG or WebP photo, or an MP4 or WebM video, in the shape of the booth screen (landscape or portrait). Pick one with a calm area where the names go. You can darken and blur it in the editor, so a busy photo still works.
- **The words:** a short lead-in ("Wedding of", "Welcome to"), the title (names or event name) and the date line. Short lines read from across the room.
- **Logo:** PNG, SVG or WebP with a transparent background.
- **Fonts** (optional): WOFF2, WOFF, TTF or OTF files, up to 4 MB each.
- **Your brand:** if you set up [your Brand](./set-up-your-brand.md), an event you have not styled starts in your colours and fonts, and **Your brand** is one of the themes.

## Step 1: Open the event's Setup tab

1. In the sidebar, click **Events** and open your event.
2. Open the **Setup** tab.

![Event page with Events in the sidebar and the Setup tab marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-event-start-screen--event-setup-tab.webp)

## Step 2: Open the editor

Still on the **Setup** tab, the numbers carry on from Step 1 (they match the marks in the picture). On the **Booth mode** card:

3. Open the **Appearance** tab. It shows the current start screen with "Make it yours: your names, a photo and your colours."
4. Click **Edit appearance**.

![Booth mode card with the Appearance tab and Edit appearance marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-event-start-screen--edit-appearance.webp)

The editor shows a live preview of the booth, with **Landscape** and **Portrait** switches, and a rail of settings groups: **Modes**, **Background**, **Text**, **Logo**, **Gallery & lock**, **Colours**, **Countdown**, **Timer** and **Steps**. Click a part of the preview, such as the title or the logo, to open its settings.

![Edit appearance editor with the live preview, the Landscape and Portrait switch, the settings rail and Save marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-event-start-screen--editor.webp)

## Step 3: Set the background and the words

1. In **Background**, pick the type: **Photo or video** (upload your file), **Colour**, **Gradient** (a soft glow from a **Top** colour into a **Bottom** colour) or **Live view** (guests see themselves behind the buttons, like a mirror).
2. With a photo, video or live view, set **Darken** (0 to 80%) until the words are easy to read, and **Blur** (0 to 100%) to soften the picture.
3. In **Text**, fill in **Subtitle**, **Title** and **Date**. Each line has its own switch, font, size (60% to 200%) and colour. Leave the colour on **Auto** to keep it readable on your background. **Use event date** fills the date for you.
4. In **Logo**, upload your logo, pick a **Position** (**Above**, **Instead** of the title, **Corner**, **Bottom**, or **Free** to drag it anywhere) and a **Size** (60 to 320 px).

## Step 4: Set the colours and the mode buttons

1. In **Colours**, pick a theme: **Your brand** (when your brand is set up), **Pixture**, **Forest**, **Ink blue**, **Plum**, **Cream** or **Darkroom**. Or set your own **Primary**, **Secondary**, **Background** and **Mode circles**. Primary buttons can be **Gradient** or **Flat**.
2. In **Modes**, choose what **Start shows**: **Mode buttons** (a round button per mode) or **Templates** (guests see the event's templates with a sample photo and tap **Start**).
3. Set the **Button style** (**Illustration**, **Mono** or **Icon**) and whether **Button names** show under the buttons. Click a mode's picture to use your own.
4. In **Gallery & lock**, choose whether guests get a **Gallery button** to reopen this event's photos, and where the **Lock button** sits (it opens the operator menu behind the PIN). **During a session**, the lock sits **In the timer** or **In its corner**.

To add or remove a mode, use **Edit modes** on the event page (the editor's **Change modes on the event page** leads there). **Photographer** and **Mingle** never show as buttons: those computers skip the start screen.

## Step 5: Set the countdown, the timers and the steps

1. **Countdown:** the seconds before each photo, 1 to 10. 3 seconds suits most guests.
2. **Timer > Session time:** **Booth's own**, **No limit**, **60 s**, **90 s**, **2 min** or **3 min**. When time is up, the booth moves on with the photos taken.
3. **Timer > Photo review** (with Photo, Notes, AI or Mingle on): how long each photo stays on screen with Retake and Next, **3 s** to **30 s**.
4. **Timer > Note time** (with Notes on): 30 seconds to 3 minutes for guests to write their note.
5. **Steps:** switch off **Adjust your photos**, **Choose a filter** or **Retake** for shorter sessions.

## Step 6: Preview and save

1. Click **Preview** at the top to try the whole event full screen with your changes, saved or not. Tap through one session as a guest would.
2. Click **Save**. The editor says "Saved. Booths update now."

Booths on the event pick up the new look for their next session: a booth waiting on its start screen at once, a booth in the middle of a session when that session ends. Back on the event page, **Try it full screen** shows the saved design.

## Check It Worked

- The **Appearance** tab on the event page shows your new start screen, and the tiles under it list your text and fonts, background, colours, logo and buttons.
- On the booth, the start screen shows your design. If a booth runs an older app, the event page names it, for example "Booth 1 runs an older Pixture Photobooth. Update it to show this start screen as designed."
- Run one session: the countdown, the review time and the steps match what you set.

## Tips

- **Live view sells the booth.** People see themselves from across the room and walk over. Add some **Darken** so the names stay readable.
- **One look per client, start from your brand.** Set up your Brand once; each new event starts there, and you only change the names, photo and maybe the colours.
- **Shorter sessions at busy events.** Switch off **Adjust your photos** and set **Photo review** to 5 seconds. Each guest moves faster and the queue shrinks.
- **Keep the lock quiet, not hidden.** Lower the **Opacity** of the **Lock button** so it does not draw attention, but keep it where your staff know to tap.
- **Check portrait and landscape.** If the event uses booths in both shapes, switch the preview and check both before saving.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Why | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| The Photo, Studio or Notes button does not show | The event has no template yet | Add a template on the event's **Template** tab |
| A booth shows an older look | The booth runs Pixture Photobooth 1.2.43 or older | Update the app on that booth |
| The changes do not show on a booth | The booth is in the middle of a session, or the design was not saved | Click **Save**; the booth picks it up when the session ends |
| The words are hard to read on the photo | The background is too bright or busy | Raise **Darken** and **Blur**, or set the text colour to **Auto** |
| "That file is too large." | The upload is over its limit (fonts are up to 4 MB) | Export a smaller file and upload again |
| A mode is missing from **Modes** | Modes are turned on and off on the event page | Use **Edit modes** on the event's **Booth mode** card |
