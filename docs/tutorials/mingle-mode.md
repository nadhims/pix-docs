# Take the Booth to the Guests

In this tutorial you will set up an event so one or more booth computers go round the room with you, and guests get a full photo session wherever they are: template, photos, filter, print and QR. Allow about 15 minutes in the dashboard and 10 minutes for a test round.

**Mingle** is the Photo session on a computer you carry. Guests still see themselves in the live view and pick a template. The difference is the shutter: you, the operator, can shoot the moment the group is ready, with the camera's own shutter or a clicker in your hand, instead of waiting for a countdown.

## When to Use It

- **A seated dinner or gala.** Guests will not leave their table for a booth in the corner. Roll the booth to each table and shoot the table where it sits.
- **A crowded wedding reception.** The booth goes to the dance floor, the family table and the grandparents who never walk up to a booth.
- **A brand activation that moves.** Staff take the booth through the crowd and shoot people where the energy is.
- **One event, two styles.** A standing booth runs Photo for guests while a second computer mingles with you.

If a photographer should shoot groups through the camera with no live view for guests, use [Photographer mode](./photographer-mode.md) instead.

## Before You Start

- **An event**, and every computer that mingles paired to it with **Pix Pro** or an **Event Pass**. See [Run an Event](./run-an-event.md).
- **Pixture Photobooth 1.2.29 or newer** on the computers that mingle. An older computer runs a Mingle event as a plain Photo session, at Mingle's price.
- **At least one print template on the event.** Mingle uses the event's templates, like Photo.
- **A carry-able booth**: a touchscreen computer on a rolling stand or a laptop rig, with the camera mounted to it.
- Optional: a **remote clicker** (a Bluetooth or USB presenter) for the operator.

## Prepare

- **Power first.** A booth on the move has no wall socket. Check how long the computer, camera and any light run on battery, and bring spares for a long event.
- **Think about the printer.** If the printer stays at a base table, share it over the network so the carried computer prints to it. See [Share One Printer Between Booths](./share-a-printer.md).
- **Expect patchy Wi-Fi.** A booth that moves drops in and out of the venue's network. Sessions, prints and QR codes carry on, and photos upload when it reconnects. See [Keep the Booth Running Offline](./keep-running-offline.md).
- **Pair the clicker with the booth computer** before the event and check it sends a key the booth listens to: Space, S, Page Up, Page Down or F13. See [Use a Button Box](./use-a-button-box.md).

## Step 1: Turn on Mingle for the event

1. In the dashboard, open **Events**, open your event and go to the **Setup** tab.
2. On the **Booth mode** card, click **Edit modes**.
3. Tick **Mingle** ("Carry the booth to your guests.") and click **Save**.

![The event Setup tab with Edit modes marked on the Booth mode card](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-mingle-mode--edit-modes.webp)

<!-- SHOT-NEEDED: dashboard: the Edit modes popup with Mingle ticked and Save -->

A **Mingle** card joins the Booth mode card. Mingle is never a button on the guest start screen: only the computers you carry run it.

## Step 2: Pick the templates

1. Open the **Template** tab and add the print templates guests can pick, as you do for Photo.
2. Mingle runs the Photo session, so the event's **Output** (prints, GIF, live photo, singles) applies to Mingle too.

## Step 3: Set up the Mingle tab

1. Click the **Mingle** card to open its tab.
2. **Remote clicker**: choose what a press on the clicker does. **Shoots right away** (the default) takes the photo at once. **Starts the countdown** runs the countdown, as a tap on the screen does. The tab reminds you: "Your camera's shutter always shoots right away. Tapping the screen always counts down."
3. **Mingle computers**: tick the computers you carry. When Mingle is the event's only guest mode, there is nothing to tick: "Every computer mingles. Turn on another mode to pick."

<!-- SHOT-NEEDED: dashboard: the Mingle tab with Remote clicker and Mingle computers -->

Changes save by themselves. While other guest modes are on and no computer is ticked, the tab shows "!" with "Pick which computers mingle", and no computer mingles. Mingle computers carry a **Mingle** badge in the **Computers** card. A computer is either Mingle or Photographer, never both: ticking it on one tab takes it off the other.

## Step 4: Give Mingle its own price (paid events)

1. At the top of the event page, click **Edit**.
2. With **Pay Per Session** on, find **Price per mode** and fill in the **Mingle** row: **Single**, **Double** and **Group**.
3. Click **Save Changes**.

<!-- SHOT-NEEDED: dashboard: the Edit Event popup with the Price per mode grid and the Mingle row -->

"Leave empty to use the event price." A package you leave empty on the Mingle row sells at Photo's price for that package, or the event price when Photo has none. Charge more for Mingle when the operator's time is part of the service.

## Step 5: Shoot on the move

1. Open the event on the computer you ticked, as for any event. A Mingle computer shows only Mingle, with no mode choice.
2. The guest picks a template, as in a Photo session.
3. Take the photos the way that suits the moment:
   - **Tap the screen**: a countdown runs, then the photo is taken.
   - **Press the camera's shutter**: the photo is taken at once, and a running countdown is cut short.
   - **Press the clicker**: what you chose under **Remote clicker**.
4. After each photo the guest sees it, then the next slot starts its countdown by itself if nobody presses anything. Then filter, print and QR follow, as in a Photo session.

<!-- SHOT-NEEDED: booth: a Mingle session on the capture screen with the live view and countdown -->

The camera's shutter does nothing while a photo is shown for review, so a nervous double press never skips a guest's check.

## Check It Worked

- The carried computer opens on Mingle with no mode choice, while the other computers show the start screen.
- The camera's shutter takes a photo straight away, and a tap on the screen counts down.
- At a paid event, the payment screen shows Mingle's price.
- The sessions show in the event's **Album** tab, recorded as Mingle sessions.

## Tips

- **Shutter for groups, countdown for selfies.** Use the camera's shutter when you are framing a group; let guests tap the screen when they want a moment to pose.
- **Live photos need a countdown.** The live photo's moving clip is recorded during the countdown. A slot shot straight away with the camera's shutter or the clicker shows as a still photo in the live photo. If the client wants live photos, set **Remote clicker** to **Starts the countdown**.
- **Notes after Photo does not run on Mingle computers.** If the event writes notes after the photos, guests do that at the standing booth.
- **Keep one computer standing.** With Photo on as well, guests who find you still have a booth to come back to.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Why | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| The carried computer shows the start screen with every mode | It is not ticked as a Mingle computer | Tick it under **Mingle computers** on the **Mingle** tab |
| No computer mingles | Other guest modes are on and none is ticked | Tick at least one computer on the **Mingle** tab |
| The camera's shutter does nothing | The photo is on review, or the computer runs an app older than 1.2.29 | Wait for the next slot; update Pixture Photobooth |
| The clicker starts a countdown instead of shooting | **Remote clicker** is set to **Starts the countdown** | Set it to **Shoots right away** |
| The clicker does nothing | It sends a key the booth does not listen to, or it is paired with another device | Pair it with the booth computer and use a key it sends: Space, S, Page Up, Page Down or F13 |
| Guests pay the Photo price | The Mingle row is empty | Fill in the **Mingle** row under **Price per mode** |
