# Read Your Dashboard

In this tutorial you will pick the workspace that shows the money you care about, set the period, and read every card on the **Dashboard**: revenue, sessions, completion, the busiest day, prints, vouchers, your most used templates and your best booths. Allow about 10 minutes.

**Dashboard** is the first page after you log in at pixture.io. It counts what your paired booths upload, so it fills up as photo sessions arrive.

## When to Use It

- **A self-service booth in a mall.** Open the Dashboard on **Day** each evening: revenue, sessions and prints tell you if the booth earned what it should, and **Peak Hour** tells you when to staff it or restock paper.
- **Event rental.** Switch to **Pixture Event** to see only the money from your events, without your mall booths mixed in.
- **A multi-booth studio.** Use **All Regions** and **Top Booth Revenue** on **Month** to see which venue carries the month and which one to move.
- **A pop-up box for a few weeks.** Pick the booth in **All Booths** and compare **Week** against the week before to see if the spot is worth keeping.

To find one photo or one payment, the Dashboard is the wrong page: use [Find a Photo or a Payment](./gallery-and-transactions.md). For costs and profit, use [Finance](../dashboard/finance.md).

## Before You Start

- A Pixture account on any plan: the Dashboard works on Pix Starter, Pix Pro and with Event Passes.
- At least one paired booth that has run a photo session. A new account shows zeros until then. See [Set Up a Booth From Scratch](./set-up-a-booth.md).
- Team members see only the cards and booths their role allows. See [Add Your Team and Set Roles](./team-and-roles.md).

## Step 1: Pick your workspace

Your account has three workspaces. They change which money and which booths you see, never how a booth takes photos.

1. Click the switcher at the bottom of the sidebar.
2. **Overview**: "Both together: total revenue and the split."
3. **Pixture Kiosk**: self-service booths that stay in a venue. Kiosk numbers leave out every event sale.
4. **Pixture Event**: booths at weddings, expos and parties. Only event money.

![The workspace switcher open at the bottom of the sidebar with the switcher button, Overview, Pixture Kiosk and Pixture Event marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-read-your-dashboard--switcher.webp)

Every sale is counted once, in one workspace, so **Pixture Kiosk plus Pixture Event always equals Overview**. A sale is Event money when the session was started for an event, or it was taken on a booth set up for events (an extra print or AI portrait for that session too). Everything else is Kiosk money.

The Overview hides **Booths**, **Vouchers** and **Events** from the sidebar, since they belong to one side. Open **Booths** or **Vouchers** and you land in Pixture Kiosk; open **Events** and you land in Pixture Event. Your choice is remembered in this browser; a browser that never chose starts on the Overview.

## Step 2: Set the period and the booths

1. **All Regions**: only the booths in one region. Regions are set on each booth's **Settings** tab.
2. **All Booths**: one booth only.
3. **Day**, **Week** or **Month**: the period every card reports.
4. The arrows and the date move to an earlier or later period. Click the date to pick one.

![The Dashboard top bar with All Regions, All Booths, Day Week Month and the period picker marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-read-your-dashboard--filters.webp)

If your booths sell in more than one currency, a **Showing** row of currency pills appears. Each currency has its own figures; nothing is converted or added up.

## Step 3: Read revenue

1. The big number is the money taken in the period, in the booth's currency.
2. The eye hides the figure, for when a customer or a venue manager looks over your shoulder. Click it again to show it; this browser remembers your choice.
3. The chip compares with the same stretch of the previous period, with those dates ("vs Oct 1–13"), so the 13th of this month is compared with the 13th of last month, not with a whole month.
4. The chart shows the period day by day. "Month to date" or "Week to date" means the chart stops at today.

![The Revenue card with the total, the eye toggle, the comparison chip and the chart period marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-read-your-dashboard--revenue.webp)

Revenue adds paid sessions, the value of sessions paid with a voucher you sold, Frame Lab purchases, and sales with no session such as online vouchers. A payment taken through a test (sandbox) gateway never counts, so test as much as you like before opening.

## Step 4: Read the session cards

1. **Sessions**: every photo session that started, paid, free or with a voucher, with the change from the previous period.
2. **Session Completion**: completed sessions out of completed plus failed. Sessions still running are left out.
3. **Peak Day** (on **Week** and **Month**) or **Peak Hour** (on **Day**): when most sessions happened.
4. **Prints**: every print your booths made in the period.
5. **Vouchers**: sessions paid with a voucher code.

![The row of cards with Sessions, Session Completion, Peak Day, Prints and Vouchers marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-read-your-dashboard--cards.webp)

## Step 5: Find your best templates and booths

These cards sit below the session cards on the **Pixture Kiosk** Dashboard (the Pixture Event Dashboard keeps **Top Booth Revenue** and **Health**).

1. **Templates Usage**: your templates ranked by sessions. Sort by **Most used**, **Least used**, **Recently used** or **Least recently used**, or search one by name.
2. **Top Booth Revenue**: your booths ranked by revenue, with each one's share.
3. **Health**: "All clear" when every booth is fine, otherwise the booths that need a look. Click a booth to open **Booth Details**: today's sessions and revenue, **First Online** and **Last Online**, **Active Hours** and **Down Hours**, **Hourly Activity** and **Paper Level**.
4. **Latest Session**: the last photo session, with its booth, template and time.

![Templates Usage with its sort menu, and Top Booth Revenue, Health and Latest Session marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-read-your-dashboard--templates-booths-health.webp)

## Step 6: Read the split on the Overview

On the **Overview**, the chart stacks Pixture Kiosk and Pixture Event, and two cards follow the session cards.

1. **Pixture Kiosk**: its total, its share of the whole and its top booths.
2. **Pixture Event**: the same for event money.

![The Overview with the Pixture Kiosk and Pixture Event cards marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-read-your-dashboard--overview-split.webp)

## Check It Worked

- Run one test session on a booth: within a few minutes **Sessions** goes up by one and **Latest Session** shows it.
- Pixture Kiosk revenue plus Pixture Event revenue for the same period equals the Overview revenue.
- **Day** with today picked shows **Peak Hour**; **Month** shows **Peak Day**.

## Tips

- **Compare like with like.** Halfway through a month, the chip already compares with the first half of last month, so a low number on the 3rd is not a bad month yet.
- **A low Session Completion** means customers start but do not finish. Look at the booth on [Health](./booth-health-alerts.md) (camera or printer errors) and at how long your capture and review screens take.
- **Clean up test days.** Rehearsal sessions count as Sessions and Prints. Delete them in the Gallery (see [Find a Photo or a Payment](./gallery-and-transactions.md)) so your numbers show customers only.
- **Name booths by place** ("Grand Mall L2"), and put them in regions, so Top Booth Revenue and the filters read at a glance.
- **On your phone**, the Pixture Manager app shows the same numbers. See [Run Your Account from Your iPhone](./iphone-app.md).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Why | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Everything is zero | No paired booth has uploaded a session yet | Pair a booth and run one test session |
| Kiosk revenue is lower than it used to be | Event sales now count under Pixture Event, not Kiosk | Look at the Overview, or switch to Pixture Event for the difference |
| No workspace switcher in the sidebar | Your role cannot open Events, so you see Kiosk money only | Ask the owner to turn on **View events** for your role |
| Some cards are missing | Your role does not include those cards, or you are on the Overview, which has no Templates Usage, Health or Latest Session card | Switch to Pixture Kiosk, or ask the owner about your role |
| A payment I took on a test gateway is missing | Test (sandbox) payments are never revenue | Nothing to fix: live payments count |
| The figure shows dots instead of a number | The eye toggle hid it in this browser | Click the eye |
