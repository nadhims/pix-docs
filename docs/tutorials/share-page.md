# Set Up the Share Page

In this tutorial you will choose which files every session puts on its share page, brand that page with your colours, links and a promo banner, save the design to your booths and check it on your own phone. Allow about 20 minutes.

Every photo session gets its own page on the web. The customer scans the QR code on the booth's Sharing screen, the page opens on their phone, and there they download the print, the single photos, the GIF and the live photo. The look of that page is called the **Microsite** in Pix Design.

## When to Use It

- **A self-service booth in a mall.** The share page is the one thing a customer keeps after they walk away. Your colours, your Instagram link and a promo banner ("Come back this weekend, 20% off") turn a download into a return visit.
- **An event rental.** Give a wedding or brand client's booth the client's colours and a link to their page, while your other booths keep your own look.
- **A multi-booth studio.** Design the page once and save it to every booth in one go.
- **A pop-up box.** Put a booking link in the menu, so people who liked the photos can book the box for their own party.

The share page is one page per session. For one page with every photo of an event, use the event album instead: see [Share an Event Album and Slideshow](./event-album-and-slideshow.md).

## Before You Start

- **Any plan for the share page itself.** Every session on a paired booth gets one.
- **Pix Pro on the booth's computer to save a design for it.** On Pix Starter you can open **Pix Design > Microsite** and try every setting, but the save needs Pix Pro on that booth's computer. An Event Pass counts as Pix Pro.
- **Pix Pro on the booth's computer for clean files and extra outputs.** On Pix Starter the photos on the page carry the Pixture watermark, and the **Singles Upload**, **GIF Upload** and **Live Photo Upload** switches are greyed out.
- **Pix Pro Yearly** only if you want to hide the **Powered by Pixture** footer (Step 5).

## Prepare

Get these ready before you open the editor. Every image is PNG or JPG, up to 10 MB.

- **Your logo**, set once in **Pix Design > Brand**. The page header shows it. Use a PNG with a transparent background so it sits cleanly on any header colour. See [Set Up Your Brand](./set-up-your-brand.md).
- **A header banner** (optional): a wide strip that sits at the top of the page. Something like 1200 by 300 pixels reads well on a phone. Keep text large; it is shown at phone width.
- **A promo banner** (optional): it pops up over the page when it opens and is cover-cropped to portrait 4:5. Design it at 4:5 (for example 1080 by 1350 pixels) and keep the words and the offer in the middle, so nothing important is cut off.
- **A background image** (optional): fills the whole page behind the photo card. A soft, quiet picture works best; a busy one fights with the customer's photos.
- **Your links**: up to 10, for example Instagram, WhatsApp, a booking form or your online shop.
- **A brand font** (optional): WOFF2, WOFF, TTF or OTF, up to 5 MB.

:::tip Colours that work
Customers open the page on a phone, often outdoors. Pick a button colour that stands out clearly from the page background, and dark text on a light page (or the reverse). The buttons are what customers tap most.
:::

## Step 1: Choose what goes on the page

1. In the dashboard, click **Booths** and open the booth.
2. Open the **Settings** tab.
3. Under **Output Settings**, turn on what the booth should upload: **Singles Upload** (each photo on its own), **GIF Upload** (an animation of the shots) and **Live Photo Upload** (a short moving clip of each shot). The print itself is always on the page.
4. Click **Save Output Settings**.

![Booth Settings tab with Booths, the Settings tab, the Singles, GIF and Live Photo Upload switches and Save Output Settings marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-share-page--booth-output-settings.webp)

Each switch is per booth. Leave them all on unless you have a reason: customers share GIFs and live photos far more than the print.

## Step 2: Brand the page

1. Open **Pix Design > Microsite**. The tabs across the top are **Page**, **Header**, **Buttons**, **Promo banner** and **Menu links**, with a live preview of the page on the right.
2. On **Page**, set the **Background**, **Text** and **Details** colours. Optionally pick a **Font**, upload a **Custom font (optional)** or a **Background image**.
3. Under **Show first**, choose which file the page opens on: **Live Photo** (the default), **Prints**, **GIF** or **Singles**. A session without that file opens on its print.
4. Watch the preview after each change, then click **Save changes** (Step 4 covers which booths get it).

![Microsite page with the Page, Header, Buttons, Promo banner and Menu links tabs, the colours, Show first and Save changes marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-share-page--microsite.webp)

On **Header**, set the header's **Background** and **Text** colours. **Header image (optional)** replaces your logo just on this page, with an **Image size** slider; **Header banner (optional)** adds the wide strip, and **Header banner link (optional)** makes it tappable. On **Buttons**, set the **Fill**, **Text** and **Outline** colours, the **Corner radius** and a **Drop shadow**.

## Step 3: Add your links and a promo banner

1. Open **Menu links** and click **+ Add link** for each link (up to 10). Customers see them in the page's menu, under their photos. If you have published a [Website](./publish-your-website.md), the menu already starts with it.
2. Open **Promo banner** and drop your 4:5 image into **Banner image**. Add a **Banner link (optional)**, for example your online shop.
3. In the preview, tap the × on the promo the way a customer would. **Show promo popup again** brings it back so you can check it after a change.

![Microsite Menu links tab with + Add link, and the Promo banner tab with Banner image and Banner link marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-share-page--microsite-links-banner.webp)

## Step 4: Save the design to your booths

The share page design is held per booth, so you choose which booths get it.

1. Click **Save changes**. With more than one booth, the **Save to booths** window opens: "This microsite design applies to the booths you pick." Every booth starts ticked.
2. Click **Choose**, untick the booths that should keep their own design, for example a client's booth at an event, and click **Apply**.
3. Click **Save**. The message reads "Saved to N booths."

A booth whose computer is not on Pix Pro is skipped, and the message says how many were skipped. With only one booth, **Save changes** saves straight away.

![Save to booths window with Choose and Save, and the booth list with Apply](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-share-page--save-to-booths.webp)

## Step 5: Hide the Powered by Pixture footer (Pix Pro Yearly)

The share page, the event album and the photo emails end with a **Powered by Pixture** footer. On Pix Pro Yearly you can show your business name there instead.

1. Open **Pix Design > Brand** and find **Pixture footer**.
2. Turn **Show on guest pages** off. The **Guest page** preview shows your business name in the footer.

On any other plan the switch stays on and locked, with "Yearly plan." and a **Go yearly** link. If the account stops having Pix Pro Yearly, the footer comes back by itself, and hides again when you go back to yearly.

![Brand page with the Pixture footer card and the Show on guest pages switch marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-share-page--brand-footer.webp)

## Step 6: Put the links on your own domain (optional)

Share links use the Pixture address by default, free on every plan. Your own address, such as photos.yourbusiness.com, is set up on request.

1. Click **Settings** in the sidebar, then **Own domain** (under **Advanced**). The **Pixture Address** card shows the address your links use today.
2. On the **Own domain** card ("Use your own domain for guest links."), click **Contact us**. Pixture sets it up with you.
3. Once your account is unlocked, the same card asks for your domain and shows the DNS record to add at your domain provider. Add it, then click **Verify DNS**.

![Settings Own domain with the Pixture Address card and the Own domain card's Contact us marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-share-page--own-domain.webp)

## Step 7: Test it on your phone

1. At the booth, run a full session.
2. On the Sharing screen, scan the QR code with your phone (or tap **Scan QR** first if the screen shows the button).
3. Check the page against the list below.

## Check It Worked

- The page opens in your colours, with your logo or header image, on the file you chose under **Show first**.
- The buttons match what you turned on in Step 1, and **Download All** names the number of files.
- Under the photos the page says until when the photos are available: 60 days after the session.
- Your menu links and promo banner show, and the footer reads as you set it.

## Tips

- **Tell customers about the 60 days.** Photos are deleted 60 days after the session, and the page counts down the days in its last two weeks. Put "Download your photos within 60 days" on your Sharing screen design or at the counter.
- **Put the QR on the print too.** In the template editor, **+ Add QR code** prints the session's own link on the strip, so customers can find their photos again weeks later. See [Design a Print Template](./design-a-print-template.md).
- **Live Photo first sells the booth.** A moving clip is what people post. Keep **Show first** on **Live Photo** unless your brand is all about the print.
- **Use the promo to bring customers back**, with a link to your online session shop or your Website.
- **One design per venue.** Because the design is per booth, a venue partner can have its own colours and links while your other booths keep yours.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Why | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| The page still has the plain Pixture look | The design was not saved to this booth, or the booth's computer is not on Pix Pro | Click **Save changes** and tick this booth; put the computer on Pix Pro or an Event Pass |
| "Saved · 1 booth skipped" | That booth's computer is not on Pix Pro | Add Pix Pro or an Event Pass to that computer, then save again |
| The photos carry a Pixture watermark | The booth's computer is on Pix Starter, or its Pix Pro ran out | Put the computer on Pix Pro or an Event Pass. See [Add a Second Computer or Move Pix Pro](./add-or-move-a-computer.md) |
| No GIF, Live Photo or Singles button | The switch is off in **Output Settings**, or the computer is on Pix Starter | Turn it on in Step 1 (needs Pix Pro) |
| The QR opens but the photos are missing | The booth is offline or still uploading | The page fills in by itself once the booth is back online. See [Keep the Booth Running Offline](./keep-running-offline.md) |
| "This photo is no longer available" | The session is more than 60 days old; its files were deleted | Nothing to restore. Remind customers to download in time |
| **Show on guest pages** is locked | The account is not on Pix Pro Yearly | Switch to yearly with **Go yearly** |
