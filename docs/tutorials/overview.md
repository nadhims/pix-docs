# Tutorials

Each tutorial takes one job from start to finish: when it is worth using, what to prepare, numbered steps with marked screenshots, how to check it worked, and what to do when it does not. New to Pixture? Start with [Set Up a Booth From Scratch](./set-up-a-booth.md). If a word is unfamiliar, see [How Pixture Works](../getting-started/how-pixture-works.md) or the [Glossary](../reference/glossary.md).

## Get the Booth Computer Ready

- [Set Up a Booth From Scratch](./set-up-a-booth.md). Create a booth in the Pixture dashboard, give it templates, a screen design, filters and pricing, pair the booth PC and run a test photo session.
- [Make the Booth Start by Itself After a Power Cut](./start-after-a-power-cut.md). Set up a Mac or Windows booth computer so it powers on when electricity returns, signs in without a password, never sleeps, does not restart for updates mid-day, and opens the Pixture Photobooth app by itself.
- [Lock the Screen to the Booth](./lock-the-screen.md). Stop the taskbar, notifications, widgets and edge swipes from appearing over the booth on a Windows touchscreen, and keep notifications, hot corners and the screen saver away on a Mac.
- [Lock the Booth and Use the Operator Menu](./operator-menu.md). Set a menu PIN for every booth or one booth, open the hidden operator menu at the booth, find your way around its pages, and get back to customers. With the shortcuts staff use most during an event.
- [Get a Bright Live View with Flash](./bright-live-view-with-flash.md). Light a booth with a flash or studio strobes and still give customers a bright live view. Camera settings for flash, a modelling light or the camera's exposure preview switch per brand, and EXTERNAL FLASH for Canon DSLRs.

## Design

- [Design a Print Template](./design-a-print-template.md). Prepare a PNG at print size with clean photo boxes, turn it into a template in Pix Design, make the boxes see-through with Remove BG in one click, check the photo slots and assign the template to your booths.
- [Customise the Booth Screens](./customise-kiosk-screens.md). Build your own booth screen design in the Pix Design UI Editor, from the Start Screen to Sharing, then assign it to a booth.
- [Set Up Your Brand](./set-up-your-brand.md). Put your logo, colours, button style and fonts into Pix Design > Brand once, so your event screens, booth designs and guest pages start in your look instead of Pixture's.
- [Add Filters to Your Booth](./add-filters.md). Choose which of the 14 built-in colour filters each booth offers, add your own looks as .cube files, and keep the Filter screen short and on-brand.
- [Add GIF and Video Overlays](./gif-and-video-overlays.md). Design a transparent frame at the right size, add it in Pix Design, and put it on a booth's GIFs or on an event's GIFs, videos and 360 clips.
- [Design an Event Start Screen](./event-start-screen.md). Style the first screen guests see at your event (background, names, logo, colours and mode buttons), set the countdown and timers, and preview it full screen before the booth goes live.

## The Booth

- [Connect a Camera](./connect-a-camera.md). Connect a Canon, Sony, Nikon or Fujifilm camera, a webcam or a virtual camera to the booth over USB, pick it as the booth's camera, and fix "Camera Disconnected" before customers arrive.
- [Use Two or More Cameras](./multi-camera.md). Put two to six cameras on one Kiosk booth, name each angle in the dashboard, give every angle its camera on the booth, and let customers choose the camera before every photo.
- [Set Up Flash and Lighting](./flash-and-lighting.md). Choose between continuous light and flash, place the lights for one person or a group, connect a speedlite or studio strobes, and set the booth's camera so every photo comes out evenly lit.
- [Set Up a Printer](./set-up-a-printer.md). Install a photo printer, pick it on the booth with the right paper and orientation, line up the print, cut 4x6 sheets into 2x6 strips on a DNP, and let the booth count the paper left on the roll.
- [Share One Printer Between Booths](./share-a-printer.md). Run two or more booths on one photo printer by sharing it over the local network from the computer it is plugged into, then pick it on every booth and keep the queue moving on a busy day.
- [Keep the Booth Running Offline](./keep-running-offline.md). Prepare a booth for a venue with bad or no internet, know what keeps working offline (sessions, prints, QR links, payment codes and vouchers), and make sure every photo uploads before anyone resets the computer.
- [Use a Button Box](./use-a-button-box.md). Let customers take, retake and keep photos with a real button, clicker or foot switch. Set the Capture, Retake and Keep keys once in the dashboard and every booth on your account follows.

## Photo Sessions and Sharing

- [Run a Studio Mode Session](./studio-mode.md). Set up a Studio booth where customers shoot freely for a set time, then pick which shots go into the print, and run your first timed session.
- [Run a Decor Session](./notes-session.md). Let customers and guests write, draw and type a note on their photos, at a self-service booth or at an event. Choose whether the note gets its own button or comes after Photo, whether it prints, which design it is written on and which fonts it can be typed in.
- [Run a Newspaper Session](./newspaper-session.md). Turn a portrait booth into a newspaper booth where customers see themselves live on the front page, swipe between your newspapers, pay and shoot, and take home their own newspaper print.
- [Run Video and 360 Sessions](./video-and-360-sessions.md). Turn an event's booths into a video booth or a 360/Slow-mo booth. Set the clip length, the slow-motion timeline, overlays, an intro and outro and a soundtrack, keep the original clips, and adjust it all at the booth during the event.
- [Shoot as a Roaming Photographer](./photographer-mode.md). Turn on Photographer mode for an event, pick the print template and the computers that shoot, then shoot each group with your camera's own shutter while the booth fills the template, saves the group and shows its QR.
- [Take the Booth to the Guests](./mingle-mode.md). Turn on Mingle for an event, pick the computers you carry, choose what the remote clicker does and set Mingle's own price, then walk the booth from table to table and shoot with the screen, the camera's shutter or a clicker.
- [Let Customers Review and Retake](./review-and-retake.md). Decide how long customers see each photo, whether they can retake it, and whether they can move, zoom, mirror and swap their photos before the print, on a booth and at an event.
- [Set Up the Share Page](./share-page.md). Choose what customers get on the page their QR code opens, brand it in your colours with your links and a promo, save it to your booths, and know how long the photos stay, what the Pixture footer and watermark do, and how to ask for your own domain.
- [Let Customers Email Their Photos](./email-my-photos.md). Give customers an Email button next to Scan QR on the booth, so they send their photos to their own inbox, with what you need, how to show the button, what the email looks like and the limits to know.
- [Share an Event Album and Slideshow](./event-album-and-slideshow.md). Share an event's public album with the client and guests, put it on the client's website, download every print, and play a live slideshow of the album on a TV at the venue.

## Payments

- [Create a Payment Gateway](./create-a-payment-gateway.md). Open a merchant account with DOKU, Stripe or another supported provider, collect the keys Pixture asks for, connect and activate the gateway, run the test charge, and add the optional webhook and card readers.
- [Accept Payments at the Booth](./accept-payments.md). Connect a payment gateway, set what a photo session costs at one booth, choose what the Payment screen offers, and take the first QR or card payment on the booth.
- [Payment Setups by Use Case](./payment-setups-by-use-case.md). The payment setup that fits each way of running a photo booth, from event rentals and self-service mall booths to multi-booth studios and pop-up boxes, with the Pixture settings for each.
- [Set Prices, Packages and Extra Prints](./prices-and-packages.md). Set what a session costs at one booth, sell Double and Group packages and extra sessions, charge for extra prints, price each mode, and add tax and fees, with the preview showing what customers will see.
- [Sell and Give Vouchers](./vouchers.md). Make voucher codes that pay for a photo session at the booth, as session tickets you sell for cash or ahead of time, or as staff passes for free tests and VIPs, one at a time or in batches.
- [Run a Promo](./run-a-promo.md). Run a happy hour, a launch week or a promo code at your booths, with an amount or percentage off, a promo price or Pay 1 get 1, on the days, hours and booths you choose.
- [Take Card, Coin and Cash Payments](./connect-a-coin-or-card-reader.md). Take money at the booth without a QR code, with a Stripe Terminal card reader, a coin or note acceptor, a Nayax reader or a Transpire QR DuitNow terminal on a CASH-Interface2 board, your own bridge on the local Hardware API, or cash at the counter turned into voucher codes.
- [Sell Sessions Online](./sell-sessions-online.md). Give customers a phone page where they buy a photo session at your booth's prices and get a code the booth accepts, even offline.
- [Sell AI Portraits From the Share Page (Beta)](./sell-ai-portraits.md). Let customers buy an AI portrait of their booth photo from their share page, digital or printed at the booth, by setting up credits, presets and a price.
- [Let Customers Design Their Own Frame (Frame Lab)](./frame-lab.md). Switch on Frame Lab, share the customer link, and follow a customer from designing a frame on their phone to paying, receiving a voucher and using it at the booth.

## Events and Self-Service

- [Run an Event](./run-an-event.md). Create an event, pair the computers that run it, start an Event Pass on the day, give guests your modes, templates and start screen, share the album with the client, and see what the event earned.
- [Run a Queue Line](./queue-line.md). Let customers take a number on their phone and walk away, then call them back to your booths with a TV screen, a voice and a buzz on their phone.
- [Set Up a Multi-Booth Group](./multi-booth-group.md). Group the booths at one venue so they share templates, a screen design and prices, sell sessions from one cashier, and run one queue line.
- [Publish Your Website](./publish-your-website.md). Build your own page at handle.pixture.io with your booths, a shop where customers buy a session, Frame Lab and photo lookup, then publish it and put the link in your bio and on a QR code.
- [Collect Consent Photos and Run a Campaign](./marketing-studio.md). Ask customers at the booth whether you may use their photos for marketing, build a library of photos you are allowed to post, collect contacts, roll a seasonal offer out to every booth with one campaign, and see how it performs.

## Running the Business

- [Read Your Dashboard](./read-your-dashboard.md). Pick the right workspace, set the period, and read revenue, sessions, prints, templates and your best booths on the Dashboard, so you know how the business did today, this week or this month.
- [Watch Booth Health and Get Alerts](./booth-health-alerts.md). See which booths are online and working, give each booth opening hours so a closed night is not an outage, get alerts on your iPhone, and report, note and resolve incidents with your team.
- [Find a Photo or a Payment](./gallery-and-transactions.md). Find one customer's photo and send them their link again, download many photos at once, find one payment, check it against your payment gateway, and export the month for your bookkeeping.
- [Track Profit and Loss](./profit-and-loss.md). Enter your costs, paper price, tax and event booking fees once, then read each month's profit and loss for the whole business or one kiosk or event, and download it as a PDF.
- [Share Revenue and Profit with Partners](./share-revenue-and-profit.md). Set up a venue on a revenue share, an investor on a profit share with a capital repayment, and staff on a profit share, read what each one gets, and download each partner's own statement.
- [Add Your Team and Set Roles](./team-and-roles.md). Invite staff, partners and managers to your Pixture dashboard, give each one a role that shows only what they need, and limit a role to some booths.
- [Set Your Country, Currency and Language](./country-currency-language.md). Set the time zone and currency your account reports in, give a booth in another country its own currency and clock, and choose the language of your dashboard and of the booth screens guests see.
- [Add a Second Computer or Move Pix Pro](./add-or-move-a-computer.md). Add a computer to your subscription for a new booth PC, move Pix Pro from one computer to another, and use Event Passes for single event days.
- [Run Your Account from Your iPhone](./iphone-app.md). Install Pixture Manager, turn on booth and payment alerts, and check revenue, events, booth health, payments and photos from your phone.
- [Ask Lucy and Connect Claude or ChatGPT](./lucy-and-ai-assistants.md). Get answers and small changes from Lucy in the dashboard and the iPhone app, and connect Claude, ChatGPT or Claude Code to your Pixture business with the Pixture connector.
