# Run Video and 360 Sessions

In this tutorial you will add **Video** and **360/Slow-mo** to an event, set up the clips guests get, and run a test at the booth. Guests record a short clip instead of photos, and get it on their phone from the QR code. Allow about 30 minutes, plus the time to make an intro, outro or overlay if you want them.

- **Video**: a short clip with sound, for example a video guest book. Guests talk to the camera.
- **360/Slow-mo**: a short take played back slowed down (and, if you want, forwards then backwards), with your music under it.

Both are **event modes**: they run on computers paired to an event. A booth outside an event shoots photos.

## When to Use It

- **A wedding video guest book.** Guests record a 10 to 15 second message for the couple. Video records their voice, so the couple hears every wish.
- **A launch, a gala or a sports event.** 360/Slow-mo turns a jump, a hair flip or confetti into a slow-motion clip that people post straight away.
- **A brand activation.** Put the brand's logo on every clip with an overlay, and its sting before and after it with an intro and outro.
- **An event rental with one camera.** Offer Photo and Video side by side: guests pick on the start screen, and you can give each its own price.

When not to use it: a self-service booth in a mall (Kiosk) cannot run Video or 360/Slow-mo; it shoots photos. For a moving keepsake from a photo session, the **Live photo** and **GIF** outputs already give every guest a short animation.

## Before You Start

- **An event**, with each computer on **Pix Pro** or an **Event Pass**. See [Run an Event](./run-an-event.md).
- **Pixture Photobooth** on every computer. Video records the guest's voice from 1.2.13. The list of overlays, one per guest in turn, needs 1.2.12 or newer.
- **A camera with a clean live view.** The clip is recorded from the camera's live view, so its quality is the live view's, not the photo sensor's full resolution.
- **For Video: a microphone** the computer uses as its default input. A USB or clip-on microphone near the guest sounds far better than the computer's own. Without one the video is silent.
- **For 360/Slow-mo: a soundtrack** (MP3, M4A, AAC or WAV, up to 15 MB), if you want music. 360/Slow-mo records no voice.

## Prepare

- **Light it like a video, not a photo.** A flash does not help a clip. Use continuous light: LED panels, a ring light or softboxes.
- **Orientation.** Decide now whether clips are **Landscape 16:9** or **Portrait 9:16**. Portrait suits clips people post to stories; landscape suits a screen at the venue. Make the overlays the same shape.
- **Intro and outro clips.** MP4 or MOV, up to 30 MB each, the same shape as the clip. Keep them short (2 to 3 seconds): they are added to every guest's video.
- **Overlays.** A PNG with see-through parts for a still frame (names, date, logo), or your own moving effect. See [Add GIF and Video Overlays](./gif-and-video-overlays.md).
- **Space for 360/Slow-mo.** Slow motion shows movement, so guests need room to move: a jump, a spin, a throw. Clear the floor in front of the camera.

## Step 1: Add Video or 360/Slow-mo to the event

1. Open **Events** and the event, then the **Setup** tab.
2. Under **Booth mode**, click **Edit modes**.
3. Tick **Video**, **360/Slow-mo**, or both. Keep **Photo** ticked if guests should also be able to take photos: each ticked mode becomes a button on the start screen.
4. Click **Save**.

![An event's Booth mode with Edit modes marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-video-and-360-sessions--event-edit-modes.webp)

You can also tick them in **Modes** when you create the event. Each mode then has its own card under **Booth mode**: click the card to open its tab.

:::info Older events
An event made before the start screen has one booth mode, changed with **Change**. There, **Video** can be picked, and **360/Slow-mo** is marked **Soon**. Make a new event to use 360/Slow-mo.
:::

## Step 2: Set up the Video tab

Click the **Video** card. Changes save as you go.

1. **Countdown**: 0 to 10 seconds before recording starts (5 by default).
2. **Video length**: 3 to 30 seconds (10 by default). 10 to 15 seconds is enough for a message; longer and guests run out of things to say.
3. **Quality** (2 to 8 mbps) and **Size** (**Rectangle 720p** or **Rectangle 1080p**). Higher means bigger files and slower uploads on venue Wi-Fi.
4. **Orientation**: **Landscape 16:9** or **Portrait 9:16**.
5. **Display text before recording**: the line guests read before they start ("Get Ready" by default). Use it to tell them what to say, for example "A wish for Rina and Dimas!".
6. **Mirror captured videos**: mirrors the preview and the saved video, like a selfie.
7. **Keep original clip**: also saves the video without the overlay on the booth computer (on by default). See Tips.
8. **Overlays**: **Add overlay** to lay a frame or an effect over the clip.
9. **Intro & outro**: drop an **Intro video** and an **Outro video** (MP4 or MOV, up to 30 MB).

![Event Video tab with each setting from Countdown to Intro & outro marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-video-and-360-sessions--video-tab.webp)

With two or more overlays, each guest gets the next one in turn. See [Add GIF and Video Overlays](./gif-and-video-overlays.md).

## Step 3: Set up the 360/Slow-mo tab

Click the **360/Slow-mo** card.

1. **General**: **Countdown**, **Quality**, **Size**, **Orientation** and **Display text before recording**, as for Video.
2. **Timeline**: pick a **Preset**. Each one is a row of speeds: a walker is slow, a runner is fast. The first is one slow clip; the others mix fast and slow.
3. **Reverse**: "Plays the video forward then backward". It doubles the length and makes the classic 360 loop.
4. **Clips**: click a clip to change its **recording duration** (0.5 to 10 seconds) and its **speed** (slower to faster). **＋** adds a clip; the bin removes the selected one.
5. **Soundtrack**: drop an MP3, M4A, AAC or WAV, up to 15 MB. "Music the booth plays under each 360 clip."
6. **Overlays** and **Intro & outro**, as for Video.

![Event 360/Slow-mo tab with General, Timeline (Preset, Reverse, Clips), Soundtrack, Overlays and Intro & outro marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-video-and-360-sessions--slowmo-tab.webp)

:::tip Keep the final clip short
A 4-second take at half speed is already 8 seconds; with **Reverse** it is 16. Clips of 10 to 20 seconds get watched to the end and upload faster.
:::

## Step 4: Check it at the booth

The booth's operator menu has a **Capture Settings** page with the same settings, so you can change them on the day without a laptop.

1. Open the operator menu (two taps in the top-right corner, then the PIN) and tap **Capture Settings**. Its subtitle names the event it is synced with.
2. The pills on top show which mode the page edits: **Photo**, **Video** or **360/Slow-mo**.
3. Change what you need on the **Video** card (for example **Video length** or **Display text before recording**) or on the **360/Slow-mo** card (**Preset**, **Recording duration**, **Reverse**, **Timeline**).
4. **Image overlay** > **Choose** puts a PNG from this computer over its clips (a transparent PNG, 1280 x 720). It replaces the event's overlays on this computer only.
5. Tap **Save**. It reads **Saved & synced ✓**: the change is on the event too.

![Capture settings on the booth with the Video pill, Video length, Display text before recording, Choose and Save marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-video-and-360-sessions--capture-settings-video.webp)

![Capture settings on the booth with the 360/Slow-mo pill, Preset, Recording duration, Reverse and Timeline marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-video-and-360-sessions--capture-settings-360.webp)

**Mirror captured videos**, **Keep original clip**, the orientation, the intro and outro and the event's overlay list are changed in the dashboard only. See [Lock the Booth and Use the Operator Menu](./operator-menu.md).

## Step 5: Run a test session

1. Tap **Start Booth**. On the start screen, tap **Video** (or **360/Slow-mo**), and pay if the event charges.
2. The guest reads your text and taps **Start**. The countdown runs, then the booth records ("Recording").
3. The booth makes the clip ("Making your video"): the overlay, the slow-motion speeds and reverse, the intro and outro, and the soundtrack.
4. The guest watches it and taps **Next** to keep it, or **Retake** to record again.
5. The Share screen plays the video next to **Scan QR**. Nothing prints. **Complete** ends the session.

On the first Video session a Mac asks for permission to use the microphone. Allow it, or every video is silent.

![The video capture screen with Get Ready, We record for 10 seconds and Start marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-video-and-360-sessions--video-ready.webp)

![The Share screen with the video playing beside Scan QR and Complete marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-video-and-360-sessions--video-share.webp)

## Check It Worked

- The start screen shows a **Video** or **360/Slow-mo** button.
- The clip plays with your overlay, intro and outro; a Video clip has the guest's voice, a 360/Slow-mo clip your soundtrack.
- Scanning the QR opens a page with one **Video** button. While it uploads, the page says "Your video is still uploading. This page refreshes by itself when it's ready."
- With **Keep original clip** on, the clean recording is in a `Pix Originals` folder, inside **Movies** on a Mac or **Videos** on Windows.

## Tips

- **Originals for the client.** **Keep original clip** saves each take as `Pix_<session>_original.mp4` in the `Pix Originals` folder (inside **Movies** on a Mac, **Videos** on Windows) on the booth computer, without overlay, intro or outro. Copy the folder after the event if the client wants to edit their own film. Only the finished clip goes to the guest's page.
- **Tell guests what to do.** "Say hi to the couple!" or "Show us your best move!" in **Display text before recording** works better than "Get Ready".
- **Keep the microphone close.** Put the microphone close to where guests stand and away from the speakers. A loud DJ drowns out every message.
- **Watch the upload.** Video files are much bigger than photos. On weak venue Wi-Fi, drop **Quality** a step or use **Rectangle 720p**; the clips still upload in the background and the QR works straight away.
- **Price video on its own.** On a paid event, **Price per mode** gives Video and 360/Slow-mo their own **Single only** price. See [Run an Event](./run-an-event.md).
- **One booth per mode at a busy event.** A guest picks the mode every session, so one computer can run Photo and Video. When the line grows, give video its own computer and microphone.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Why | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| The video has no sound | No microphone, or the computer is not allowed to use it | Plug in a microphone and set it as the default input. Allow the booth app to use it (below) |
| No **Video** or **360/Slow-mo** button on the start screen | The mode is not ticked on the event, or the computer is not on the event | Tick it in **Edit modes**, and check the computer is paired to this event |
| A Kiosk booth only takes photos | Video and 360/Slow-mo run only at an event | Pair the computer to an event |
| **360/Slow-mo** says **Soon** | The event was made before the start screen | Make a new event |
| The clip looks soft | Video is recorded from the camera's live view | Use **Rectangle 1080p**, more light and a camera with a clean live view |
| After **Save** at the booth, the button says it was saved here only, with no ongoing event | The computer is not running an event | The setting stays on this computer only. Make changes on the event page |
| Every guest gets the same overlay | There is only one overlay on the list, or the booth uses its own **Image overlay** | Add more overlays on the event, or clear the booth's **Image overlay** |

### Allow the microphone

- **Windows 11:** **Start > Settings > Privacy & security > Microphone**. Turn on **Microphone access**, **Let apps access your microphone** and **Let desktop apps access your microphone**.
- **Windows 10:** **Start > Settings > Privacy > Microphone**. Turn on microphone access for the device, **Allow apps to access your microphone** and **Allow desktop apps to access your microphone**.
