# Share an Event Album and Slideshow

In this tutorial you will share the public album of an event, embed it on a website, download every print as one file, and set up a slideshow that plays the event's photos on a screen at the venue while the party is still going. Allow about 15 minutes, plus a few minutes at the venue to set up the screen.

Every event has one online album with everything its booths captured. Guests open it on their phone to find their photos, and the client keeps the link after the day. The slideshow is the same album, full screen, with new photos joining by themselves.

## When to Use It

- **A wedding.** Send the couple the album link the same night, and play the slideshow on the venue's TV during the reception so guests see the photos come in.
- **A corporate event or a product launch.** Embed the album on the client's event page, and show the slideshow with the brand's colours and a QR to the album on a screen by the stage.
- **A festival or a school event.** Guests who lose their QR code still find their photos in the album, sorted by time.
- **After any event you rent out.** **Download all** gives you every print in one zip to hand to the client.

The album is for events. A self-service booth outside an event has no album: each customer gets their own share page. See [Set Up the Share Page](./share-page.md).

## Before You Start

- **An event** with computers paired to it. See [Run an Event](./run-an-event.md).
- Access to the event in the dashboard (the account owner has it).
- **For the slideshow**: a TV or monitor at the venue with a computer or a smart TV browser, and an internet connection at the screen.
- Any plan that runs the event: every computer at an event runs on **Pix Pro** or an **Event Pass**.

## Prepare

- **Agree with the client who may see the album.** Anyone with the link can open it. For a private event, share the link only with the client, or turn sharing off after the day.
- **Set the share page look first.** The album page takes its colours and font from your share page in **Pix Design > Microsite**, and shows your logo. See [Set Up the Share Page](./share-page.md).
- **For the slideshow, check the screen's shape.** A TV hung on its side (portrait) but fed a wide picture by the computer needs the **TV on side** setting in Step 4.
- **Use a computer that will not sleep** for the slideshow screen, and plug it into power.

## Step 1: Turn on Link Sharing and copy the album link

1. In the dashboard, open **Events** and open your event.
2. The **Link Sharing** card shows **ON** with its switch. It is on for every new event. Switch it off to make the album private.
3. Click **Copy** next to the album link, or the arrow button to open the album. The link looks like `pixture.io/album/` followed by a code for this event.

![The event page with the Link Sharing switch, the album link with Copy, and Download all, Embed and Slideshow marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-event-album-and-slideshow--link-sharing.webp)

Send the link to the client, or turn it into a QR code for a table card. While **Link Sharing** is off, the album and the slideshow links open a "not found" page; switch it back on and the same link works again.

## Step 2: Check the album page

Open the link on your phone, as a guest would.

1. The top reads "Photo album", the event's name, the venue and date, and "Photos are kept for 60 days after they are taken."
2. The photos show newest first, 48 to a page, with **Previous** and **Next**.
3. A menu switches between the kinds of files the album has, with a count for each: **Prints**, **Live photos**, **Decor** and **Singles**. It shows only when the album has more than one kind.
4. **Newest first** or **Oldest first** changes the order, and a time menu (**All times**, then each hour) helps guests find their photo by when they took it.
5. Tapping a photo opens that guest's own share page, where they download their files.

Before the first photo the page says "No photos here yet. Check back soon!"

## Step 3: Download every print, or embed the album

**Download all**, in the bar beside the event's tabs, saves every print of the event as one zip, named after the event (for example `rina-dimas-wedding-photos.zip`). It holds the prints only, not the singles, GIFs or live photos.

To show the album on the client's website:

1. Click **Embed**.
2. In **Embed the album** ("Paste this into any website to show the live album."), click **Copy code**.
3. Paste the code into the website's HTML. The album on the site shows every photo uploaded so far each time the page loads.

![Embed the album with the code box and Copy code marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-event-album-and-slideshow--embed.webp)

The embed shows the public album page, so it needs **Link Sharing** on.

## Step 4: Design the slideshow

1. On the event page, open the **Setup** tab, then the **Slideshow** tab. It sums up the look, for example "Fits the screen · Blurred photo · 6s per photo". Click **Change**.

   ![The event Setup tab with the Slideshow tab marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-event-album-and-slideshow--slideshow-tab.webp)

2. The **Slideshow** window ("How the album plays on a screen at the venue. New photos join on their own.") has a **Live preview** on the left. Sample photos stand in until the album has a few of its own. Set it up on the right:
   - **Screen**: **Fit screen**, **16:9**, **9:16**, or **TV on side** for a TV hung vertically while the computer still sends a wide picture.
   - **Background**: **Blurred** (the photo itself, blurred), **Solid** (one colour), **Glow** (a glow of the accent colour) or **Image** (your own, JPG, PNG or WebP up to 10 MB).
   - **Text & colour**: the **Text** colour and the **Accent**, or tick **Match microsite**.
   - **Font**: **Same as the microsite**, one of the listed fonts, or your own font file (WOFF2, TTF or OTF, up to 5 MB).
   - **Caption**: the **Label** ("Photo album" until you change it, up to 40 characters; empty hides it), the **Event name** and the **Date**, each with its own font and colour. The date line also shows the venue when the event has one.
   - **Show**: which kinds play, **Prints**, **Singles**, **GIF** and **Live photo**. Only **Prints** is ticked at first. Each single is a slide of its own.
   - **Timing**: how long each photo stays on screen, 3 to 20 seconds (6 by default).
   - **On screen**: **Event name** and **QR to the album**, both on at first.
3. Click **Save slideshow**.

![Slideshow window with the Live preview on the left, the settings on the right and Save slideshow marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-event-album-and-slideshow--slideshow-window.webp)

## Step 5: Play the slideshow at the venue

1. On the computer connected to the venue screen, open the event page and click **Slideshow** in the bar beside the event's tabs, or **Open full screen** in the Slideshow window. Either opens the album's slideshow in a new browser tab. You can also open the album link and add `?slideshow=1` to it, which is handy on a computer that is not signed in to Pixture.
2. Put the browser in full screen. In Google Chrome: press F11 on Windows, or Control-Command-F on a Mac.
3. Leave it running. After each full round, the slideshow reloads and picks up the photos taken since.

![The slideshow playing full screen with the caption and the QR to the album](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-event-album-and-slideshow--slideshow-full.webp)

A click, a tap or the Esc key stops the slideshow and shows the album grid. Keep the mouse and keyboard away from guests, or open the slideshow again if someone stops it.

## Check It Worked

- The album link opens on a phone that is not signed in to Pixture, and shows the event's photos.
- A new photo from the booth appears in the album once its upload finishes.
- The venue screen plays the slideshow full screen, in the shape and colours you set, and a new photo joins after the next round.
- The QR in the slideshow corner opens the album on a phone.

## Tips

- **Show the QR on the slideshow.** Guests who walked away without scanning at the booth scan the TV instead and find their photo in the album.
- **Pick the kinds with care.** **Singles** turn one session into several slides, so a busy event loops slowly; **Prints** alone keep the loop short.
- **Photos are kept for 60 days.** Tell the client to save what they want before then; after that, a photo leaves the album and its tile in the dashboard **Album** tab reads **Expired**. Download the prints with **Download all** if you keep an archive.
- **Turn sharing off when the client asks.** The **Link Sharing** switch closes the album and the slideshow at once, and turning it on again brings the same link back.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Why | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| The album link shows a page not found | **Link Sharing** is off | Turn **Link Sharing** on |
| The **Slideshow** button is missing from the bar | It only shows while **Link Sharing** is on | Turn **Link Sharing** on |
| New photos do not appear on the venue screen | The slideshow adds photos after each full round, and a booth uploads only when it is online | Wait for the next round; check the booth's uploads |
| The slideshow stopped and shows the album grid | Someone clicked, tapped or pressed Esc | Open the slideshow again and keep the mouse away from guests |
| The photos look sideways or squeezed on a TV hung vertically | The computer still sends a wide picture | Set **Screen** to **TV on side** |
| The Live preview shows photos nobody took | Sample photos stand in until the album has a few of its own | Use **Open full screen** to see the real album |
| **Download all** says "No photos to download yet." | No print has uploaded for this event yet | Wait for the booths to upload, then try again |
