# Run Your Account from Your iPhone

In this tutorial you will install **Pixture: Photobooth Manager** on your iPhone, sign in, turn on the alerts that matter, and learn the five checks an owner makes from the phone during a working day: money, events, booth health, payments and photos. Allow about 10 minutes.

The app is your Pixture dashboard in your pocket. It uses the same account as [pixture.io](https://pixture.io): anything you change in the app shows on the website, and the other way round. It does not take photos. Your booths still run the Pixture Photobooth app on a computer.

## When to Use It

- **Event rental.** You are on the floor at a wedding with no laptop. Pair a second computer, check that the event is taking sessions, and see the guests' photos arrive, all from your phone.
- **A self-service booth in a mall.** You are at home. A **Booth Offline** or **Paper low** alert tells you to send someone before customers start walking away.
- **A multi-booth studio.** One glance at **Health** shows which of 20 booths need a look, and the Dashboard shows which one carries the month.
- **A pop-up box at a cafe.** A customer had a bad session: make a free-session voucher on the spot and hand it over.

Use the website instead for buying Pix Pro or Event Passes, designing booth screens in the UI Editor, connecting a payment gateway and anything under **Settings** beyond your own profile. The app tells you when you reach one of these.

## Before You Start

- An iPhone with iOS 18 or later. An iPad can install the app, but it runs at iPhone size.
- Your Pixture account: the same Google, Apple or email sign-in you use on the website.
- Any plan: the app is free and works on Pix Starter, Pix Pro and with Event Passes.
- Your role decides what you see. A team member whose role cannot see transactions gets no **Finance** tab. See [Add Your Team and Set Roles](./team-and-roles.md).

## Step 1: Install the app and sign in

1. On your iPhone, open the App Store and search for **Pixture**, or open [Pixture: Photobooth Manager](https://apps.apple.com/app/pixture-photobooth-manager/id6819138305). Tap **Get**. On the home screen it is called **Pixture Manager**.
2. Open it and choose the way you sign in on the website: **Continue with Google**, **Continue with Apple** or **or continue with email**. The button you used last carries a **Last used** chip.
3. With email, enter your **Email**, tap **Continue**, then your password and **Sign in**.

<!-- SHOT-NEEDED: phone: the Pixture Manager sign-in screen with Continue with Google, Continue with Apple and the email option marked -->

The next time you open the app it asks for Face ID (**Unlock Pixture**). Changing a booth's prices or its UI project asks for Face ID or your passcode again, so a borrowed phone cannot change what customers pay.

:::info More than one organization?
The app opens the organization you last picked on the website. To switch, log in to [pixture.io](https://pixture.io), pick the other organization, then reopen the app.
:::

## Step 2: Turn on alerts

Alerts are why most owners install the app: they reach you when the app is closed.

1. Allow notifications when the app asks. Missed it? Open the side menu (swipe in from the left edge), tap your profile at the bottom, open **Settings** and turn on **Push Notifications**.
2. Open **Notification Settings** and keep the alerts you want:

| Alert | When it arrives |
|---|---|
| **Booth Offline** / **Booth Online** | A booth loses or gets back its connection |
| **Booth Error** | The camera, printer or app reports a problem |
| **Paper low** | A printer is running out of paper |
| **Payment Received** / **Payment Failed** | A customer's payment goes through or fails |
| **Pix Pro Ending** | A computer's Pix Pro is about to end |
| **Event Pass Started** / **Event Pass Ended** | An Event Pass starts or ends on a computer |

3. Going on holiday or into a meeting? **Pause All Notifications** turns every alert off until you switch it back on.

<!-- SHOT-NEEDED: phone: Settings > Notification Settings in Pixture Manager with Push Notifications and the alert switches marked -->

Every alert also lands in the inbox behind the bell at the top of the side menu. **Mark All Read** clears the dots.

## Step 3: Check the day on the Dashboard

1. The workspace switcher. On an account with events it switches between **Overview**, **Pixture Kiosk** and **Pixture Event**, the same as the website. See [Read Your Dashboard](./read-your-dashboard.md).
2. The period. Tap the arrows or the month to look at another one.
3. The eye hides your revenue when someone is looking over your shoulder. Tap it again to show it.
4. The tab bar: **Dashboard**, **Events** (or **Booths** on an account without events), **Gallery**, **Finance** and **Pix Design**. Everything else (Vouchers, Promo, Booths, Health, Computers, Users) is in the side menu.

![The Dashboard tab in Pixture Manager with the workspace switcher, the month, the eye and the tab bar marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-iphone-app--iphone-dashboard.webp)

**Paid Rate** under the revenue is the share of sessions that were paid ("1920 of 2208 paid"). A drop usually means a payment problem at one booth, so check **Finance** next.

## Step 4: Run an event from the floor

1. On the **Events** tab, tap **+** to create an event. The app asks one question per screen: name, what guests can do, what they take home, when and where, which designs, free or paid.
2. Open the live event (marked **Live now**). Its page has **Pair**, **Share**, **Slideshow** and **Edit** at the top.

![The Events tab with the + button and the live event marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-iphone-app--iphone-events.webp)

To add a computer at the venue, tap **Pair** on the event page. **Pair a computer** shows a 6-digit code with a countdown: type it in the Pixture Photobooth app on that computer. A computer that has never had Pix Pro sees **Free trial ready for this computer**. Buying Event Passes happens on the website, so buy them before the event day. See [Run an Event](./run-an-event.md).

## Step 5: Check booth health

1. Open **Health** from the side menu. The two cards at the top show how many booths are **Online** and how many **Need a look**.

![The Health screen with the Online and Need a look cards marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-iphone-app--iphone-health.webp)

Each booth row says what is wrong in plain words, for example "Paper is jammed inside the printer" or "Camera not responding". Tap a booth for its details. Below the booths, **Last 7 days** lists the incidents of the past week. See [Watch Booth Health and Get Alerts](./booth-health-alerts.md).

## Step 6: Check payments

1. On the **Finance** tab, pick **Day**, **Week** or **Month** and use the arrows for an earlier one.
2. Filter by **All**, **Paid**, **Pending** or **Failed**, or search by booth, template or ID.
3. **Export** sends the list out of the app, for example to your accountant.

![The Finance tab with the period picker, the status filter and Export marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-iphone-app--iphone-finance.webp)

Open a transaction for its **Breakdown**. If a payment is stuck on **Pending** while the customer shows you a paid screen, tap **Check with payment gateway**: it asks the gateway for its latest status. See [Find a Photo or a Payment](./gallery-and-transactions.md).

## Step 7: Find and share photos

1. Narrow the **Gallery** with **Today**, **7 days**, **30 days** or **Booths**.
2. Tap **Select** to pick several photos, then **Share** or **Delete** them.

![The Gallery tab with the Booths filter and Select marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-iphone-app--iphone-gallery.webp)

Press and hold a session for **Open Softfile** (the photo page guests get from the QR code) or **Share**, to send its link to a customer who lost their QR code. Photos are kept for 60 days. A whole event album downloads on the website (**Download all on the web**).

## Check It Worked

- The app opens on your Dashboard with the same revenue as the website for the same period and workspace.
- **Settings** shows **Push Notifications** on, and a test brings a **Booth Offline** alert: unplug a booth's internet for about 15 minutes while it is open (a booth is offline after 12 minutes without a check-in).
- **Health** lists every booth you can see on the website.

## Tips

- **Make a voucher on the spot.** Side menu > **Vouchers** > **New Voucher** creates a code at once, for example a free session for a customer whose print failed.
- **Ask Lucy on the phone.** The small tab with Lucy's face on the edge of the screen opens the same Lucy as on the website. See [Ask Lucy and Connect Claude or ChatGPT](./lucy-and-ai-assistants.md).
- **Support asks for booth logs?** Side menu > **Booths** > the booth > **Request computer logs**. The booth computer sends them; it must be on and online.
- **Edit a template between sessions.** **Pix Design** > **Template Editor** lets you fix a typo in a date or a name on the phone. Artwork over 15 MB and some cut-out slots need the website's editor.
- **Give staff their own login**, not yours. A role with only the booths and pages they need keeps your revenue private. See [Add Your Team and Set Roles](./team-and-roles.md).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Why | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| No alerts arrive | Notifications are off for Pixture Manager in the iPhone's Settings, **Push Notifications** is off in the app, or **Pause All Notifications** is on | Turn notifications on in iPhone Settings > Notifications > Pixture Manager, then check the app's **Settings** and **Notification Settings** |
| A booth closed for the night shows as offline | The app counts any booth without a recent check-in as offline | Nothing is wrong. The website's **Health** page knows the booth's opening hours |
| No **Finance** tab | Your role cannot see transactions | Ask the owner to add it to your role |
| The app shows the wrong business | It opens the organization you last picked on the website | Pick the right one on pixture.io, then reopen the app |
| Nowhere to buy Pix Pro or Event Passes | The app sells nothing | Buy on the website under **Computers**. See [Add a Second Computer or Move Pix Pro](./add-or-move-a-computer.md) |
| The pairing code ran out | Codes expire | Tap **Pair** again for a fresh code |
