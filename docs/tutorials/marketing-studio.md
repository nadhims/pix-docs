# Collect Consent Photos and Run a Campaign

In this tutorial you will turn on the consent question at your booths, so customers can say yes to you posting their photos, find those photos in Marketing Studio, collect contacts, roll an offer out to your booths with a campaign, and read the results. Allow about 20 minutes.

## When to Use It

- **A self-service booth that needs content.** Your Instagram and TikTok need fresh photos of real customers every week. With consent on, each customer who taps yes adds a photo you are allowed to post.
- **A multi-booth studio running a season.** A school holiday or a mall anniversary: set the offer up on one booth, then switch it on at every booth with one campaign, and off again on the last day.
- **Bringing customers back.** With Pay 1 get 1 and contact capture, every customer who claims the free session leaves an email or a phone number you can invite back.

Not for posting event guests' photos: an event's photos belong to your client. Ask the client, and use the event album instead. See [Run an Event](./run-an-event.md).

## Before You Start

- **Pix Pro.** Marketing Studio appears in the sidebar only on Pix Pro, and only for team members whose role includes at least one Marketing Studio area (the owner always has it).
- **A booth that shows the share screen** after the photos: the consent question appears over it.
- **Your own consent text (optional).** The built-in text works in English and Indonesian. If you write your own, use your business name and keep it clear: it is a legal permission (in Indonesia, under UU PDP).

## Prepare

- **Decide what you will use the photos for** (Instagram, ads, posters) and say so in the consent text. A customer can only agree to what you tell them.
- **Tell your staff:** the answer is the customer's own choice, and saying no never changes their photos or their download.
- **For a campaign, set the offer up on one booth first** (its Frame Lab, consent and promo banner), so the campaign has something to copy.

## Step 1: Turn On the Consent Question

1. Click **Marketing Studio** in the sidebar. It opens on **Consent photos**.
2. Pick the **Booth** at the top.
3. Switch **Marketing consent** on ("Ask guests if you may reuse their photos for marketing. Opt-ins appear in the gallery below. Off by default.").
4. Under **Consent text**, write your own words, or leave it empty for the built-in text in the booth's language. The preview on the right shows the question as customers see it.
5. Click **Apply to all booths** to use the same setting on every booth, or **Save changes** for this booth only.

![Marketing Studio Consent photos with the Booth picker, Marketing consent switch, Consent text, Apply to all booths and Save changes marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-marketing-studio--consent-settings.webp)

## Step 2: See What Customers See

After each session, when the share screen opens, the booth asks:

1. **May we feature your photos?** with your text (or the built-in one, which names your booth).
2. **Yes, I agree** saves the photo as a consent photo.
3. **No thanks** (or tapping outside) saves nothing. If the customer does not answer before the screen times out, nothing is recorded either.

![The booth's consent question with the title, Yes, I agree and No thanks marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-marketing-studio--booth-consent.webp)

The title and buttons are fixed and follow the booth's language; your text replaces only the body.

## Step 3: Find the Photos You May Use

1. On **Consent photos**, scroll below the settings. Every photo a customer agreed to share appears there, newest first, with the booth and date, and a **GIF** or **Live** badge when the session has one.
2. Switch booths with the **Booth** picker to see each booth's photos. **Load more** shows older ones.
3. Click a photo to open that session's share page in a new tab, where you can download the photo, GIF or Live photo.

![Consent photos gallery with made-up photos, each with its booth, date and GIF or Live badge](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-marketing-studio--consent-photos.webp)

## Step 4: Collect Contacts with Pay 1 Get 1

The **Audience** tab lists the customers who left an email or a phone number, with **Contact**, **Visits** and **Last seen**.

1. Create a **Pay 1 get 1** promo on the **Promo** tab. See [Run a Promo](./run-a-promo.md).
2. On each booth's **Pricing** tab, set **Ask for contact after Pay 1 get 1** to **Email**, **Phone** or **Email + phone** (the default is **Don't ask**).
3. Customers who claim the free session and leave their contact appear on **Audience**. **Remove** deletes a contact who asks to be forgotten.

![Audience tab with made-up contacts, their Visits and Last seen, and Remove marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-marketing-studio--audience.webp)

## Step 5: Roll an Offer Out with a Campaign

1. Open **Campaigns** and click **New campaign**.
2. Give it a **Name** (for example "School Holiday").
3. Under **Copy settings from**, pick the booth where you already set the offer up.
4. Under **Roll out to**, tick the booths that get it. Leave every box empty to roll it out to every other booth.
5. Under **Promos to include**, tick what to copy: **Frame Lab**, **Marketing Consent** and **Promo Banner / Microsite**. A campaign needs a name and at least one of these.
6. Set **Starts (optional)** and **Ends (optional)**, then click **Save campaign**.

![New campaign with the New campaign button, Name and Copy settings from marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-marketing-studio--new-campaign.webp)

With dates, the campaign switches on and off by itself (Pixture checks every few minutes). Without dates, click **Activate now** on its row; **Deactivate** ends it. Each row shows **Scheduled**, **Active** or **Ended** and when it last ran.

When a campaign ends, each booth goes back to exactly how it was before the campaign, for the parts the campaign changed. You do not have to undo anything by hand.

## Step 6: Read the Performance

1. Open **Performance** and pick **This month** or **All time** at the top.
2. Read the cards: **Promo uses**, **Pay 1 get 1 granted** and **Pay 1 get 1 used**, **Consented photos**, **Frame Lab sales** (with the revenue), **Microsite visits** (always all time), and the ticket and staff pass counts.

![Marketing Studio Performance with This month, All time and the Consented photos card marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-marketing-studio--performance.webp)

## Check It Worked

- Finish a session on a booth with consent on: the question appears over the share screen.
- Tap **Yes, I agree**: the photo appears on **Consent photos** for that booth, and **Consented photos** on **Performance** goes up by one.
- After **Activate now**, the target booths show the copied settings; after **Deactivate**, they are back as before.

## Tips

- **Ask for consent every day, post once a week.** A booth with steady traffic fills the library fast; pick the best five on Monday for the week's posts.
- **Keep the consent text short.** Two sentences that name your business and where you will post work best; long text gets skipped and answered "No thanks".
- **Promise and keep it.** If a customer later asks you to remove their photo from your posts, do it. The built-in text tells them they can withdraw by contacting you.
- **Test a campaign on one booth** with **Roll out to** set to that booth only, then widen it.
- **Pay 1 get 1 is the fastest win:** it costs one extra session and, with contact capture on, gives you a contact to invite back.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Why | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Marketing Studio** is not in the sidebar | The account is on Pix Starter, or your role has no Marketing Studio area | Get Pix Pro; ask the owner to add a Marketing Studio area to your role in **Settings** |
| The booth never shows the consent question | **Marketing consent** is off for that booth, or the booth skips the share screen | Switch it on for that booth and **Save changes**; keep the share screen in the booth's design |
| "No consented photos for this booth yet" | Nobody has said yes yet, or you are looking at another booth | Check the **Booth** picker; wait for a few sessions |
| **Audience** stays empty | **Ask for contact after Pay 1 get 1** is **Don't ask**, or there is no Pay 1 get 1 promo | Create the promo and set the contact field on the booth's **Pricing** tab |
| "Name and at least one lever are required." | The campaign has no name, or nothing ticked under **Promos to include** | Fill in the name and tick at least one |
| A campaign did not switch on at its start | It switches within a few minutes of the start time | Wait a few minutes, or click **Activate now** |
