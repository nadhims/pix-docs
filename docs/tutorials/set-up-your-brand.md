# Set Up Your Brand

In this tutorial you will set your business's look once in **Pix Design > Brand**: your logo, three colours, a button style and two fonts. After that, new event screens, your booth screen designs and the pages customers open on their phone start in your look. On Pix Pro Yearly you can also switch off the Powered by Pixture footer. Allow about 10 minutes.

## When to Use It

- **Event rental:** every event you run carries your logo and colours, so guests remember who ran the booth, and the next client finds you.
- **Self-service mall booth:** the voucher shop and photo pages customers open from the QR code look like your shop, not like a generic service.
- **Multi-booth studio:** set the brand once and every branch, every new UI project and every event starts from it. No one has to copy hex codes by hand.
- **White label for agencies (Pix Pro Yearly):** hide the Powered by Pixture footer so the guest pages show only your business.

For one client with its own look (a wedding couple, a sponsor), keep your brand as the default and change only that event or UI project. See [Design an Event Start Screen](./event-start-screen.md) and [Customise the Booth Screens](./customise-kiosk-screens.md).

## Before You Start

- **Plan:** Pix Pro or the free trial to save your brand. On Pix Starter you can try the page, but saving opens the "Pix Pro keeps your work" window. While your brand is not in use, the page reads "Turns on with Pix Pro or an Event Pass."
- **Switching off the Pixture footer** needs Pix Pro Yearly, held right now.
- **Access:** you need the right to change the account's settings (the account owner has it).

## Prepare

- **Logo:** a PNG or SVG, up to 2 MB, with a transparent background. A logo on a solid white box looks like a sticker on dark booth screens.
- **A light version of the logo** (optional). Booth screens are often dark. If your logo is dark, have a white or light version ready, or let the page make a white one for you.
- **Your three colours as hex codes** (for example #D97756): an **Accent** for buttons, a **Dark** for backgrounds and dark text, a **Light** for text and icon discs. Pick an Accent that is not too pale: it has to carry white text on buttons.
- **Fonts** (optional): your own .woff2, .woff, .ttf or .otf files, up to 4 MB each. Check that your font licence allows use on screens and the web.

## Step 1: Open the Brand page

1. In the sidebar, click **Pix Design**.
2. Click **Brand**.

![Pix Design page with Pix Design in the sidebar and the Brand card marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-set-up-your-brand--hub.webp)

## Step 2: Set your logo, colours, buttons and fonts

Every change saves by itself: the top of the page shows "Saving", then "Saved".

1. **Logo:** click **Add your logo** and pick your file. The logo shows twice, **On light** and **On dark**. If it is hard to see on dark, click **Make a white version**, or **Upload a light version** to use your own. **Change logo** and **Remove logo** replace or drop it later.
2. **Brand palette:** set **Accent** (buttons and highlights), **Dark** (backgrounds and dark text) and **Light** (text and icon discs) with the colour picker or by typing the hex code. The reset button next to a colour puts the Pixture colour back.
3. **Buttons:** pick **Gradient** (a soft shine) or **Flat**.
4. **Fonts:** pick a **Heading** font (titles and the countdown) and a **Body** font (buttons and everything else), or click **Upload a font**.
5. **Preview:** switch between **Start screen**, **Capture**, **Share** and **Guest page** to check your brand on each.

![Brand page with the Logo, Brand palette, Buttons, Fonts and the preview tabs marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-set-up-your-brand--brand-page.webp)

The page warns you when a choice will be hard to read, for example "This logo has a solid background. A transparent PNG looks cleaner." or "Accent is very light. Share icons and guest page buttons are hard to read." Fix the warning before you move on: your guests will see the same problem.

## Step 3: Switch off the Pixture footer (Pix Pro Yearly)

The pages and emails your customers get end with a **Powered by Pixture** footer. On Pix Pro Yearly you can hide it, and your business name shows there instead.

1. On the Brand page, find **Pixture footer**.
2. Turn **Show on guest pages** off. The **Guest page** preview shows your business name in the footer.

![Brand page on a Pix Pro Yearly account with the Pixture footer card and the Show on guest pages switch marked](/img/docs/steps/tutorials-set-up-your-brand--brand-footer.webp)

On every other plan the switch stays on and locked, with "Yearly plan." and a **Go yearly** link to the Computers page. If your account stops holding Pix Pro Yearly, the footer comes back by itself; your choice is kept, so it hides again when you go back to yearly.

## Check It Worked

- The Brand page reads "Used as the default for your events, microsites and UI projects."
- Open an event you have not styled yet and click **Edit appearance**: the start screen uses your colours.
- Open a UI project in the UI Editor: colours you never changed by hand now show in your brand colours, and your logo is placed on the pages.
- Scan the QR code after a test session: the guest page uses your colours, and with the footer switched off it shows your business name at the bottom.

## Tips

- **Your brand is a default, not a lock.** An event or UI project you style by hand keeps its own choices. A colour you set by hand that matches a Pixture default may still follow your brand, so check a hand-styled design after changing your brand.
- **A light Accent is the most common mistake.** White text on a pale button is unreadable on a bright venue screen. Test the Start screen preview from across the room.
- **Changing brand colours mid-season** changes every event and UI project that follows the brand. Change it between events, not during one.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Why | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| The page reads "Turns on with Pix Pro or an Event Pass." | The account has no Pix Pro, trial or Event Pass running | Click **Get Pix Pro**, or start the free trial |
| Saving opens the "Pix Pro keeps your work" window | The account is on Pix Starter | Start the free trial or buy Pix Pro |
| "You can't change the Brand." | Your team role cannot change the account's settings | Ask the account owner, or an admin, to make the change |
| "Logos can be up to 2 MB." / "Use a PNG or SVG logo." | The file is too big, or not a PNG or SVG | Export a smaller PNG, or save it as SVG |
| The logo disappears on dark booth screens | The logo is dark and there is no light version | Click **Make a white version**, or **Upload a light version** |
| **Show on guest pages** is locked with "Yearly plan." | The account is not on Pix Pro Yearly | Click **Go yearly**, or keep the footer |
| One event still shows other colours | That event was styled by hand | Open it with **Edit appearance** and pick **Your brand** under **Colours** |
