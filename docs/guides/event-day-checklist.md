# Event Day Checklist

Running a smooth event comes down to preparation. Use this checklist before, during, and after every gig to avoid surprises.

## Pre-Event (1-2 Hours Before)

### Hardware

- [ ] Charge your MacBook/laptop to 100% and bring the power adapter
- [ ] Connect and test your Canon EOS camera (check battery and SD card)
- [ ] Load printer paper and ribbon — do a test print
- [ ] Confirm your iPad/iPhone has the iOS Dashboard installed and logged in

### Software

- [ ] Open the Pix Desktop App and log in with the correct booth
- [ ] Verify your templates are loaded — tap through the home screen to confirm
- [ ] Run one full test session: capture, composite, print, and share via QR
- [ ] Check that the upload queue shows "0 pending" (no stuck items from a previous event)

### Network

- [ ] Connect to the venue Wi-Fi or your mobile hotspot
- [ ] Confirm the desktop app shows "Online" status
- [ ] Test that QR sharing works by scanning with your phone

:::tip
Arrive early enough to troubleshoot. Venue Wi-Fi can be unreliable — always bring a personal hotspot as backup.
:::

## During the Event

### Monitoring

- [ ] Keep the **iOS Dashboard** open on your phone or tablet
- [ ] Watch the **Dashboard home** for session counts and upload status
- [ ] Periodically check printer paper and ribbon levels — replace before they run out

### Common Issues

| Problem | Quick Fix |
|---------|-----------|
| Camera disconnects | Unplug and reconnect USB. The app will auto-recover within 10 seconds. |
| Prints are faded | Replace the ribbon cartridge. Check printer driver settings for quality. |
| Uploads stuck | Check Wi-Fi. Items queue offline and retry automatically when reconnected. |
| App becomes unresponsive | Close and reopen the desktop app. Your session data is preserved. |

:::caution
Never power off the laptop while there are pending uploads. Let the offline queue drain first.
:::

## Post-Event

### Wrap-Up

- [ ] Let any remaining uploads finish — check the queue in the desktop app
- [ ] Review the event gallery in the iOS Dashboard
- [ ] Export transaction data from **Billing > Transactions** if you charged guests
- [ ] Check **Analytics** for session count, popular templates, and sharing stats

### Hardware

- [ ] Power down the camera and remove the battery for storage
- [ ] Pack printer ribbon and paper separately to avoid damage
- [ ] Back up any local files if needed

:::info
All photos are automatically uploaded to your cloud storage. You don't need to manually transfer files from the laptop unless you want a local backup.
:::
