# Connect an AI Assistant

Pixture has a connector for AI assistants. Once connected, Claude, ChatGPT or Claude Code can read your business (booths, revenue, sessions, templates) and do a few safe things for you after asking: finish your setup, create a pairing code, assign templates, create vouchers. It only ever sees the business you signed in with, and it acts with your role's permissions.

You do not need this to use Pixture. It is a second way to get set up and to ask about your numbers, for people who already talk to an assistant every day.

- **Connector address:** `https://pixture.io/mcp/tenant`, with a **Copy** button under **Settings > General > Connected AI apps**.
- **Plans:** on Pix Starter the setup and basic tools work (who you are, guided setup, pairing code, booths, booth health, templates, product help). Revenue, analytics, reviews and actions need Pix Pro or an Event Pass.
- **Disconnect:** **Disconnect** next to the assistant under **Connected AI apps**. Access ends within a minute.

The step-by-step setup for Claude, ChatGPT and Claude Code, the setup prompt and what to ask is in the tutorial [Ask Lucy and Connect Claude or ChatGPT](../tutorials/lucy-and-ai-assistants.md).

## Things an assistant cannot do

- Install the Pixture Photobooth app or sign in to it. That happens on the booth computer.
- Upload a logo or design templates and booth screens. Those need the browser.
- Buy Pix Pro or an Event Pass. Payments stay on the **Computers** page.
- See any other Pixture business, or create accounts.
