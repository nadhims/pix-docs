# Pixture Manager for iPhone

**Pixture: Photobooth Manager** is the dashboard as an iPhone app. Watch revenue, run events, pair computers, check booth health, answer alerts and edit templates from your phone. It uses the same account as the [dashboard at pixture.io](../dashboard/overview.md): anything you change in the app shows on the website, and the other way round.

The app manages your account. It does not take photos: your booths still run the [Pixture Photobooth app](../desktop-app/overview.md) on a computer.

![The Dashboard tab in Pixture Manager: total revenue, sessions, paid rate and revenue trends, with the tab bar at the bottom](/img/docs/iphone-dashboard.webp)

## Get the App

1. On your iPhone, open the App Store and search for **Pixture**, or open [Pixture: Photobooth Manager](https://apps.apple.com/app/pixture-photobooth-manager/id6819138305).
2. Tap **Get**. The app is free and needs iOS 18 or later.
3. On your home screen it is called **Pixture Manager**.

The app is made for iPhone. An iPad can install it, but it runs at iPhone size.

## Sign In

The start screen offers **Continue with Google**, **Continue with Apple** and **or continue with email**. Use the same method you use on the website. The button you used last carries a **Last used** chip.

With email, enter your **Email**, tap **Continue**, then your password and **Sign in**. **Forgot password?** sends a reset link.

When you come back to the app, it asks for Face ID (**Unlock Pixture**). Changing prices or a booth's UI project asks for Face ID or your passcode again.

:::note More than one organization?
The app opens the organization you last picked on the website. To switch, log in to [pixture.io](https://pixture.io), pick the other organization, then reopen the app.
:::

## Create an Account

New here? Tap **Create an account**. With Google or Apple you skip straight to your business details. With email the app asks, one screen at a time:

1. **What's your email?** Then **Check your email** for a 6-digit code.
2. **Create a password**.
3. **What's your business called?**
4. **Your WhatsApp number**, then **Enter the code** sent by WhatsApp. No WhatsApp? Tap **No WhatsApp? Use email instead**.

If someone invited you to their team, you see **You've been invited**. Tap **Accept invitation** to finish on the website.

## Quick Setup

A new account opens **Quick setup**: your **Business name**, a **Booth Name**, **Currency**, **Timezone** and an optional **Location**. Then **Connect your booth** shows a pairing code: install the booth app on your computer and type the code there. Tap **Skip** to do it later.

Until your first booth is live, the Dashboard shows a checklist card, **Your photobooth isn't live yet**, with three steps: **Install the app**, **Pair your photobooth** and **Run a test session**. **Send link to my computer** sends you the download link (pixture.io/download), so you can open it on the computer that will run the booth.

## Find Your Way Around

**The tab bar** at the bottom has five tabs:

| Tab | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Revenue, sessions, paid rate, revenue trends, top templates, booth health and the latest sessions |
| **Events** | Your events, live ones first. Accounts without events see **Booths** here instead |
| **Gallery** | Photos from every booth |
| **Finance** | Transactions. Shown to people allowed to see money |
| **Pix Design** | Brand, Template Editor, UI Editor, Photo Filters and more |

**The side menu** holds everything else. Swipe in from the left edge of the screen, or tap the small tab on the left edge. It lists **Dashboard**, **Finance**, **Vouchers**, **Promo**, **Payment Gateway**, **Booths**, **Events**, **Health**, **Computers**, **Gallery**, **Users** and **Pix Design**, with the notifications bell at the top. Your role decides which items you see.

**The workspace switcher** sits at the bottom of the side menu and at the top of the Dashboard. On an account with events it switches between **Overview**, **Pixture Kiosk** and **Pixture Event**, the same as the website. See [Pixture Event, Pixture Kiosk and Overview](../dashboard/views.md).

**Lucy**, the Pixture guide, is one tap away on every screen. See [Notifications, Lucy and Settings](./notifications-lucy-and-settings.md).

## Do These on the Website

A few things live only on [pixture.io](https://pixture.io). The app says so when you reach them.

- **Buying.** Pix Pro, Event Passes and Pix AI credits are bought on the website, under [Computers](../dashboard/billing.md). The app shows what you have but sells nothing.
- **Creating or editing UI projects.** The app can assign a UI project to a booth. Design it in the [UI Editor](../dashboard/pix-design/ui-editor.md) on the website.
- **Creating or editing promos.** The app lists them.
- **Connecting a payment gateway.** The app lists gateway transactions. Set the gateway up under **Settings** on the website; see [Create a payment gateway](../tutorials/create-a-payment-gateway.md).
- **Connecting Instagram**, **downloading a whole event album** and detailed **organization settings**.
- **Switching organization** and **accepting a team invitation**.
- **Very large templates.** Artwork over 15 MB and some cut-out slots need the website's Template Editor.

## Language and Dark Mode

The app's screens are in English. Lucy answers in your phone's language. The app follows your iPhone's light or dark setting.
