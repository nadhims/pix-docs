# Notifications, Lucy and Settings

## Notifications

Pixture Manager can tell you when something happens at a booth, even when the app is closed. Allow notifications when the app asks, or turn on **Push Notifications** in **Settings**.

Open **Notification Settings** to choose which alerts you get:

| Alert | When it arrives |
|---|---|
| **Booth Offline** / **Booth Online** | A booth loses or gets back its connection |
| **Booth Error** | The camera, printer or app reports a problem |
| **Paper low** | A printer is running out of paper |
| **Payment Received** / **Payment Failed** | A customer's payment goes through or fails |
| **Pix Pro Ending** | A computer's Pix Pro is about to end |
| **Event Pass Started** / **Event Pass Ended** | An Event Pass starts or ends on a computer |

**Pause All Notifications** turns every alert off until you switch it back on.

Every alert also lands in the inbox behind the bell at the top of the side menu. **Mark All Read** clears the dots.

## Lucy

Lucy is the Pixture guide, the same Lucy as on the website. Tap the small tab on the edge of the screen to open the chat. Ask anything about your booths or the dashboard in plain words, in your own language.

**Clear chat** starts over. **Chat with our team on WhatsApp** reaches a person.

Lucy in the app cannot sell anything. Ask her about buying Pix Pro or Event Passes and she points you to the Pixture website.

## Settings

Open **Settings** from your profile at the bottom of the side menu.

- **Account**: your **Display Name** and **Timezone**.
- **Change Password**.
- **Push Notifications**: on or off for this iPhone.
- **About**: the app **Version** and **Build**, the **Privacy Policy** and the **Terms of Service**.
- **Sign Out**.

### Delete Your Account

**Advanced settings > Danger Zone > Delete Account** deletes your Pixture account. Type **DELETE** and enter the verification code to confirm.

:::caution
If you own the organization, its booths, templates, sessions, photos and billing data are deleted 24 hours later. You can cancel from the website until then.
:::
