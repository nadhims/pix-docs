# Money, Gallery and Pix Design on iPhone

## Finance

The **Finance** tab is your list of transactions. Filter by **Paid**, **Pending** or **Failed** and by **Date range**, and **Export** the list. Open a transaction for its **Breakdown**. If a payment looks stuck, **Check with payment gateway** asks the gateway for its latest status.

![The Finance tab: transactions with their amounts and status](/img/docs/iphone-finance.webp)

Finance shows only to people whose role can see transactions. See [Transactions](../dashboard/transactions.md).

## Payment Gateway

**Payment Gateway** (side menu) lists the payments your gateway received. **Validate Transaction** checks one against the gateway. Connect or change the gateway itself on the website; see [Create a payment gateway](../tutorials/create-a-payment-gateway.md).

## Vouchers and Promo

**Vouchers** shows your codes and **Batches**. **New Voucher** creates one on the spot, for example a free session for a customer at the counter. See [Vouchers](../dashboard/vouchers.md).

**Promo** lists your promos. Create and edit them on the website, in [Marketing Studio](../dashboard/marketing-studio.md).

## Gallery

The **Gallery** tab shows photos from every booth. Narrow it with **All booths**. Tap **Select** to pick several photos, then **Share** or **Delete** them. Press and hold a session for **Open Softfile**, which opens the photo page guests get from the QR code, or **Share** to send its link.

![The Gallery tab: photo strips from recent sessions](/img/docs/iphone-gallery.webp)

Photos are kept for 60 days. To download a whole event album at once, use **Download all on the web**. See [Gallery](../dashboard/gallery.md).

## Users

**Users** manages your team, with tabs for **Members**, **Invitations** and **Roles**. **Invite a teammate** sends an invitation. Open a role to choose its permissions and its **Booth access**. See [Settings > Members](../dashboard/settings.md).

## Pix Design

The **Pix Design** tab has the same areas as the website, as round buttons along the top: **Brand**, **Website**, **Template Editor**, **UI Editor**, **Photo Filters**, **GIF/Video overlay**, **Microsite** and **Pix AI**. **Website** shows on accounts with a Website, and **Pix AI** on accounts in the beta.

![Pix Design on iPhone: the area buttons at the top and the Template Editor's templates below](/img/docs/iphone-pix-design.webp)

| Area | On the iPhone |
|---|---|
| **Brand** | Logo, colours and fonts |
| **Website** | Home, links, shop and photos. **Publish** and **Share** it |
| **Template Editor** | Photo layouts for your booth (below) |
| **UI Editor** | Buttons, backgrounds and screens. See your UI projects; **Edit on the web** opens the website editor |
| **Photo Filters** | Colour grading for every photo. **Add filter** |
| **GIF/Video overlay** | Frames on GIFs and videos. **Add overlay** |
| **Microsite** | The page guests get photos on. **Copy to booths** |
| **Pix AI** | AI styles guests can choose. **Browse styles** |

### Edit a Template on Your Phone

Tap **+** in the Template Editor to start a template, or tap one to edit it. The editor has **Undo** and **Redo**, **Replace artwork**, **Template settings**, **Select several**, **Remove background**, **Add a QR code** and **Arrange**. Tap **Save** when you are done.

![The template editor on iPhone](/img/docs/iphone-template-editor.webp)

Artwork over 15 MB and some cut-out slots need the website's editor. See [Creating Templates](../dashboard/pix-design/creating-templates.md).
