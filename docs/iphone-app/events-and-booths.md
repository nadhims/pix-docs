# Events, Booths and Health on iPhone

Everything you need on the floor of an event, from your phone. For the full picture of each feature, see its dashboard page linked below.

## Events

The **Events** tab lists live events first, then the ones coming up and the past ones, each with how many computers it uses. Tap **New event** to create one, or **Filter by date** to find an old one.

![The Events tab: a live event at the top, then events coming up, each with its number of computers](/img/docs/iphone-events.webp)

### Create an Event

**New event** walks you through one question per screen:

1. **Name your event**
2. **What can guests do?** Pick the booth modes: Photo, Video, 360/Slow-mo, Studio, Notes, Photographer or Mingle.
3. **What do guests take home?** Prints, GIF, Live photo or Singles.
4. **When and where?**
5. **Which designs?** The templates for this event.
6. **Free or paid?**
7. **Ready to go.** Check the summary and tap **Create event**.

### The Event Page

Open an event for its page. The buttons at the top are **Pair**, **Share**, **Slideshow** and **Edit**. Below them:

| Section | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Computers** | The computers running this event and their Event Pass or Pix Pro |
| **Modes** | What guests can do at the booth |
| **Outputs** | What guests take home. **Configure** changes it |
| **Templates** | **Add a template**, **Add a template pack** or **Create a template** |
| **Appearance** | A live preview of the start screen. Tap it to see it full screen |
| **Details** | Dates, venue and the **Album link** |
| **Photos** | The event's photos so far |

The **Report** shows how the event went. The **More** menu has **Archive event** and **Delete event**. See [Events](../dashboard/events.md) for what each setting does.

## Pair a Computer

On an event page, tap **Pair**. **Pair a computer** shows a 6-digit code with a countdown. Type it in the Pixture Photobooth app on the computer. A computer that has never run Pix Pro sees **Free trial ready for this computer**: 3 days of Pix Pro, no card needed.

For a self-service booth, open **Booths**, open the booth and tap **Generate Pairing Code**. The code expires in 10 minutes. See [Pair your first booth](../getting-started/pair-your-first-booth.md).

:::note A computer needs a licence
Each computer needs Pix Pro or an Event Pass to run. Buy them on the website, under [Computers](../dashboard/billing.md).
:::

## Booths

**Booths** (in the side menu, or the second tab on accounts without events) lists your self-service booths. **Add a booth** creates one. A booth's page has:

- **Pricing**: the booth's prices. **Add pricing rule** adds one. Saving a price asks for Face ID.
- **Templates**: **Assign** or **Unassign** templates.
- **UI Project**: choose which UI project the booth shows, or remove it. Design UI projects on the website.
- **Settings**: **Output Settings**, **Payment Gateway Override** and **Reset Booth Data**.
- **Activity Log**: what happened at the booth and when.
- **Computer logs**: **Request computer logs** asks the booth computer to send its logs, handy when support asks for them.
- The computer itself: **Link computer**, **Rename computer** or **Unlink computer**.

See [Booths](../dashboard/booth-management.md) and [Booth Pricing](../dashboard/booth-pricing.md).

## Computers

**Computers** lists each computer with its Pix Pro status and its **Pix AI credits**. **Deactivate this computer** takes Pix Pro off it. Adding computers and buying passes happens on the website. See [Computers](../dashboard/billing.md).

## Health

**Health** shows which booths are online and the incidents of the **Last 7 days**: camera, printer, paper and errors. Tap a booth for its details. Turn on booth alerts to hear about problems without opening the app; see [Notifications](./notifications-lucy-and-settings.md#notifications).
